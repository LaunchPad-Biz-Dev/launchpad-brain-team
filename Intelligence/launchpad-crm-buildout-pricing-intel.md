# Launchpad CRM Build-Out Pricing Intel

**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Source:** Market Researcher (Agent #4) — full report at `marketing/output/reports/launchpad-crm-buildout-pricing-research-2026-07-16.md`
**Context:** Research to finalize the one-time build-out fee ladder sold on top of "Launchpad CRM" ($197/mo GHL resale, 6-month contract, 15-day trial, pre-built industry snapshots for Real Estate/Health Care/Taxes/Home Services/Law).

## Verdict
**$500 / $1,000 / $1,500 is correctly priced** — not underpriced. It sits at/above the GHL gig-economy floor ($80–$500) and well below every comparable CRM's onboarding fee (HubSpot Pro $3,000, HubSpot Enterprise $7,000, Salesforce SMB $2,000–$5,000+, generic CRM "QuickStart" $5,000–$10,000, Keap $500–$1,500). The gap wasn't price — it was **missing scope boundaries**. Full tier spec (deliverables + hard caps + escalation triggers) is in the full report, Section 3.

## Key Market Numbers (cite these in sales convos)
- HubSpot Marketing Hub Pro onboarding: **$3,000** mandatory / Enterprise: **$7,000** mandatory ([hubspot.com/pricing/marketing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/marketing))
- Keap: **$500–$1,500** required implementation, no published flat rate
- Salesforce SMB implementation: **$2,000–$5,000+** minimal, **$5,000–$15,000** typical
- GHL vendor 3-tier public benchmark: Basic $500–$1,500 → Standard $1,500–$5,000 → Advanced/Agency $5,000–$15,000+ ([GHLLogic, 2026-07-04](https://ghllogic.com/gohighlevel-expert-cost/))
- "Complete GHL System Build" (CRM+automations+funnels+payments+30-day support) starts at **$1,000** at one first-party GHL setup shop ([gohighlevelsetupservice.com](https://gohighlevelsetupservice.com/))
- Snapshot templates themselves are near-commodity ($97 for a lifetime 120+ snapshot pass on the open marketplace) — confirms the paid value is in **business-specific configuration**, not template existence.

## Universal Market Patterns
- Every tier, everywhere: at least 1 pipeline, 1 calendar, and 4 "textbook" automations (lead nurture, missed-call text-back, appointment reminder, review request).
- Data migration, real integrations, and email auth (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) separate Basic from Mid.
- Full workflow library + reporting/dashboards + reputation system + multi-integration separate Mid from Advanced.
- **Custom objects, API/webhook work, and multi-location/multi-sub-account rollout are NEVER bundled into a fixed-price tier anywhere in the market** — always advanced add-on or standalone custom project. This is the cleanest signal for when to route to a CUSS-style custom consult.
- 30-day post-launch support window before hourly billing is the de facto industry norm.
- 1–3 capped revision rounds, time-boxed, is standard productized-service practice (not GHL-specific, but universal in fixed-price service pricing).

## Objection Handling: "Why pay to set up something pre-built?"
- Reframe: the template is commodity: the fee pays for making it run *this* business (real phone/hours/pipeline names/service menu/integrations, tested end-to-end).
- Borrow HubSpot's own stated logic: mandatory onboarding fees exist to prevent "shelfware" — unconfigured software nobody adopts.
- Let clients self-select tier/depth instead of negotiating the fee down (repeated best practice across productized-service sources).
