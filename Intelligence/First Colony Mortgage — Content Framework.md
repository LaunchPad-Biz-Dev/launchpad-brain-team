# First Colony Mortgage — Content Framework

**Status:** draft, built 2026-10-06 from the Notion discovery page. Not yet reviewed with the client. Vocabulary corrected 2026-10-07: what the Notion page calls Bucket 1/2 are pillars; Awareness/Desire/Story/Choice are the buckets; "Educate" is the job inside Desire, not a bucket name. Promote to Projects/ once approved. Client: First Colony Mortgage, Las Vegas lender. NMLS 557156. Launch month: November.

## The insight: trust is the product
Taco Bell's gap was "All Culture, Little Crave." First Colony's is the opposite: plenty of information, not enough trust. The client's objections doc says disengagement rarely means no. People pause because they don't know us yet, don't understand the process, aren't ready, fear being found out (recruits), had a bad experience, or don't know what it costs.

- A website informs; a person educates. For complex products the content's job is a path to a human conversation, not an application.
- Local beats anonymous. Real Las Vegas company, real people, before or right after first contact.
- Language is strategy. "Equity Advantage" / "home equity conversion," not "reverse mortgage." "Debt consolidation," not "cash-out refinance." Adult children (40s–50s) research for parents.

Client's progression: Awareness → Familiarity → Education → Trust → Engagement → Conversation → Decision.

## Pillars
1. **Direct to Consumer** (HELOC, Equity Advantage/reverse, traditional)
2. **LO Recruiting**
3. **Culture and Community** (office and out of it; flagged by Anthony 2026-10-07, not yet built out)

Also flagged, not built: Total Mortgage franchisees (250+); could be a fourth pillar or fold into Direct to Consumer.

## Buckets (the four, never renamed; Desire here carries job Educate)

| Bucket | Purpose for First Colony | Main jobs | Objection it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Recognizable as a real, local Las Vegas lender and employer | Inspire, Entertain | "I don't know you" / "Is this an internet lead company?" |
| Desire | Turn information into confidence; confidence is what creates want in this category | Educate, Empathize | "I don't understand this" / "What will it cost me?" |
| Story | Familiarity and trust through the people | Prove, Empathize | "Can I trust the people?" / "We've seen this movie before" |
| Choice | Lower the barrier to a human conversation, not an application | Convert, Educate | "I was just looking" / "What happens if I reach out?" |

## Pillar 1: Direct to Consumer

**Awareness** — Local, Human, Consistent, Recognizable, Credible
- Hero Team (people, office, Las Vegas, same look every time)
- Product Anchors (one clean named piece per product, approved names, no rates)
- Hooks and Trends (entertaining, on-message; refs @whatsamortgage, @mgthemortgageguy)
- Placement: TOF paid social (purchase test campaign), profiles/cover art, YouTube pre-roll, co-branded print with Toro (equal logos), OOH

**Desire (job: Educate)** — Clear, Plain-spoken, Layered, Honest, Reassuring. Primary bucket for this pillar, most volume.
- Explainers (Equity Advantage vs HELOC vs debt consolidation; Loan Estimate; RESPA rights; underwriting)
- Process Walkthroughs (first call to close; why each document; what it costs to start: credit report covered)
- Myth-busters (reverse stigma, now FHA-backed; "will it cost me to find out"; "do I have to be ready")
- Family Guide (adult children researching for parents; 62+, proprietary to 55)
- Placement: reels/carousels, YouTube, email nurture, landing-page education, mid-funnel retargeting
- Angles: freedom from monthly payments, staying in the home, stress-free retirement. Refi/debt consolidation outperforms first-time buyer now.

**Story** — Real, Warm, Local, First-person, Unscripted
- Client Stories (outcome and feeling, never figures) · Meet the Loan Officer (bilingual) · Community and Local · Behind the Desk (cradle-to-grave in-house team)
- Placement: organic social, email, YouTube, landing-page proof, retargeting

**Choice** — Simple, Direct, Low-pressure, Transparent, Repeatable
- Product Comparison (structural only) · What to Expect (who calls, how fast, no credit pull first; mirrors Toro light first step) · Soft CTAs ("just looking is fine") · Cost Transparency
- Placement: landing pages, lower-funnel retargeting, email, product pages, QR print in Toro offices, final frame of every Educate video

## Pillar 2: LO Recruiting
Audience: producing LOs and RE teams in Las Vegas who research before replying. Hard part is awareness → engagement → conversation. Sell culture and people, not splits.

| Bucket (job) | Qualities | Series | Objection answered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness (Inspire) | Confident, Selective, Local, Consistent | Leadership Brand (Rob: sales/biz dev; Matt: scale/ops; non-producing ownership) · Branch Identity | Don't know the people; status quo |
| Desire (Educate) | Specific, Transparent, Show-don't-tell | The Model Explained (P&L branch, free LOA, in-house ops, benefits) · Amplify 90 / reverse referral (refer it, we run it, full comp) · Fusion 360, Toro leads, Launchpad marketing | Fear of wrong move; lack of urgency |
| Story (Prove) | First-person, Unfiltered, Energetic, Proud | LO Voices (roster named in deck, no one off limits) · Level-Up Stories (training, coaching) · Culture Moments | "We've heard this before" |
| Choice (Convert) | Confidential, Low-pressure, Selective, Clear | The Confidential Conversation · We're Selective · Stay-in-Touch | Confidentiality; assuming they'll be sold |

Placement: LinkedIn primary, IG/YouTube for leadership and culture, email drip, local events, retargeting.

## Pillar 3 / 4 (flagged, not built): Culture and Community; Total Mortgage franchisees
250+ franchise owners; preferred-office and ancillary services. Weight to Educate ("what happens when I have a lead") and Story (proof of execution; others promised and disappeared).

## Compliance guardrails (every piece)
- [ ] NMLS 557156 on every page and ad, IG bio, Facebook cover
- [ ] No rates · no real numbers, payments or timelines · no situational scenarios
- [ ] "Equity Advantage" / "home equity conversion," not "reverse mortgage" as lead term; "debt consolidation," not "cash-out refinance"
- [ ] Co-brand logos equal size; nothing reads as paid/sponsored
- [ ] RESPA Section 8: nothing implying kickbacks, referral fees, fee splitting or value for referrals
- [ ] Affiliated business relationships disclosed
- [ ] Testimonials: experience and outcome, never figures
