## Prompt Engineering Principles Anthony Uses

### 1. Be Specific, Not Vague
**Bad:** "Write some ad copy"
**Good:** "Write ad copy for a solar company targeting homeowners in Las Vegas, age 30-55, with a lead gen form for a free estimate. Focus on cost savings and avoiding airport exchange rates. Keep it under 150 words."

### 2. Include Context
**Include:**
- Target audience
- Business/product details
- Desired tone or voice
- Specific goal or outcome
- Any constraints (length, format, etc.)

### 3. Role-Play & Persona Assignment
**Pattern:** "You are [expert/role]. Your job is [task]. Consider [context]."

**Example:** "You are Veronica, our in-house media buying specialist. Help me create a media buying strategy for a solar company targeting homeowners. Consider budget, platform fit, and audience targeting."

### 4. Ask for Iterations, Not Just Drafts
**Pattern:** "That's good. Now [adjust/add/refine X]."

**Example:** "That outline is solid. Now make it longer for 'Scope of Services' section and include a deeper breakdown for each category."

### 5. Use Structured Frameworks
**Pattern:** "Create a [structure] that includes [elements]"

**Structures Anthony uses:**
- Lists with sub-items (decision issues + implications)
- Three-column comparisons (Gold Medals & German Shepherds format)
- Stage-by-stage breakdowns (Quarterly Plan Stage 1 & Stage 2)
- Before/after or problem/solution pairs

### 6. Leverage AI Memory in Same Chat
**Pattern:** Reference previous context in the same conversation

**Example:** "We built these 5 decision issues earlier. Now create a longer sublist for Budget Constraints, similar to the one we did for Scope of Services."

## Key Insights on AI Usage (From Anthony's Approach)

### 1. AI is a Collaborator, Not a Generator
**Anthony's philosophy:** ChatGPT is a thought partner, not a replacement
- He uses it to brainstorm and iterate
- He refines outputs, doesn't use them as-is
- He asks follow-up questions to improve results
- He provides context so AI understands his business

### 2. Specificity Drives Quality
**The more specific the prompt, the better the output**
- Vague prompts get generic answers
- Detailed prompts get targeted, useful content
- Including constraints (length, tone, format) improves results

### 3. Testing & Iteration
**Anthony tests multiple approaches:**
- Asks for initial version
- Requests variations and improvements
- Provides feedback: "That's good. Now add X" or "Make it longer/shorter"
- Combines best elements into final version

### 4. Speed Over Perfection
**AI is useful for:** Getting 80% of the way there fast, then refining
**Examples:**
- First draft of ad copy (then edits for brand voice)
- Framework outlines (then fills in examples)
- Email templates (then personalizes for each client)

### 5. Consistency Through Prompts
**By using detailed, reusable prompts:**
- Same process applied to all quarterly plans
- Same decision-making framework for sales positioning
- Same course structure for different topics
- Easier for team to follow and replicate

## Practical Tips for Business Owners (From Class)

### ROI Calculation
- ChatGPT costs ~$20/month (Plus subscription)
- Time saved on content creation: 2-3 hours/week
- At $25/hour: ~$50-75/week savings = $200-300/month saved
- **Net savings:** +$180-280/month after subscription cost

### Limitation Awareness
**ChatGPT cannot:**
- Access real-time data (knowledge cutoff in training)
- Browse the internet for current information
- Make decisions (only recommendations)
- Guarantee 100% accuracy (always verify important facts)
- Understand highly specialized domains without training

**Always:**
- Review outputs for accuracy and brand fit
- Customize generic responses for your brand voice
- Fact-check claims (especially in regulated industries)
- Add your human judgment and expertise

### Ethics & Transparency
**Best practices:**
- Tell customers when content is AI-assisted
- Use AI for efficiency, not deception
- Maintain human touch in customer-facing communications
- Balance automation with personal relationships
- Don't use AI to bypass important business processes

## Key Takeaways for Launchpad Team

1. **AI is a time-saver, not a replacement** — Use it to draft, brainstorm, and iterate. Always add human judgment.

2. **Prompt specificity matters** — The more context you provide, the better the output. Vague prompts = generic answers.

3. **Iteration improves results** — Ask for refinements, variations, and improvements. Don't settle on the first draft.

4. **Consistency through frameworks** — Reusable prompts (like the QP master prompt) ensure consistent quality across clients.

5. **Ethics matter** — Always review for accuracy, be transparent with customers, and maintain brand voice.

6. **Right tool for the job** — Use AI for fast drafting, brainstorming, and iteration. Use humans for judgment, creativity, and relationships.
