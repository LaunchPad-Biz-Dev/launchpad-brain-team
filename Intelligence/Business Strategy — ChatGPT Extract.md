## Core Business Vision & Philosophy

### Mission Statement
"Launch Pad Business Development is more than just a full-service marketing agency, we're the perfect partner for businesses that want to scale and grow. We use innovative marketing strategies, including digital advertising and social media management, to help businesses find solutions and reach their goals.

Our team is made of two parts: the engineers and the artists. The engineers make sure you scale and grow while the artists make sure you look amazing through the process. Whether you're just starting out or are ready to take your business to the next level, we can help!"

### The Three-Part Framework
Launchpad's entire business and philosophy is built around three pillars:
1. **Better Content** — Strategic, intentional content creation that follows research and structure
2. **Better Leads** — Converting attention into action through paid advertising and systems
3. **Better Systems** — CRM setup, automation sequences, and nurture workflows that convert leads to customers

Every proposal, quarterly plan, service offering, and training module at Launchpad is positioned around these three pillars.

### Two-Part Team Structure
- **The Engineers** — Drive growth through systems, ads, lead generation, CRM, and metrics
- **The Artists** — Create beautiful, brand-aligned content (video, photography, graphics, design)

Both sides must work in harmony for success.

## Pricing Strategy & Package Philosophy

### Pricing Philosophy
Launchpad operates on a **packaging model** for recurring monthly services, but always offers flexibility:
- **Standard packages** are pre-configured for different business sizes and goals
- **A La Carte services** allow businesses to buy specific services project-by-project
- **Custom packages** are built for unique situations (with Anthony's final approval)
- **Pilot programs** let prospects test services before committing to full packages
- **Discounts are strategic** — offered for long-term commitments, affiliate partnerships (LBEM), or strategic relationships

### Cost Justification Framework
When objections arise on price:
- Break down the cost per deliverable (4 videos + 8 graphics + ads + social + CRM for $2,500/mo is below market rate for each individually)
- Show full-service advantage (consolidate 5 different vendors into one partner = lower total cost + better coordination)
- Highlight scalability (start small, grow as business grows)

### Onboarding Fees
- **Standard onboarding:** Often included in packages
- **Custom onboarding:** $3,000+ (for startups or heavy rebrands)
  - Covers logos, branding, taglines, brand kit, brand strategy, content strategy, mockups, graphic assets, full buildout
- **Rationale:** Ensure proper foundation before execution phase

### Ad Spend Positioning
Launchpad separates service fees from ad spend:
- Service fee (creative + management) is one line item
- Ad spend (dollar amount going to platforms) is a separate line item
- Minimum ad spend recommendations typically $500-$5,000/month depending on package and goals
- Ad spend models can include:
  - Fixed monthly budgets
  - Performance-based pricing (e.g., cost per lead targets)
  - Multiple platform tiers (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Yelp, etc.)

## Sales Process & Proposal Strategy

### Two-Meeting Sales System
This differentiates Launchpad from other agencies:

**Meeting 1: Discovery Call**
- Deep understanding of prospect's business, challenges, goals
- Ask about current marketing, what's working, what isn't
- Understand growth goals (6-12 months), timeline, budget
- Learn about past agency experiences
- Identify decision-makers
- No pitch — pure listening and understanding

**Meeting 2: Tailored Presentation**
- Recap what was learned in Meeting 1
- Show you listened (reference specific pain points)
- Recommend specific package that fits
- Walk through deliverables and timeline
- Address concerns
- Send PandaDoc proposal for signing

### Proposal Template Strategy
Launchpad uses base proposal templates for standard packages (Essential, Comprehensive, Ultimate). The only customized section is the **"Your Unique Digital Marketing Package"** summary that explains why the specific package fits the client's goals.

**Customization Formula:**
- Position wording as: "This package will amplify [Client Name]'s [specific goal]..."
- Focus on their core objective (brand awareness, lead generation, conversion, retention)
- Keep tone professional but aligned with client's brand voice
- Include concrete deliverables tied to their goals
- Frame within the three pillars (Content → Leads → Systems)

### Lead Qualification Criteria
A qualified lead has:
- **Budget:** Minimum $2,500/mo for packages OR $500+ for a la carte
- **Need:** At least one of: marketing, ads, content, social media, CRM
- **Timeline:** Ready to start within 30 days
- **Decision-maker:** Can sign or has direct access to signer
- **Industry fit:** Service-based business preferred or local Las Vegas business

### Proposal Follow-Up Strategy
After sending PandaDoc proposal:
- Confirm follow-up call (e.g., "tomorrow at 9:00 AM") in personalized email
- Reference specific proposal highlights
- Create urgency and excitement ("We're really excited about the opportunity")
- Offer to answer any questions before the call
- Include proposal attachment link for easy review
- Keep tone warm and professional

## Content Strategy & Execution

### Content Creation Philosophy
**Research comes first.** Every piece of content should be intentional, not random.

**Research Strategy:**
- Study what's working (scroll intentionally, not mindlessly)
- Look for patterns: hooks, transitions, camera angles, text overlays, editing style, CTAs, pacing
- Reverse engineer the psychology: Why did this stop the scroll?
- Apply patterns to your niche (trends are transferable — adapt hooks, not invent new ones)
- Look for repeating structures: hook styles, emotional triggers, editing pace

**Content Creation Phases:**
1. **Pre-Production** — Research, planning, shot lists, client pre-production forms
2. **Production** — Shoot day, capturing raw assets
3. **Post-Production** — Assignment to right editor, editing, client revisions, approvals
4. **Delivery** — Confirmation to social media manager, ad team, and client

### Content Buckets & Themes
Different industries use different content mixes, but general buckets include:
- **Educational/How-To** — Industry tips, tutorials, "red flags," problem solutions
- **Service/Offer Promos** — Feature specific services, seasonal offers, hero content
- **Testimonials/Case Studies** — Client success stories, before/after, transformation
- **Behind-the-Scenes/Culture** — Team introductions, office tours, company values, lifestyle
- **Engagement-Driven** — Q&A, polls, interactive content, relatable humor

### Monthly Content Breakdown (Ultimate Package Example)
For a **16 video/month** schedule:
- 6 Educational/How-To
- 4 Service Promos
- 3 Testimonials/Case Studies
- 2 BTS/Brand Culture
- 1 Seasonal Offer Hero

For **24 graphics/month**:
- 8 Service/Offer Promos
- 6 Educational/FAQ carousels
- 6 Reviews/Before-After
- 4 Culture/BTS

### Platform Strategy
- **TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts** — Best for brand awareness (organic reach)
- **Facebook/Meta Ads, Google PPC, LinkedIn** — Best for lead generation (paid focus)
- **Choose 2-3 platforms maximum** — Focus beats volume; prevent burnout and inconsistency

## Brand Archetypes & Voice Strategy

### Using StoryBrand Archetypes
Launchpad positions clients using archetypal brand personas (based on Building a StoryBrand by Donald Miller). Each archetype has:
- **Innate advantages** (strengths the brand naturally has)
- **Brand villains** (what customers fear, what the brand fights against)
- **Voice/tone** (how the brand communicates)
- **Customer emotion** (how the customer should feel)
- **Potential pitfalls** (things to avoid)

### Examples
- **The Hero** (Toro Taxes, ICON Injury) — Motivational, self-confident, self-disciplined, direct. Customers feel inspired, empowered, motivated.
- **The Caregiver** (Complete Family Care, Fuerza Law) — Considerate, thoughtful, kind. Customers feel loved, cared for, safe, thankful.
- **The Everyman** (Pioneer Air, Western Title) — Friendly, humble, honest, practical. Customers feel understood, included, "I can do that too."

### Client Onboarding: Discovery Form
Launchpad uses a **Discovery Form** to understand:
- Client email and preferred contact method
- Brand identity (archetype, voice, values)
- Business background and experience
- Competitors and differentiators
- Target audience and geographic reach
- Products/services and keywords
- Core values and brand strategy

## Key Strategic Decisions & Rationale

### Why Monthly Recurring Services Over Projects
- Provides predictable revenue
- Allows deeper relationships and optimization
- Enables team planning and resource allocation
- Shows commitment to client success (not one-off transaction)
- Aligns with growth goals (10 new clients/year = meaningful MRR impact)

### Why Three-Service-Tier Model
- Serves different business sizes and budgets ($2,500-$4,500/mo range)
- Easy for salespeople to position ("What's your budget? Growth stage?")
- Creates upgrade path (start Essential, grow to Ultimate)
- Packages include must-haves (CRM, ads, content) not à la carte upsells

### Why Two-Meeting Sales Process
- Differentiates from high-pressure "pitch and close" agencies
- Allows deeper discovery of actual client needs
- Gives time for custom recommendation without assumption
- Builds trust (client feels heard, not sold)
- Sets up better fit and longer relationships

### Why Pilot Programs
- Removes buying anxiety for prospects ("What if it doesn't work?")
- Lets Launchpad prove value (work speaks louder than words)
- Often converts to full-package contracts
- Positions as partner, not vendor

### Why Content → Leads → Systems Framework
- Mirrors the customer journey (awareness → consideration → conversion)
- Aligns all team communication
- Makes scaling easier (everyone understands priorities)
- Differentiates from "we just make content" agencies
- Connects to client business outcomes (not vanity metrics)

## Media Buying Strategy & Approach

### Core Media Buying Philosophy
Launchpad positions media buying as a complement to content creation — great creative is worthless without proper targeting and paid distribution. Media buying decisions are driven by:
1. **Platform fit** — Where the ideal customer spends time
2. **Budget efficiency** — Maximize reach within constrained budgets
3. **Creative testing** — Test variations to identify highest-performing angles
4. **Audience targeting** — Precision targeting to avoid wasted spend

### Primary Platforms by Use Case
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** — Lead generation, conversions, remarketing (primary for most clients)
- **Google Ads** — Local search intent, keyword-driven campaigns
- **TikTok** — Brand awareness, younger demographics, viral potential
- **LinkedIn** — B2B, professional services, recruitment
- **YouTube** — Educational content, longer-form storytelling, pre-roll ads

### Media Buying Strategy: Lead Gen Campaigns (Solar Sales Example)
**Objective:** Generate qualified leads for high-ticket services (solar, AC, HVAC, legal, real estate)
- Use **Facebook Lead Gen ad format** — Form fills without leaving Facebook
- Target **homeowners** by interest, age range, and income
- Use **value-add magnets** (free eBooks, consultations, guides)
- Separate **prospecting** (cold audience) from **retargeting** (warm audience)
- Test **multiple creative angles** (problem-aware vs. solution-aware messaging)

### Budget Allocation Framework
- **Prospecting:** 40-50% of budget (finding new leads)
- **Retargeting:** 30-40% (warming up engaged users)
- **Creative testing:** 10-20% (testing new messages and angles)
- Minimum recommended monthly spend: **$500-$5,000** depending on package and market

### Performance Metrics & Optimization
Key KPIs for paid advertising:
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** — Target depends on industry (legal: $50-100, solar: $100-200, restaurants: $10-20)
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** — Breakeven at 1:1, profitable at 2:1+
- **Conversion Rate** — % of leads who become customers (tracked via CRM)
- **Frequency** — How often an ad is shown to same person (optimal: 2-5x per week)

### Testing & Iteration
Launchpad's media buying approach is **always testing**:
- A/B test headlines, images, videos, and CTAs
- Pause underperforming creatives
- Scale winners (increase budget to proven performers)
- Refresh creative frequently (audiences fatigue on same ad)
- Combine learnings into next quarter's strategy

## Quarterly Planning Framework & Execution

### Why Quarterly Planning Works
**Problem:** Most agencies bill monthly without strategy. Clients see deliverables but not direction.
**Solution:** Launchpad builds a **90-day roadmap** that shows:
- What content will be created and why
- How much budget goes to which platforms
- What seasonal opportunities exist
- How CRM campaigns will nurture leads
- Hard totals for deliverables (not estimates)

### Quarterly Plan Components (Stage 1)
A client-facing, strategic 90-day plan that includes:
1. **Client overview** — Business context, objectives, constraints
2. **Review of wins & challenges** — What worked before, what didn't
3. **Quarterly focus areas** — Services to emphasize, campaigns to run
4. **Narrative arc** — Monthly themes tied to holidays, seasons, promotions
5. **Detailed deliverables** — Hard totals (e.g., 48 videos, 72 graphics, specific posts/shoots)
6. **CRM strategy** — Lead gen campaigns, nurture sequences, review prompts, greetings

### Monthly Execution Plans (Stage 2)
Built only after QP is approved. Team-facing, tactical breakdown including:
- **Executive summary** — Month focus, connection to quarterly plan
- **Sales objectives** — Primary and secondary goals, ad mix
- **Deliverables breakdown** — Videos, graphics, posts, CRM campaigns by type
- **Weekly cycle** — Shoot day, edit sprints, posting schedule, ad optimization
- **Team assignments** — Who owns what, clear accountability

### Hard Deliverables vs. Estimates
Example (Ultimate Package, Q1):
- **NOT:** "Approximately 4 videos/week" (vague, not binding)
- **YES:** "Exactly 16 videos, 24 graphics, 12 organic posts, 3 shoots in January" (specific, trackable, delivered)

### Template & Reusability
Launchpad has created **master prompts** for building quarterly and monthly plans collaboratively with AI (e.g., Veronica, Claude). This allows:
- Consistent planning across all clients
- Faster QP development
- Collaborative process with clients
- Easier training for new team members
