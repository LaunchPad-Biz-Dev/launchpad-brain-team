## How to Work with Veronica

### Best Prompting Practices

1. **Be Specific About Context**
   - Name the client, industry, campaign goal
   - Share relevant details (product names, pricing, benefits, pain points)
   - Example: "Create a caption for Pioneer Air (HVAC). Leo is talking about VIP membership, which includes winter maintenance checks + summer 21-point AC inspection."

2. **Request Format Upfront**
   - "Reformat as: headline, problem/copy, solution/CTA"
   - "Make it visual — bullet points, emojis, concise"
   - "Create 3-5 options for A/B testing"

3. **Specify Tone Adjustments**
   - "More inspirational, less bro factor"
   - "Speak to middle-aged female audience"
   - "Authority tone for medical niche"
   - "Warm and relatable for community-focused brand"

4. **Provide Examples When Possible**
   - Share previous successful captions/emails
   - Reference competitor styles
   - Show examples of tone/structure you want

5. **Give Feedback in Rounds**
   - First round: General direction
   - Second round: Specific tweaks ("add more emphasis on X", "dial back Y")
   - Third round: Final polish

### Current Limitations & Notes
- Cannot access external links or sales pages directly
- Works best with detailed context about product/service
- Quality improves with specific audience/pain point descriptions
- May need 2-3 iterations to nail exact tone

## Known Effective Patterns

### Caption Structure
**Works Well For:** Social media posts, short-form video thumbnails

```
[Headline/Hook] 🎯

[Problem/Pain Point] 😟

[Solution/Benefit] ✅

[CTA - Link/Learn More] 👉 [Link]
```

### Email Structure
**Works Well For:** Educational sequences, welcome emails, re-offers

```
Dear {{contact.name}},

[Warm greeting / context-setting]

[Main content with 3-4 key points/sections]
- Point 1: [Benefit/tip]
- Point 2: [Benefit/tip]
- Point 3: [Benefit/tip]

[Closing statement / next step]

[Signature - from Anthony or team]
```

### Monthly Recap (Visual Version)
**Works Well For:** Client updates, internal performance sharing

```
🎉 Celebrating Last Month's Wins:
- 🎥 [Achievement 1]
- 🎨 [Achievement 2]
- 🤖 [Achievement 3]

💡 What's Next:
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]
- [CTA/Link for action]
```
