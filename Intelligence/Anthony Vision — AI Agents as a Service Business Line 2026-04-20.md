## The Idea (Cleaned Up from Voice Note)

Right now AI is booming — but Anthony is noticing that the real opportunity isn't just *using* AI inside your own business. It's **building AI agent teams FOR other businesses**.

The difficulty of building agents is the actual need gap. Most business owners know AI exists. They can't build it. They don't know where to start. That's where Launchpad could come in — not as a marketing agency, but as an **AI team builder**.

The model Anthony is thinking about:
- Go into a company (example: a home services company like HVAC, plumbing, roofing)
- Build agents that handle their specific pain points: lead follow-up, booking, lead nurture, deal progression
- The client doesn't think of it as "software" — they think of it as **hiring an employee**
- "This is the employee I want to hire. Awesome." — that's the buying frame Anthony wants to build around

The service isn't "here's an AI tool." It's "here's your AI team." Could mirror Launchpad's internal structure:
- A sales team of agents
- A marketing team of agents
- A customer service team of agents
- Etc.
