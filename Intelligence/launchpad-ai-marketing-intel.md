## Top Performing Content Angles on AI Marketing (Social Media)
**Updated:** 2026-04-04

- Tool stack reveals ("here's our exact AI stack")
- Process reveals showing prompt-to-output workflow
- Time savings proof ("this used to take 4 hours, now 11 minutes")
- Myth-busting ("AI won't replace you, but...")
- Comparison carousels (Claude vs. ChatGPT for specific tasks)
- ROI-focused content (dollar return, not feature lists)
- Practical prompt giveaways (high save rates)
- "I built an AI [employee/team/OS]" — DOMINANT ANGLE April 2026
- "AI employees" frame outperforming "AI tools" frame on LinkedIn (Claire Vo viral)
- Specific number hooks ("87% of AI content fails — here's the 13%") — peak performance
- First-person process reveals with screen recordings (show the tool running, not stock footage)

## Hook Patterns Ranked by Save Rate (April 2026)
1. Time proof: "This took 6 hours. Now 11 minutes." (highest save rate)
2. Specific number reveal: "87% of [thing] fail. Here's the 13%."
3. "I built" first-person: "I built an AI marketing team that runs 21 posts without lifting a finger."
4. Mistake/myth callout: "Stop using AI like a search engine."
5. Direct comparison: "Claude vs ChatGPT for client deliverables. 90-day test."

## What Gets Scrolled Past (Do Not Do)
- Generic AI tool lists
- "AI is transforming marketing" as a hook — saturated
- Polished corporate graphics with no perspective
- Talking about features, not outcomes
- Raw unedited AI output — Instagram caps reach at ~10% of followers
- Stock footage of robots instead of actual screen recordings
