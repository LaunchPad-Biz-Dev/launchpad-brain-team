### 1. Personal injury: 5-minute response time + "cost per signed case" is the new differentiator
- **Score:** 16/20 (R:5 V:3 A:4 C:4)

### 2. YouTube changes how it counts a "view" — effective August 24, 2026
- **Score:** 16/20 (R:3 V:5 A:5 C:3)
- **Source:** [SocialPilot Algorithm Update Aug 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/social-media-algorithm)

View counts on Shorts/short-form video will jump industry-wide, independent of actual performance change.
- **Content Angle:** Not a content play — a reporting-accuracy flag. Any view-count "win" reported after Aug 24 needs a note that methodology changed, not just performance.
- **Best For:** Internal — data-analyst, ads-reporter, video-production agents | **Format:** Internal note
- **Urgency:** Post today — affects any report going out this week
