# Lucky RX — Paid Research (2026-10-07)

**Date:** 2026-10-07 · **By:** Apollo, media-buyer Workflow 6 · **Client:** [[Lucky RX]] · **Full sources:** `marketing/output/media-buying/lucky-rx/ad-strategy/research.md` (every claim linked and dated there).

## Competitor ads — what's live on Meta (Ad Library, US, 122 ads scraped 10/07)
- **BlueChew (50 ads):** ~70% are Advantage+ catalog ads assembled from a product feed. Two hand-written copies proven since Jun–Sep 2026: ingredient list + "works in 15 minutes on average" + full compounded disclaimer; and "stay present, connected, and confident." LP: "Try 2 months of Gold, Get 1 FREE", "From $7.30/ea", "Trusted by 5 Million+ Men", comedian endorsements. Real moat is off-Meta: 11.5k podcast episodes, 124 YouTube creators.
- **Hims (21 ads, fresh batch 9/23):** one switcher hook ("If traditional ED pills don't work for you, Hard Mints might be an option—if prescribed") + 4 emoji convenience bullets + "no office visit required"; CTA is the free consult, not the purchase. Hard Mints $3.60–7.50/use.
- **Affiliate layer (ScoreBlue, Trusted By Men, BrickedRx, North Valley urologist, Rugiet digest):** long-form first-person story ads (300–600 words, image + Learn more → advertorial) and "which ED pills are junk" listicles. ScoreBlue attacks all compounded brands as "not FDA approved." Buyers will have seen this.
- **Gaps nobody owns:** (1) the couple / partner-addressed ads — zero in the set; (2) B12/energy, 5 ingredients; (3) premium unboxing; (4) authority without "FDA" (LegitScript + licensed pharmacy + physician); (5) long-form story told from a couple's side.
- **Lucky RX's own two live ads** ("Distance in the bedroom usually isn't about love…") are already compliance-shaped and own the couple angle. Keep that floor.

## Policy — what the platforms actually allow (verified 10/07)
- **Meta Drugs & Pharmaceuticals:** Rx ads allowed for efficacy/access/affordability. Online pharmacies / telehealth need **active LegitScript certification + Meta authorization**, 18+, US/CA/NZ only, consult-a-professional disclaimer. Lucky has seal #50479743 and live Rx ads, so authorization is presumed on the client BM — verify in Account Quality and run inside that BM (agency LegitScript = $975 + $2,150/yr, 6–10 weeks; don't).
- **Meta Health & Wellness:** ED products allowed 18+, framed on health/medical efficacy, **not sexual pleasure or enhancement**. The site headline "Best Sex You've Ever Had" is the top rejection risk because Meta crawls the LP. Need a Meta-safe LP variant (salesM1).
- **Targeting:** health-condition interests are gone; plan on broad + creative qualification + first-party retargeting. **Correction 10/07:** "Household income: top X% of ZIP codes (US)" detailed targeting still exists and the client uses it — it costs 2–3× CPM and 2.5–10× per form_submit vs broad in their own account. Use creative and offer placement for quality, not the income toggle.
- **Client rules are stricter than Meta** (no speed/duration claims even though BlueChew makes them; no bedroom; no promo in cold). Site/ad-rule contradiction (5 min / 36 h on page) is the client's to resolve.
- **TikTok:** flags sexual health aggressively, needs its own Rx certification; not before month 3.
