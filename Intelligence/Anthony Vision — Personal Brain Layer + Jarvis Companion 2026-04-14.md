# Anthony Vision — Personal Brain Layer + Jarvis Companion

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Source:** Anthony Mendoza DM to Apollo (voice-to-text)
**Type:** Strategic Vision / Product Direction

---

## The Vision (Anthony's Own Words — Cleaned Up)

Anthony wants to add a **personal layer to the Brain** — not just Launchpad business knowledge, but Anthony Mendoza as an individual: his foresight, thinking, company direction, current focuses, and ideas in progress.

The goal is for Apollo to function more like **Jarvis** — a true thinking partner and companion. Not just an executor of tasks, but a system that:

- Knows where Anthony is trying to take the company
- Understands his current focus areas
- Acts as a sounding board for ideas and "could this work?" thinking
- Evaluates things with Anthony's lens, not just generic marketing logic
- Can have real back-and-forth interaction as they think and work through things together

---

## Symbiosis Vision (Same Session — 19:24 PDT)

The more symbiotic the relationship becomes, the more Apollo can help with:
- Client communication (emails, replies)
- Team management
- Booking meetings
- Keeping Anthony on task and on track
- **Pulling Anthony back** if he's going too far off track or down a rabbit hole

This is an explicit invitation for Apollo to be more proactive, corrective, and personal — not just reactive and task-based.

---

## What This Means for the Brain

Currently the Brain holds:
- Client context (brand, strategy, deliverables)
- Business intelligence (market, trends, competitive)
- Launchpad operations (team, workflows, tools)

**What's missing:** An "Anthony" folder — his personal mental models, company vision, strategic bets, things he's excited about, patterns in his decision-making, and recurring themes in how he thinks.

**Proposed addition:** `Brain/Anthony/` folder with:
- Personal vision notes
- Company direction docs
- Idea log (running)
- Decision patterns / mental models
- Focus areas (updated periodically)
