## Overview

"Building in Public" is the content series documenting the real-time build of LaunchPad's AI OS — the 9-agent marketing team, skills, infrastructure, and lessons learned as it all comes together. The series is Launchpad Business Development's flagship content play for Q2 2026.

**Series hashtag:** `#BuildingInPublic`

**Core philosophy:** Document over create. The build IS the product. Every post is a receipt.

## Content Pillars (5)

1. **Tool Drop** — Each new agent or skill released, treated like a streetwear drop
2. **Build Log** — What shipped this week, lessons, wins, fails
3. **Fear Breakdown** — Honest posts about the uncertainty of building AI for an agency
4. **The Win** — Concrete outcomes: time saved, clients onboarded, content shipped
5. **The Glossary** — Educate the audience on AI, agents, skills, MCP, etc.
