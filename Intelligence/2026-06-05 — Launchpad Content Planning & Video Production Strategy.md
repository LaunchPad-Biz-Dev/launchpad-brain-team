# Launchpad Content Planning & Video Production Strategy — 2026-06-05

**Attendees:** Anthony Mendoza, Amber Nardo, Shumi team members, Kyle Fuller, Emigen Lagman (absent, tasks assigned in-absentia)
**Meeting date:** 2026-06-05 (Notion last edited 2026-06-08) — synced to Brain 2026-08-17

## Why this matters

This meeting is the team's first concrete attempt to design the **content consistency engine** that [[2026-05-20 — Strategic Pivot — Personal Brand + AI-for-Business]] flagged as the #1 open risk for the personal-brand pivot ("Consistency is the proven weak spot... Design this FIRST"). It's not the full answer — no named ops owner or delivery-at-volume model yet — but it's the first structured production plan.

## Key Decisions

1. **Funnel strategy locked:** leverage existing marketing/educational videos to drive viewers to LP University rather than building a fully separate content track. Quote: *"For me, the point of funneling to LP University is leveraging what we're already showing and teaching to push people over there."*
2. **Volume target:** each team member (Anthony, Amber, Kyle, Emigen) commits to a minimum of 4 video ideas = **16 videos/month**, supplementable with graphics for full monthly coverage.
3. **Content categories approved:** Content Leads & Systems (educational frameworks), Marketing & Branding Fundamentals, AI & Automation, Trends & Pop Culture Analysis, Design & Visual Systems, Personal Brand Building.
4. **Every video needs a defined CTA + tracking purpose:** engagement, LP University subscribe, book consultation, or join email list.
5. **New graphic series approved:** "Graphic Design 101" / "Daily Dose of Design" — Word/Canva/Photoshop tutorials + design principles + brand reviews + a breakdown of Launchpad's own logo redesign (old vs. new, strategic reasoning).

## Video Production Framework (per video)
1. Hook (establish interest)
2. Value (educational payload)
3. Re-hooks (visual/audio/example-based)
4. Call-to-Action (specific, trackable)
5. Point (what does the viewer do next?)

Tracking priority stated directly: *"If what you're creating is not doing anything, it's not working. So we have to figure out: is it the messaging? Is it the type of content? Is it that people don't know what to do?"*

## Newsletter-Based Video Concepts (10 discussed)
1. **Trends and Copying** — how the internet copies trends, how fast TikTok creates patterns; angle is educational (trend-making, not trend-following)
2. **Human Perspective as Creative Asset** — positions human creativity against AI commoditization: *"Human perspective may be the most valuable creative tool left."*
3. **Borrowed Interest** — pop culture/summer movie moments used to capture attention while educating (timed for June)
4. **Will AI Replace Social Media Marketers?** — debate format; pro-AI (bots generate images/copy/captions/scheduling) vs. con-AI (*"AI is only as good as the person who is prompting it"*). Assigned to Anthony.
5. **Claude AI & How to Build a Brain** — deliberately reframed from "Claude AI capabilities" to "how to build a brain" to lean into systems/frameworks positioning rather than pure AI-tool positioning. Assigned to Anthony.
6. **Stealing Like an Artist + Personal Brand Building** — curation vs. creation as a personal-brand-building lens
7. **Graphic Design Fundamentals Series** — foundations before brand-building; tutorial format + Launchpad's own logo redesign case study
8. **Thinking vs. Feeling Marketing** — emotional vs. rational messaging in ads; framework: Audience → Message → Mechanism
9. **Email & SMS Marketing Design & Frequency** — systems approach to direct marketing, framed as entertainment
10. **Market Research & Competitor Ad Analysis** — pattern recognition for identifying whether competitor ads are performing
