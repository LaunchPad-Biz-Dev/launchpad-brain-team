## Top Signals (Score 16+)

### 1. Instagram's new "Originality Score" is quietly killing reach on recycled/reposted content
- **Score:** 19/20 (R:5 V:5 A:5 C:4)
- **Source:** [Velreel](https://velreel.com/blog/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-shifts-2026), [Vidyback](https://vidyback.com/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-2026-small-ecommerce)
- **Summary:** Instagram is rolling out an Originality Score across Reels that limits recommendations for accounts whose posts are mostly reposted or repurposed content that wasn't materially re-created or edited. Brands recycling the same asset across platforms unchanged are seeing 40–80% reach reduction. This is a bigger structural shift than a normal algorithm tweak — it directly penalizes the "same asset everywhere" workflow.
- **Content Angle:** Internal-first: audit whether any client content is being cross-posted identically across IG/FB/TikTok without a platform-specific edit (caption, crop, first-frame, sound). Every asset needs at least one materially distinct element per platform before it ships. Externally, this is also a Launchpad thought-leadership post: "Instagram just started penalizing lazy content — here's what 'materially edited' actually means."
- **Best For:** All active clients (posting workflow audit) + Launchpad Business Development (thought leadership) | **Format:** Internal process check + Carousel/Reel explainer
- **Urgency:** Post today — this is a reach-loss risk sitting live in every account right now, not a future trend

### 3. TikTok's viral bar jumped to 70%+ completion rate (from 50% in 2024); original audio now weighted heavier than trending sounds
- **Score:** 17/20 (R:5 V:4 A:4 C:4)
- **Source:** [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-works-now), [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works)
- **Summary:** TikTok's follower-first distribution model is intact, but the bar to break out has risen sharply — a 70%+ completion rate is now needed to go wide, up from ~50% two years ago. TikTok also rebalanced to reward original audio over recycled trending sounds, and watch time/completion rate now make up 40–50% of all ranking signals.
- **Content Angle:** Edit briefs for every TikTok-active client need tighter hooks (first 2 seconds) and pacing built for full-watch-through, not just a strong opener. Push original voiceover/audio over trending-sound overlays where it fits the brand.
- **Best For:** All TikTok-active clients (food, law, HVAC, health) | **Format:** Adjust edit brief standards going forward
- **Urgency:** Evergreen — update the video-production agent's edit brief defaults
