# ICP v1 Review — Antonia Andrade's Draft

Antonia Andrade (COO/Partner) produced an ICP v1 on 2026-04-20 in Notion (`Sales` page — `33ab01f77d6d80ccbe49e238a9be4366`). Anthony asked whether to use as-is or rebuild. Verdict: **use as strategic framing layer, rebuild as v2 with operational targeting on top.**

## What's strong (keep)
- **Positioning statement:** *"Launchpad partners with growth-stage, revenue-generating businesses in competitive industries to build owned marketing systems, scalable brand authority, and predictable revenue growth."* Tighter than existing [[Ideal Client Profile]] v1.
- **"They don't buy marketing, they buy predictability and control"** — real insight. Content hook.
- **Decision-making structure** (Primary Buyer / Operational Influencer / Strategic Buyer) — directionally correct.
- **"Mid-market" framing** — more disciplined than old ICP's "mid to large."
- **Trigger events (expansion, stagnation, leadership change)** — real and findable.

## What's too vague (fix in v2)

### 1. Firmographic ranges too wide to filter on
- Revenue $500K–$50M = 100x spread. A $500K HVAC shop and a $50M law firm have nothing in common.
- Team 5–50 = 10x spread.
- Marketing spend $5K–$30K/mo = 6x spread. Our Essential package is $2,500/mo — $30K/mo spenders are above our delivery capacity.
- **An AI prospecting agent can't filter on "5–50 employees and $500K–$50M."**

### 2. Contradicts our actual client base
- [[Sales Department]] doc lists 10+ industries. Antonia's lists 4.
- Missing from her list but in active roster: Financial ([[Continental Currency Service]]), Health ([[Upbeat Health]]), Tax ([[Toro Taxes]]), Restaurants/Food ([[Arby's Food Truck]], [[Quesadilla Gorilla]], [[Taco Bell Cantinas]]), B2B Products ([[Sobel Westex]], [[Muller Masks]]), Education ([[Welding School of Nevada]]).

### 3. "Strong Latino market advantage" not operationalized
Mentioned in a table cell. This is one of LP's biggest real differentiators (Anthony, Antonia, Toro Taxes network, Vegas demographics) but nothing tells an SDR where to apply it.

### 4. Missing operational targeting layer
- No disqualifier list ("we do NOT work with…")
- No metro-level geography ("U.S." is not targeting)
- No scrapable signals (behavioral indicators stated but unsearchable)
- No tier structure matched to packages
- No LTGP estimate per tier
- No lead magnet → ICP tier map

### 5. "Post product-market fit" is SaaS language
Reads like a B2B SaaS template applied to local-services. Not wrong — generic.

### 6. Doesn't match Anthony's 4/13 outbound bot spec
His weekly meeting defined outbound targeting as *"businesses running only graphics or only video ads → offer the missing format."* That's specific and scrapable. Antonia's ICP has no equivalent granularity.

### 7. No "best-5-clients-to-clone" reference
Hormozi's rule: find your best 5, study them, sell their twins. Antonia's ICP doesn't name specific clients.

## Resolution (per Scoping Session 2026-04-21)
ICP v2 is built by merging:
1. Antonia's positioning statement + decision-making structure + trigger events + "they buy predictability" insight (strategic framing layer)
2. Anthony's actual client data — the [[Scoping Session 2026-04-21|Case Study Quintet]] (operational proof layer)
3. Hormozi's targeting discipline — disqualifiers, scrapable signals, tier structure, LTGP, lead magnet map (operational targeting layer)
4. Anthony's 4/13 outbound bot spec (tactical scraping layer)

Result: [[Ideal Client Profile]] v2 — Home Services Vegas primary, Law / Franchise secondary, full operational targeting.
