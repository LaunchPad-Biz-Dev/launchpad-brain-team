# PART II — STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

## 2.1 Why this exists

Captured 2026-04-07, Anthony's words:

> *"We have a problem at LaunchPad with not having enough sales and need to work on generating more leads so we can sell more. I want to create a solid agent network for sales and arm it with strong skills so we can 10x our business."*

## 2.2 The intellectual base — Hormozi's `$100M Leads`

We chose one canonical framework so every agent shares vocabulary and every build decision has a precedent. Anthony supplied the book on 2026-04-08; we produced a full breakdown on 2026-04-09. The parts that are load-bearing for this system:

**Lead definitions.** A *lead* is a person you can contact. An **engaged lead** is one who has shown interest. Engaged leads are the real output of advertising. Optimizing list size instead of engagement rate is the classic error.

**The Value Equation.** Every ad, script, magnet, and landing page must push all four levers:

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

**Lead magnets.** A complete solution to a *narrow* problem, given free, whose resolution reveals the bigger problem the core offer solves. 7 steps: pick narrow problem → pick solution type (Reveal a Problem / Sample or Trial / One Step of a Process) → pick delivery (software / info / service / physical) → test the name → make it easy to consume → make it paid-quality → clear CTA with a reason to act now.

**The Core Four** — the only four ways one person can advertise:

|  | Warm (know you) | Cold (strangers) |
|---|---|---|
| **1-to-1** | #1 Warm Outreach | #3 Cold Outreach |
| **1-to-many** | #2 Post Free Content | #4 Paid Ads |

Benchmarks we adopted: warm — 100 reach-outs → ~20 replies → ~4 take a free offer → ~1 pays. Cold email — target 3% engaged-lead rate (30% open × 10% reply). Cold DM with personalized video — up to 20% reply. Cold outreach takes **6–12 months to scale**, and we committed to protecting that timeline rather than killing it at month 2.

**More / Better / New.** More = Rule of 100 (you are doing ~1/100th the required volume). Better = test one thing per week per platform and fix the *constraint*. New = new placements → new platforms → new Core Four activity.

**The 4 Lead Getters** (other people doing the Core Four for you): customer referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates. Referrals compound whenever referral% > churn%.

**The efficiency formula:** LTGP : CAC (Lifetime *Gross Profit*, net of fulfillment, over Cost to Acquire Customer). **3:1 minimum.** Diagnostic: CAC below 3x industry average → your problem is the business model; above → your problem is advertising.

**Client Financed Acquisition.** If a new customer pays more than (CAC + 30-day fulfillment cost) inside 30 days, you can scale on a credit card indefinitely.

### Where Hormozi needed adaptation for an agency

This mattered and is worth flagging to any reviewer:

1. He sells info products, gyms, supplements, software — near-zero marginal fulfillment cost. We sell **services with real human delivery cost**. Our LTGP:CAC math and time-to-prove are different.
2. "Give away the farm" is dangerous for us. A free half-day shoot is a real cost. We **productized** the giveaway instead (see the Growth Audit).
3. "First 5 customers free" applies to *new service lines*, not a 10-year-old agency.
4. Affiliates bring leads to a **magnet**, not the core offer — our team still qualifies and closes.
5. 100/day warm outreach creates an approval bottleneck, because our AI is never permitted to impersonate Anthony. Resolved with a draft → approve → send pattern.
6. Cold outreach compliance (CAN-SPAM, state rules, opt-out lists) is a hard constraint for B2B services.

# PART III — THE LOCKED DECISIONS

A 90-minute scoping session on **2026-04-21** answered 21 open questions and greenlit the build. These are the decisions the whole system was built against.

## 3.1 North Star
**10 new LPBD full-service clients in 60 days (by 2026-06-20).**

Consequence: cold outreach alone could not hit it (30-day email warmup minimum), so Phase 1 led with channels that close in 0–45 days.

## 3.2 Offer & positioning

**Grand Slam Offer (locked):**

> *"We help mid-market service businesses generate predictable monthly leads and build a brand that reflects their service quality — done-for-you in 90 days, without you touching content, ads, or CRM."*

Value-equation decomposition: Dream = predictable leads + brand authority. Likelihood = done-for-you + case studies across 10+ verticals + 10-year track record. Time delay = 90 days to measurable lift. Effort = owner never touches content/ads/CRM.

**One front door:** LPBD. Productions and BWU become cross-sells *after* the client is in. This simplified messaging, ICP, magnet, and every ad.

**Parked (and still parked):** true fulfillment cost per package. Requires a QuickBooks pull and a working session. **This blocks real LTGP:CAC math** — see Section 12.

## 3.3 Channel priority (revised in-session once the 80%-referral fact surfaced)

1. **Referral engine** (moved from Phase 3 → #1 priority)
2. **Warm outreach** (Anthony's + Abril's + Alex's lists, 100/day, ACA framework)
3. **Growth Audit lead magnet** (the distribution mechanism for every channel)
4. **Content-for-leads** (@anthony.launchpad, 4:1 give:ask, Growth Audit CTA)
5. **CRM hygiene + scoring** (enables all of the above)

Cold outreach: **infrastructure warmup Day 1, active sending Day 30.**
Paid ads: start Month 2, $500–$1,500/mo, scale with closes, pending LTGP data.

**Closing split:** Anthony closes all hot/qualified leads, all Comprehensive ($3,500) and Ultimate ($4,500) deals, all custom packages. Alex closes Essential ($2,500) standard deals. **Routing happens post-discovery, never pre-discovery** — Anthony qualifies first.

**Approval flow:** Weeks 1–4 the AI drafts every message; Anthony or Alex approves batches in Slack once daily (~10 min). Week 5+ Anthony approves *templates* and the AI sends at volume. Replies always route to a human. Hot replies escalate immediately, always.

## 5.2 What actually got built (8 agents, shipped)

The roster was reordered once the 80%-referral fact surfaced. These exist as real, deployed agent definitions:

### `referral-agent` — Phase 1 Priority #1
Owns the referral engine end-to-end. Tracks Quintet referral calls, drafts BAMFAM check-in follow-ups, manages the $500 payout ledger (triggers at the 90-day retention mark), runs quarterly goodwill audits scoring clients on likelihood to refer, generates referral-ask content for Anthony's posts, and converts warm referrals into CRM contacts with attribution tags.

### `warm-outreach`
Mines Anthony's 10+ years of contacts across phone, email, LinkedIn, Slack, past clients, past leads. Generates up to **100 personalized drafts/day** using the **ACA framework (Acknowledge → Compliment → Ask)**, plus the **9-word reactivation email** (*"Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"*) for dormant contacts. Drafts approved in Slack, then sent via Instantly (email), LinkedIn, or CRM SMS. Escalates any contact who has replied 3x without an ask to offer-stage.

### `growth-audit` — the lead magnet engine
Given a business name, city, and website, it scrapes GMB, Meta/Google/TikTok ad libraries, IG/FB/TikTok presence, website speed and conversion signals, and reviews — scores against a weighted rubric — and produces a 1-page PDF report plus internal call-prep notes. Two tracks (Lead / Content). Feeds three workflows: live 30-min delivery calls, the public landing page (`growthaudit.launchpadbizdev.com`), and CRM contact creation with raw data attached.

### `ig-cold-prospecting` — the Meta-safe outbound pattern
Runs daily. Scrapes IG business accounts filtered by Vegas metro + industry + follower range + activity signals. For each of **20 prospects/day** it reads the bio and last 3 posts and writes a personalized 2–3 sentence DM referencing something specific. Writes all 20 to the CRM with tags, then Slack-pings Anthony with the queue link. **Anthony sends manually — 10–15 min of copy-paste per day. No automation ever touches his IG account.** This design exists entirely because of the ban-risk constraint in Section 6.3.

### `lead-magnet-builder`
Produces Grand-Slam-quality magnets via Hormozi's 7 steps, with Value Equation math shown. Outputs the magnet itself, headline/CTA variants, landing-page brief, and a justification doc. The Growth Audit is the flagship; this agent produces vertical variants and test candidates.

### `content-for-leads`
Hook → Retain → Reward content engineered for *engaged leads*, not audience growth. Enforces the 4:1 give:ask ratio. Every post is tagged by hook type, retain structure (List / Steps / Story), and reward payload. Ask posts route to the Growth Audit CTA. Coexists with our brand-building Content Creator agent — different job, shared skill.

### `crm-hygiene-scoring`
Runs 7 AM daily. Sweeps new contacts from every channel, dedupes, applies a scoring rubric (Hot / Warm / Cold / Dead plus a 1–5 fit score), routes Hot leads to Slack, queues 9-word reactivation emails for 90+ day dormant contacts, and produces a **"Top 20 to Work"** digest Anthony opens when he starts. Never auto-deletes — proposes merges in a weekly review.

### `proposal-invoice-agent`
Drafts proposals (cover, package framing, deliverables, pricing table, terms) and 3-piece invoice packages (executive summary + email + SMS). **Never sends** — all output is DRAFT routed to Anthony or Abril, then delivered by a human via PandaDoc (proposals) or the CRM (invoices).

## 6.3 Outbound infrastructure + the security constraint

**The constraint that shaped everything:** Anthony's main Meta/Facebook profile holds access to 10+ client ad accounts through Business Manager. A ban would be a catastrophic loss of client delivery capacity — far more expensive than any outbound campaign is worth. **Therefore no automation ever touches the main profile.**

| Platform | Strategy |
|---|---|
| @anthony.launchpad (IG) | Organic content only. No automation. Primary top-of-funnel. |
| New IG (e.g. @launchpad.growth) | New email + phone, fully isolated from Business Manager |
| New FB page | Tied to the new IG, isolated |
| New TikTok | Isolated for outreach |
| LinkedIn | Anthony's existing profile with conservative automation at 20–30/day. A second profile is a TOS violation — not an option. |

**New-account warmup rule:** separate Gmail + separate phone number; post 2–3x/week of real content for **30 days before any outreach**; cross-post from @anthony.launchpad during warmup; bio + link to the Growth Audit landing page; verified business profile where possible.

**Email:** secondary domains only for cold — the main CRM sending domain stays clean. 1 pre-warmed inbox + 2 fresh domains, warmed via a dedicated warmup tool. Warmup starts Day 1, active sending Day 30.

**Estimated net new tooling spend:** ~$180/mo (warmup/sending tool + LinkedIn tool + domains). An earlier external plan estimated $280/mo; it double-counted tools we already own.

**Volume math we planned against:** 1,000 touches → ~35 replies → ~12 qualified → 4–6 booked calls → **1–2 clients/month.** Multichannel sequences benchmark at roughly 287% more responses than single-channel.

**Reply qualification schema:** Hot / Warm / No / Out-of-office — only *engaged* leads get promoted into the CRM, keeping the pipeline clean.

# PART VIII — WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED

This is the most important section for anyone evaluating the system. **We are reporting the failure honestly because the failure is the finding.**

## 8.1 The Phase 1 retro (2026-04-21 → 2026-05-20)

**What was built:** 8 agents, ~40 CRM assets, the Growth Audit tool, the ICP, the playbooks, the dashboards spec, the infrastructure plan. All of it real, all of it working when invoked.

**What it produced in 30 days:**
- **0 contacts**
- **0 opportunities**
- **1 test record**

**What Anthony produced in the same 30 days, by hand:** **3 signed clients** — WYLEE'S School, Total Tax Resolution, SHUMI Nation.

**The lesson, stated plainly in our own retro:** *Anthony in front of people closes. The autonomous robot did not.*

## 8.2 Why it didn't run — our honest read

1. **Every agent was built draft-first with a human approval gate, and the human gate never opened.** The approval bottleneck was correctly identified as a risk and then became the actual failure mode. A system that requires 10 minutes of daily approval from the busiest person in the company will produce zero output on any day that person is busy — which is every day.
2. **No owner.** Alex was designated, onboarding was pending, and the daily operator role was never actually staffed. The agents had no one whose job was to run them.
3. **We built the machine before proving the motion by hand.** Nobody had manually run 100 warm reach-outs and measured the reply rate before we automated 100 warm reach-outs.
4. **The North Star was too aggressive for the channel mix.** 10 clients in 60 days, with cold requiring a 30-day warmup, meant the plan depended almost entirely on referral + warm, which are exactly the channels that depend most on Anthony's personal time — the scarcest resource in the company.
5. **Build velocity felt like progress.** Shipping 8 agents and 40 assets in 30 days produced a strong sense of momentum that masked the fact that zero prospects had been contacted.
6. **The blocking data was never unblocked.** Fulfillment cost and LTGP were parked on day one as "not blocking Phase 1." True in a narrow sense, but it meant no channel could be evaluated economically, so no channel could be confidently scaled or killed.

## 8.3 The pivot (2026-05-20)

Direction shift, not a teardown. The spear became:

> **"Watch a real marketing agency run itself on AI."**

Not "AI for business" in the abstract (crowded, commoditizes fast) — the defensible angle is **proof, not theory**: Anthony documents the real, ongoing build of LaunchPad's AI system, failures included (the Phase 1 retro itself becomes content). The market research finding: the AI-creator space is full of *teachers*; nobody at scale is documenting a **real client-serving agency actually operating on AI**. That lane is open.

**New offer ladder:** free content → free AI Readiness Audit + low-ticket webinar (~$100) → AI build session (~$1,500, "build your Brain") → full-service marketing / ongoing AI retainer / courses. **LPBD, Productions, and BWU become the back end, not the front door.**

**How the Phase 1 assets reposition — the chassis is reused:**

|---|---|
| Growth Audit | → **AI Readiness Audit** — same scrape/score/report mechanic, new rubric (where AI saves this business time and money) |
| Audit scraper/scorer/report skills | Reused as-is; swap rubric + template |
| content-for-leads | Side channel → **centerpiece**, running the anchor series |
| referral-agent | Keep, lean — referrals are still ~80% of revenue |
| warm-outreach | Keep, de-emphasized — warm list invited to the webinar/audit, not pitched a retainer |
| ig-cold-prospecting / cold | **Shrinks hard** — Anthony does not want a cold grind; at most light webinar invites |

**The three questions flagged as make-or-break, still open:**
1. **The consistency engine** — 3 posts/day plus an anchor series for months. Consistency is our proven weak spot. Design it *first*.
2. **The ops owner** — who runs LPBD/Productions/BWU day-to-day while Anthony becomes creator + closer.
3. **The delivery model** — who fulfills $1,500 AI builds at volume. Currently only Anthony + the AI, which does not scale past a handful.

# PART IX — WHAT WE'D KEEP AND WHAT WE'D CHANGE

Our own assessment, offered so an external reviewer can push back on it rather than rediscover it.

## 9.1 Keep

- **The Growth Audit as the magnet.** The mechanic is right, it's productized, the scraping works, and it gives away real value at near-zero marginal cost.
- **The scrapable-signal targeting layer.** "You're running graphics but no video" is a specific, verifiable, personalized opener that makes the first touch about them.
- **The Meta-safe manual-send pattern** for IG. AI researches and drafts, human sends. It respects the real constraint instead of pretending it away.
- **The isolated CRM sub-account.** Clean data, contained blast radius.
- **Referral as priority #1.** 80% of revenue and it was, at the time, entirely informal.
- **The Hormozi vocabulary.** It eliminated a large class of taste-based arguments — build decisions now cite a framework.

## 9.2 Change

- **The approval gate has to be redesigned, not tightened.** Requiring the CEO's daily approval is what killed throughput. The realistic patterns: pre-approved templates with post-hoc audit, a non-CEO operator owning the daily queue, or hard batch-sending on a fixed schedule with an exception-only escalation path.
- **Prove the motion manually before automating it.** 50 manual DMs with a measured reply rate is worth more than 8 agents.
- **Unblock the economics.** Fulfillment cost per package → LTGP → max allowable CAC. Everything downstream (paid ads, affiliates, channel kill/scale decisions) is guessing until this exists.
- **One channel, 30 days, measured.** Anthony wrote this himself in June and it is still the right instruction.
- **Name a daily operator who is not Anthony.** Every version of this plan that depends on Anthony's daily attention has failed the same way.
- **Set channel-appropriate timelines.** Cold takes 6–12 months. A 60-day North Star built on cold is a plan to quit at day 45.
