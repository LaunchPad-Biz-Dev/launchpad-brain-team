DRG and Taco Bell Cantinas should test one texture-first hero shot (crispy taco shell, crunchy topping) this week instead of a wide plate shot. ### 2. Instagram now actively penalizes reposted content and reweights DM shares above likes/comments
- **Score:** 17/20 (R:5 V:4 A:4 C:4)
- **Source:** [LeeSeoHits — Instagram Algorithm Update 2026](https://leeseohits.com/blog/industry-news/instagram-algorithm-update-2026-new-ranking-factors), [Velreel — Instagram/TikTok Algorithm Shifts 2026](https://velreel.com/blog/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-shifts-2026)
- **Summary:** Instagram's AI fingerprinting now detects content originally posted elsewhere (including unmodified TikTok reposts) and suppresses it — original-to-Instagram content gets up to 3x more distribution. Separately, DM shares ("sends") now outweigh likes/comments as a ranking signal; Instagram is measuring total watch time + replay rate over raw view count.
- **Content Angle:** Internal workflow note more than a client post: stop cross-posting unmodified TikTok exports to Instagram — re-cut or re-export natively. Caption CTAs should nudge toward "send this to someone who..." rather than "like if..." to earn the send-weighted signal.
- **Best For:** All clients (SMM workflow change) | **Format:** Internal posting-process adjustment
- **Urgency:** Queue for this week — update social-media-poster's export habits
