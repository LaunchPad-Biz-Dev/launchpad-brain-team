## What Google Antigravity Is

**Google Antigravity is Google's AI-powered agentic IDE** — their direct competitor to Claude Code. Think of it as Google's version of what we use at Launchpad, built on top of VS Code but redesigned around autonomous AI agents.

Announced November 2025 alongside Gemini 3. Currently in public preview, free for individuals. Credits cost $25 for 2,500.

### Key Features
- **Editor View** — AI-powered coding with tab completions and inline commands (similar to Claude Code's standard mode)
- **Manager Surface** — spawn, orchestrate, and observe multiple agents working asynchronously across different workspaces (similar to Claude Code's multi-agent orchestration)
- Powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini Flash models
- Fork of Visual Studio Code

### How It Compares to Our Stack

### Why This Matters for Launchpad

- Could be worth testing for specific use cases where Gemini's image gen or search grounding is stronger
