## Platform Updates

- **Instagram:** Likes downgraded as a ranking signal; saves, shares, and DM conversations now weighted higher. Accounts that repeatedly repost others' content lose recommendation eligibility. Instagram now pays creators for photos, not just Reels.
- **TikTok:** Distribution now runs on a "follower test" — a new video is shown to a small slice of your followers first; if they don't engage, it dies before reaching a wider audience. Keep follower engagement (not just reach) top of mind.
- **YouTube:** Starting August 24, 2026, YouTube counts a view the instant a video starts playing (previously required a few seconds), aligning it with how TikTok/Reels/Shorts already count views. Expect view counts to look inflated vs. historical benchmarks — don't over-read a "spike."
- **Google Ads:** Starting September 1, 2026, campaigns using Automatically Created Assets or campaign-level broad match will be auto-upgraded to AI Max for Search. Google is also merging Local Service Ads into the main Google Ads interface (migration running through 2027). Flag for any client running Google Search — settings may shift without an explicit opt-in.
