### 1. Instagram Flipped Its Algorithm: DM Shares Now Worth 3-5x a Like
- **Score:** 19/20 (R:5 V:5 A:4 C:5)
- **Source:** Buffer, Sprout Social, CreatorFlow, GoneTech — confirmed by Instagram's own Adam Mosseri
- **Summary:** Instagram's biggest algorithm overhaul since Reels went live. Sends/DM shares are now weighted 3-5x higher than likes for distribution. A DM shares-per-reach ratio above 2% triggers viral push; above 3% Instagram aggressively pushes Reels to non-followers. Original content gets up to 3x more distribution than repurposed content; accounts posting 10+ reposts in 30 days get excluded from recommendations entirely.
- **Content Angle:** Every client needs content designed to be SHARED, not just liked. Shift caption strategy to include explicit "send this to someone who needs it" CTAs. Audit every piece of content for reshareability before posting. For law clients: "Send this to someone dealing with X." For HVAC: "Tag a homeowner who needs to hear this." For health: "Send this to a friend."
- **Best For:** ALL CLIENTS (immediate strategy shift) | **Format:** Reels, Carousels with shareable takeaway slides
- **Urgency:** POST TODAY — this is live now and many competitors don't know yet
