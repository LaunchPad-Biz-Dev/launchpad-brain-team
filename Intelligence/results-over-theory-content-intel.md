# Results Over Theory — Content Format & Trend Intelligence

Living note for market-research findings specific to Anthony's ROT personal brand. Append new dated entries below — do not overwrite.

## 2026-07-15 — Top-Performing Formats/Hooks Research (feeds video-production shoot brief)

Full report: `marketing/output/reports/results-over-theory-trend-report-2026-07-15.md`

**Key findings:**
- The "teach you to build an AI agency" lane (Nick Saraev, Liam Ottley, Maker School, AI Automation Agency Hub) remains saturated and increasingly tainted by "AI course scam" search-result association. ROT's "show a real client-serving agency" lane is still the open gap — confirms 2026-05-20 niche research, now with fresh July 2026 corroboration.
- **LinkedIn document/carousel posts are the highest-engagement format in the niche (6.60% avg engagement, 3.7x text posts).** Named frameworks (proprietary-sounding methods, not generic tips) drive the highest save rates. The existing Anchor Series (12-15 slide LinkedIn doc) is already the correct format — do not change it.
- **Short-form is consolidating around single-person, straight-to-camera or screen+narration "real work" footage over skits/green-screen/polish.** 30-90 sec, 50-60s is the sweet spot (76% watch-through). Cheapest to produce — screen recording + voice narration needs no separate shoot day.
- **"100% human" / anti-AI-slop backlash is a live tailwind for ROT.** Audiences are ~4x more likely to distrust brands when AI content is recognizable as AI-generated (em-dash overuse, words like "delve/leverage/robust/streamline/harness/tapestry/landscape/paradigm/synergy/ecosystem"). ROT's "AI runs ops, a human runs the voice" framing directly rides this trend — say it explicitly in content.
- **Build-in-public content rewards specificity + sustainable cadence over frequency.** Real client-conversation-sourced posts on a rhythm you can hold for a year outperform viral-hook-chasing daily posting. This validates the Anchor Series' weekly, sacred 16-week cadence — do not increase frequency to chase early reach.
- **Founder vulnerability converts only when tethered to a real business stake** (a mistake, a trade-off, a specific lesson) — not generic personal disclosure. "Almost lost a client" style stories outperform "entrepreneurship is hard" style stories.
- **Instagram's "Your Algo" filtering has expanded from Reels to the main feed** — off-pillar posts now risk real suppression, not just lower reach. Every ROT IG post must telegraph one of the four pillars (Built Live / Founder Story / Anti-Template / Operator's Playbook) within the first 2 seconds.
- **Winning hook shapes:** Contrarian Claim ("Most people get this wrong about—"), Mistake Warning ("I almost lost a client because—"), Named-Framework List Tease ("Here's the exact system we used to—"). Hooks must be proof-driven (a number, a before/after) not adjective-driven, and land in 3 seconds / 10-14 words for short-form.
- **What to avoid:** AI-agency-teacher framing, AI-tell vocabulary, skits/green-screen/trending-audio-first Reels, oversharing untethered to a business stake, replacement-fear AI framing (2026 SMB sentiment data shows "AI as productivity multiplier freeing human judgment" beats "AI will replace you" messaging).

**7 proven templates identified** (full detail in report): Anchor Doc (LinkedIn carousel), Screen+Narration clip, Almost-Loss founder story, Named Framework carousel, Side-by-Side Teardown, Day-in-the-Build mini-vlog, Receipts-not-Claims proof clip.
