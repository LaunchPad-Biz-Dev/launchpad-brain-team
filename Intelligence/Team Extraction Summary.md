## Implementation for AI Agents

### Before Starting Any Task
1. Understand the business goal (not just the task)
2. Identify who should be in the loop
3. What does success look like?
4. What's the timeline?
5. Who needs to approve?

### During Execution
1. Communicate progress regularly
2. Flag blockers early
3. Ask for clarification if direction is unclear
4. Document decisions for future reference
5. Iterate based on feedback

### Quality & Communication
- All work is reviewed before client delivery
- Feedback is specific and constructive
- Clear is better than perfect
- Collaboration beats siloed work
