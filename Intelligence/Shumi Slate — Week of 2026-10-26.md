## This Week At A Glance

The entire scan this week bends around one event: **Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Episode 50, "DEATH & STRAWBERRY,"** the series finale, which premiered Monday Oct 26 — a title that calls back to Kubo's original 2001 chapter one, closing a 25-year loop. The TikTok scan confirms it's not just news, it's the dominant content format right now: five of the top signals this week are Bleach nostalgia/finale edits (one nostalgia retrospective alone pulled 794.6K views using the real licensed ending theme). Shumi stocks Bleach — GEM Series, Megahouse, Funko, Chimi-Mega Buddy — but a site check shows most current SKUs reading sold out, which turns the finale into a built-in restock/waitlist moment, the same proven pattern from last week's exclusives push. One Piece gets real product news too: a new Funko wave including the Saint Jaygarcia Saturn 10" Jumbo Pop in beast form, plus Perona chase variants and an Usopp Elbaf-outfit figure — strong even with the manga on a planned hiatus until Nov 1 (ch. 1197). JJK keeps showing up as the account's most reliable high-leverage format, this week via Toji Fushiguro and "You Rock My World"-style edit content rather than a specific news beat.

Competitor read: Hot Topic's "choose your MVP (most valuable pj's)" static post is the standout of the week at 19,648 likes/102 comments — low-text, casual, no hard sell — while Tokyo Otaku Mode's spec-sheet carousel format (product photo + full series/manufacturer/height/materials breakdown) is a format worth borrowing for figure showcases. Crunchyroll's own IG leans into meta-memes over screenshots ("people you may know... all your enemies," 7,394 likes) rather than product content — useful proof that humor-first, product-later still works even for the big licensed players. One scrape caveat: no single trending sound dominated TikTok this week — nearly every top performer runs original/custom-mixed audio tied to the specific edit, so the signal to copy is the fast-cut AMV-edit *format*, not one sound ID (see Trending Audio section).
