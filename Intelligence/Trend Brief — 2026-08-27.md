### 2. Instagram is quietly killing likes as a ranking signal
- **Score:** 18/20 (R:5 V:4 A:4 C:5)
- **Source:** [Social Media Algorithm Aug 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/social-media-algorithm), [Instagram/TikTok Ecommerce Changes](https://vidyback.com/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-2026-small-ecommerce)
- **Summary:** Instagram has downgraded likes and is weighting saves, shares (especially DM shares), and comments heavily. Content designed to be sent to a friend now outperforms content designed to be liked.
- **Content Angle:** Shift caption CTAs from "double tap if you agree" to "send this to someone who needs it" / "save this for later." Favor carousels and list-format posts (naturally saved/shared) over single-image posts.
- **Best For:** All active social clients — flag to social-media-poster agent as a caption-writing rule
- **Urgency:** Post today — this changes how every caption should be written going forward
