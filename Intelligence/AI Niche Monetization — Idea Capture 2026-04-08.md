# AI Niche Monetization — Idea Capture
**Date:** 2026-04-08
**Source:** Anthony Mendoza via Apollo Slack DM
**Category:** Business Strategy / New Revenue Stream Idea

---

## The Idea

Mass produce content in the AI niche, then create and sell **skill packs and courses** on how to use AI.

**Anthony's exact message:**
> "Mass produce content in ai niche, create and sell skill packs and courses on how to use ai"

---

## What This Could Mean

### Skill Packs
Package the actual Claude Code skills we're building (content-creator, campaign-strategist, brand-compliance, etc.) as downloadable, installable products that other agencies or business owners can drop into their own Claude setup.

- Price point: $47–$297 per skill pack depending on complexity
- Market: Other marketing agencies, freelancers, business owners curious about AI
- Distribution: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or a dedicated LP University product shelf

### Courses — "How to Use AI in Your Business"
Tie directly into the Building in Public series. Document the journey → convert the journey into a course curriculum.

- LP University already exists as the enrollment funnel
- The Building in Public series IS the free course teaser content
- Course curriculum could follow the series pillars: The Brain → The Agents → The Workflows → The Outputs → The Results
