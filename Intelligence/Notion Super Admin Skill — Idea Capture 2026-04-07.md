## Anthony's Vision (cleaned up from voice-to-text)

Create a skill to be a powerful Notion agent that works as a super admin:

### Core Capabilities
- **Database management** — Create databases, fill out empty fields in existing databases (like client portals)
- **Stuck item detection** — See when things are stuck in areas too long (like onboarding sections)
- **Overall optimization** — Help maintain and optimize Notion as a whole

### Safety Rules
- **Never delete without permission** — Must ask Anthony, Emigen, or Abril before deleting files or making big structural changes
- **Run-of-the-mill tasks are autonomous** — Routine Notion admin work can be done without asking
- **Big changes require approval** — Contact Anthony, Emigen, or Abril

### Key Functions
- Go through meeting transcripts and extract action items
- Send action items to Emigen so she can follow up with the team
- Track task assignment and completion
- Ping/message people when deadlines are approaching
- Make sure tasks from meetings get into deliverable trackers and accountability follow-ups
- Keep bringing clients and team members back to Notion as the central hub

### Philosophy
Optimize Notion as a task management system. Make it the main source/hub for the business so everyone can work efficiently through projects.
