## The core idea — the Anchor Series

- Find **ONE** simple, repeatable content series. Double down. Repeat it until it stops working (done right, it may never stop).
- This is **top-of-funnel** — "client acquisition content." Its only job is to bring people in.
- Pattern: a **fixed hook + ONE variable** that changes each episode. The format never changes.
- Stop the "content treadmill" (new idea / film / edit / post / repeat for 200 views). One thing that works, forever.
- Examples cited: daily gambling game (10k→110k followers, 50M views), 31 days no hair wash (78M views, 170k followers), one debate question per day (100M views), bucket of water daily.

## The litmus test — 3 pillars: Relevant, Structured, True

Run every content idea through these. Power compounds R → S → T.

1. **Relevant** — grabs attention, stops the scroll. Tap a current cultural conversation OR an always-relevant problem. You *join* a conversation, you don't create one. This is the price of admission — it gets the algorithm to show you to the right people.
2. **Structured** — keeps them watching. Story: **setup → challenge/conflict → resolution (catharsis)**. Beginning, middle, end. The setup must land in the first few seconds. Good structure also makes content memorable + retellable.
3. **True** — **human truth**: a universal, unspoken emotional reality. This is what makes people **share and act** — the core engine of virality. (e.g. the friction of coexisting with others, power dynamics, the need to be seen and accepted.) Show it, don't state it.

Relevant alone = decent views. All three = mega-viral (5M+).

## Operating rules

- **One goal per piece of content** — views OR sales OR followers. Not all three. Start with views.
- **Simplicity is harder than complexity.** Strip back, remove elements. Start simple; add layers only once it's working.
- **It's execution, not the idea.** If a series underperforms, the concept is usually fine — fix the execution. One word of on-screen text can 10x it ("boss" beat "CEO").
- **Iterate daily, track average views.** Milestones: 200 → 2,000 → 10–20k → 100–200k. Rising average = it's working.
- **Sell the feeling, not the product.** Content shows the *dynamic/feeling* associated with the business — never a sales pitch.
- **Cross-post everywhere the format is accepted** — IG, TikTok, FB, YT Shorts, LinkedIn, Snap. (Pizza Hut case: adding FB + YT = +50% views at $0 extra.)

## Most relevant to the Anthony Mendoza personal brand

The webinar directly backs the personal-brand play:

- **"B2B = selling trust."** Companies all offer similar services — what makes you unique is the *people* behind it. Service businesses sell people, not services.
- attn:seeker says **~half their own business** comes from the founder writing daily on LinkedIn about the personal journey of being a business owner — no video required.
- Content *about people* is the easiest, most repeatable content there is.

→ The "Anthony Mendoza as the funnel" instinct isn't just a hunch — it's the exact model Stanley runs.

## How to apply

- **Content Creator + content-for-leads agents:** adopt the R/S/T litmus test as a pre-publish check.
- **Brand discovery session:** Anthony needs ONE anchor series + ONE human truth — not a fan of scattered topics. Ties directly to the "spear, not a fan" point from the 2026-05-20 strategy discussion.
- Don't optimize Anthony's content for sales early — optimize for **views**, then followers, then conversion.
