### 1. Instagram just changed three carousel rules at once — double the slides, "Sends" beat "Likes," and reposting without adding value gets suppressed
- **Score:** 19/20 (R:5 V:5 A:5 C:4)
- **Source:** [Social Media Algorithm Changes 2026 — PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/social-media-algorithm-changes-2026), [Instagram Algorithm Update 2026 — Velreel](https://velreel.com/blog/instagram-tiktok-algorithm-shifts-2026), [How the Instagram Algorithm Works — Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/)
- **Summary:** Instagram carousels can now hold up to 20 photos/videos (double the old 10-slide cap), the algorithm now weights private Shares ("Sends") above Likes, and accounts that repost without adding real value get actively suppressed in favor of the original creator. A new "Reset Suggested Content" setting also lets users wipe their recommendation history.
- **Content Angle:** Two concrete changes for every client's content batch: (1) carousels can now go deeper — a "complete guide" or before/after/during story can run the full 15-20 slides instead of getting cut at 10; (2) the last slide should always carry a reason to share ("send this to someone who needs to see it"), since Sends now outrank Likes as a ranking signal.
- **Best For:** All clients, especially carousel-heavy ones (BWU, Toro Taxes Franchise, Matsuda Nadig/MN Van Law/Afshar Law) | **Format:** Carousel structure + caption CTA update
- **Urgency:** This week — update the carousel template and CTA line before the next batch goes out

### 2. TikTok raised the bar for going viral — 70% completion rate is the new floor, and original audio now beats trending sounds
- **Score:** 17/20 (R:4 V:5 A:5 C:3)

TikTok also rebalanced to reward original audio more heavily — trending sounds still work, but a client's own voice/audio now gets a boost it didn't get before.
- **Content Angle:** Tighten the first 3 seconds and trim anything that doesn't earn its place — a 70% completion bar punishes slow opens hard. Also: stop defaulting to trending audio for every clip; a clean original voiceover (the client talking, not a trending sound) is now algorithmically favored, not just a brand-voice nice-to-have.
- **Best For:** All clients running Reels/TikTok, especially video-heavy ones (Durango HVAC, Arby's Food Truck, Upbeat Health) | **Format:** Video pacing + audio standard update
