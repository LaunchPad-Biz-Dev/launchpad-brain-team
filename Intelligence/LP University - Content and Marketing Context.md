## OBJECTION HANDLING (In Copy & Ads)

**"Isn't this just another course?"**
→ "No. You get weekly modules, worksheets for your actual business, live calls with the team, and a community of people executing together. Most courses are passive video. This is a system."

**"I've wasted money on courses before"**
→ "Understood. That's why Chapter 1 is free so you can see how we teach. And certification programs have live feedback on real projects—not just 'pass the quiz.'"

**"I don't have time"**
→ "It's 3-5 hours per chapter, spread over weeks. You watch on your schedule. And the worksheet + template save you 20+ hours of figuring it out from scratch."

**"Will this actually work for my business?"**
→ "This is the framework we use with clients paying $20K/month. The principles work across industries (real estate, fitness, solar, services, SaaS, etc.). What changes is the tactical execution."

**"Why should I trust you?"**
→ "Kyle Fuller has 2.5M followers on TikTok. Anthony has 10+ years running a full-service marketing agency. We're not selling theory. We're selling what we actually do."

**"Isn't this expensive?"**
→ "Chapter 1 is $97. Chapter 2 is $197. Compared to a single month of an agency ($3K) or a bootcamp ($2K), this is an investment, not an expense. And you keep the templates forever."
