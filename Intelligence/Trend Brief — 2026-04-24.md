- **Score:** 19/20 (R:5 V:5 A:5 C:4)

- **Content Angle:** Every client needs to hear this. Launchpad creates a carousel: "Why likes don't grow your account anymore (and what does)." Every content brief going forward should end with a share hook — "Send this to a business owner who needs to hear it." Client-facing implication: if your content isn't being shared in DMs, your reach is capped.

- **Urgency:** Post today — evergreen insight but currently peaking in discussion
