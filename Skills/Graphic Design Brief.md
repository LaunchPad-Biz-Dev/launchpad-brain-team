# Graphic Design Brief

## Purpose
A graphic design brief gives designers clarity on the visual direction, messaging, and deliverables for a graphics project. It prevents back-and-forth revisions by aligning on aesthetic, tone, and technical specs upfront.

**Golden Rule:** Show, don't just tell. Use moodboards, color references, and do/don't examples alongside written direction.

---

## When to Use
- Social media graphics (carousels, single posts, stories)
- Print materials (flyers, business cards, posters)
- Ad creative (static images for Meta, Google, TikTok)
- Moodboards or design system documentation
- Client brand guideline graphics

---

**Brand Positioning:**
[How does the client position themselves?]

[Who are we speaking to? What do they value?]

[Professional, playful, urgent, educational, warm, etc.]

## 3. VISUAL DIRECTION

### Color Palette
- Primary colors: [hex codes or brand colors]
- Secondary colors: [additional palette]
- Avoid: [colors that clash or don't fit]
- Special effects: [aura, gradients, overlays, etc.] and HOW OFTEN to use them

### Typography
- Headline font: [font name or style]
- Body font: [font name or style]
- Sizing hierarchy: [H1 = X, H2 = Y, body = Z]

### Imagery Style
- Photography style: [clean, raw, lifestyle, product-focused, diverse, etc.]
- People representation: [ages, ethnicities, contexts - be specific]
- Product/asset presence: [should product always be visible? how prominent?]
- Avoid: [clinical, hospital, cold, overly corporate, etc.]

### Visual Elements
- Patterns or textures: [if any]
- Icons or illustrations: [style and when to use]
- White space: [generous/minimal/balanced]
- Overall feel: [modern, classic, playful, serious, etc.]

## Real-World Examples

### Example 1: Upbeat Health – Graphics Moodboard Brief

```markdown
# 🎨 UPBEAT HEALTH – MARCH GRAPHICS BRIEF

**Client:** Upbeat Health
**Designer:** Arija (El Salvador Team)
**Deliverables:** 24 Graphics (12 stills + 12 carousels)
**Due Date:** March 31, 2026

---

## OBJECTIVE

Design a monthly content calendar of 24 social media graphics that build **trust, education, and connection** around Upbeat Health's two core services: **Mobile Wound Care** (60%) and **Primary Care** (20%), with 20% supporting services/brand content.

---

## VISUAL DIRECTION

### Color Palette
- Primary: Upbeat brand blue + warm neutral tones
- Aura-inspired colors: Soft lavender, rose gold, peachy undertones (use OCCASIONALLY, not in every graphic)
- Accent: Calming greens, warm whites
- Avoid: Clinical whites, sterile hospital colors, overly medical blues

### Imagery Style
- **Tone:** Upbeat, uplifting, radiant, hopeful
- **People:** All ages, all health stages (seniors softer/warmer, adults vibrant, families inclusive)
- **Contexts:** Real-life moments (conversations, smiles, movement), NOT clinical settings
- **CRITICAL:** No hospital environments, medical rooms, or clinical details in mobile wound care imagery
- **Avoid:** Sickly aesthetics, downcast expressions, overly medical or injury-focused visuals

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold, clean sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- Body: Readable sans-serif (Open Sans or Proxima Nova)
- Hierarchy: Large headlines, supporting subtext

---

## CONTENT BREAKDOWN

### STILL GRAPHICS (12 total)

**Wound Care (7 stills):**
1. "Advanced Wound Care Done Right" — clinical excellence + innovation
2. "Chronic Wounds Need Consistent Care" — progress messaging
3. "Healing Does Not End After Surgery" — aftercare focus
4. "Preventing Infection Is Part of Healing" — care details
5. "Modern Tools, Better Outcomes" — advanced therapies
6. "You Are Doing the Right Thing" — caregiver reassurance
7. "Care With Respect and Intention" — dignity messaging

**Primary Care (3 stills):**
8. "Primary Care That Grows With You" — lifelong care
9. "More Than a Visit" — relationship-driven
10. "Care for Every Stage of Life" — all ages welcome

**Brand & Other Services (2 stills):**
11. "Feel Better With Intention" — IV therapy
12. "Healthcare Should Feel Human" — brand message

---

### CAROUSEL GRAPHICS (12 total)

[Repeat carousel structure with slide-by-slide direction for each of the 12 carousels]

---

## MOODBOARD REFERENCES

[Insert links to inspirational designs, color palette references, imagery styles]

---

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **Instagram Posts:** 1080 x 1350px (square safe zone)
- **Instagram Stories:** 1080 x 1920px (if used)
- **Color Mode:** RGB
- **Resolution:** 72 dpi
- **Deliverable:** PSD (editable) + PNG (final)

---

## REVISION ROUNDS

- 2 rounds of revisions included
- Approval: Anthony Mendoza + Upbeat Health lead
- Feedback turnaround: 48 hours
```

### Example 2: Ad Creative Brief (Meta/Google Ads)

```markdown
# 🎨 BLUE MOUNTAIN ENERGY – AD CREATIVE BRIEF

**Client:** Blue Mountain Energy
**Designer:** David Canizalez
**Deliverables:** 6 static ad graphics (offering variations)
**Due Date:** June 15, 2026

---

## OBJECTIVE

Create 6 high-converting static image ads for Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads that promote BME's core offers:
- $29 AC Tune-Up
- Buy One, Get One Tune-Up
- Free Estimates
- Free Duct Cleaning with Purchase

---

## VISUAL DIRECTION

### Color Palette
- Brand blue (BME primary)
- Warm accent: orange/gold (trust, warmth)
- Clean whites and light grays (clarity)
- Avoid: Red (feels urgent/pushy; contradicts preservation-first messaging)

### Imagery Style
- Professional + approachable (HVAC tech, happy customers, working systems)
- Show: Real technicians, real homes, real comfort
- Avoid: Stock photos, overly polished, clinical settings

### Typography
- Large, bold offer ($29, FREE, SAVE)
- Supporting copy: clear CTAs
- Readability at mobile size (minimum 16pt for body text)

---

## AD SPECS

**Ad 1: $29 AC Tune-Up**
- Headline: "Beat the Vegas Heat"
- Primary Text: "Professional AC Tune-Up – Only $29"
- Visual: Tech at AC unit + comfortable homeowner
- Dimensions: 1200 x 628px (Facebook), 1080 x 1080px (IG Square), 1080 x 1350px (IG Portrait)

[Repeat for Ads 2–6 with specific offers and visuals]

---

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Resolution:** 72 dpi (web)
- **Color Mode:** RGB
- **Text-to-Image Ratio:** Keep text to <20% of image (Meta requirement)
- **File Size:** < 5MB per file
```

## Graphic Design Best Practices

1. **Show, Don't Tell:** Use actual color swatches, typography samples, and mood imagery alongside written direction
2. **Specificity Matters:** "Clean sans-serif" > "modern font"; "warm lighting" > "professional look"
3. **Audience First:** Always note who will see this and in what context
4. **Avoid Negatives:** Instead of "don't use hospital colors," say "use warm, calming tones like..."
5. **Reference Often:** Provide 3–5 examples of work you love; show what NOT to do
6. **Platform Awareness:** Note dimensions, aspect ratios, and how the design will appear on each platform
7. **Moodboard Magic:** A solid moodboard saves 5 revision rounds
8. **Approve Early:** Get final approval on direction before design starts, not after

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Vague direction:** "Make it pop" > "Use high contrast and bold typography"
- **Forgetting constraints:** Providing dimensions after the designer has started
- **No context:** Not explaining where the graphic will live or who will see it
- **Approval chaos:** Multiple people giving conflicting feedback; establish one approval voice
- **Missing assets:** Designer waiting for photos or logos that should have been provided upfront
- **No revision limit:** Endless rounds without boundaries; cap revisions in the brief
