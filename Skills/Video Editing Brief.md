# Video Editing Brief

## Purpose
A video editing brief gives editors crystal-clear direction on style, pacing, tone, and technical specs before they start cutting. It prevents misalignment between vision and execution, reduces revisions, and ensures final deliverables match the campaign's voice and platform needs.

**Golden Rule:** The editing brief should tell the editor how the video should *feel*, not just what it should contain.

---

## When to Use
- Post-production hand-offs from shoots
- Short-form content (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
- Long-form content (YouTube, testimonials, educational)
- Batch editing projects (multiple pieces with consistent style)
- Client deliverables with specific direction

---

## Video Editing Brief Template

```markdown
# 🎬 VIDEO EDITING BRIEF

**Editor:** [Name or Team]
**Client:** [Client Name]
**Project:** [Project Name]
**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low]
**Due Date:** [Deadline]

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

**What are we editing?**
[Brief description: # of videos, types, total footage hours, etc.]

**Final Deliverables:**
- Video 1: [Title] – [Duration] – [Platform]
- Video 2: [Title] – [Duration] – [Platform]
- [etc.]

**How will these be used?**
[Social media, ads, website, client presentation, etc.]

---

## 2. OVERALL STYLE DIRECTION

**Visual Vibe:**
[Fast-cut and punchy? Slow and cinematic? Raw and documentary-style? Polished corporate?]

**Pacing:**
[Quick jump-cuts? Long burns? Mix of both? Specific beat structure?]

**Color Grade:**
[Warm and inviting? Cool and professional? High contrast? Natural? Vibrant?]
[Reference: "Match the feeling of [previous project link]" or "Like [external brand/creator]"]

**Sound Design:**
[Music style, SFX presence, voiceover treatment, how much raw audio to keep?]

---

## 3. VIDEO-BY-VIDEO BREAKDOWN

### Video 1: [Title]

**Length:** [Seconds or minutes]
**Platform(s):** [TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, YouTube long-form, etc.]
**Format:** [Vertical, horizontal, square]

**Content Summary:**
[What's in this video? Talking heads, B-roll, skits, testimonials, etc.]

**Tone:**
[How should it feel? Funny? Urgent? Educational? Heartfelt? Energetic?]

**Pacing Preference:**
[Fast cuts every 1–2 seconds? Longer holds on key moments? Rhythm-based to music?]

**Color Grade:**
[Specific for this video, or match overall style?]

**Sound:**
- Music: [Genre, energy level, placement in edit]
- Voiceover: [Any VO? Voice color, delivery style?]
- Natural sound: [Keep raw audio from talking heads? How prominent?]
- SFX: [Whooshes, impacts, transitions? How much?]

**Special Effects/Transitions:**
[Crossfades, jump cuts, zoom transitions, text overlays, speed ramps, B-roll style, etc.]

**Graphics/Lower Thirds:**
[Any text, captions, or on-screen graphics needed?]

**CTA/Ending:**
[How does it wrap? Outro card? Text CTA? Link in bio reminder?]

**Key Takes/Moments:**
[Which takes are must-includes? Any footage to avoid or discard?]

**Reference Edit:**
[Link to a previous edit or external video that matches the vibe]

**Do NOT:**
[What style or treatment to avoid for this specific video]

### Video 2: [Title]
[Repeat template above]

[Continue for all videos]

---

## 4. PRIORITY ORDER

Start with these videos in this order (and why):
1. [Video name] — [Reason: highest priority, social media use, client deadline, etc.]
2. [Video name] — [Reason]
3. [Video name] — [Reason]

**Raw Footage Location:**

**How it's organized:**
```

```
## 6. TECHNICAL SPECS

**Output Format:**
- Codec: [H.264, ProRes, etc.]
- Resolution: [1080p, 4K, etc.]
- Frame rate: [24fps, 30fps, 60fps]
- Aspect ratio per video: [16:9, 9:16, 1:1, etc.]

**Delivery:**
- Format: [MP4, MOV, etc.]
- Compression: [Quality tier]
- Where to upload: [Dropbox folder, Google Drive, Slack, etc.]

**Captions:**
- Burned-in captions (yes/no)?
- Closed caption file (.srt, .vtt) needed?
- Caption style: [white text, black outline? position?]

---

## 7. MUSIC / AUDIO

**Music:**
- Provided by: [You provide, editor finds, stock library?]
- Links: [If provided, include links]
- Placement: [Start at 0:00? Enter at beat 1? Outro at :45?]
- Licensing: [Already licensed? Royalty-free needed?]

**Voiceover:**
- Provided: [Yes/No, include file if needed]
- How to mix: [High and dry? Light music under?]

**Sound Effects:**
- Style: [Subtle and cinematic? Punchy and comedic? Meme-style?]
- Examples: [Link to reference for SFX style]

---

## 8. REVISION ROUNDS & TIMELINE

**How many rounds included?**
[1 round? 2 rounds? Unlimited within reason?]

**First delivery date:**
[When do you want to see draft 1?]

**Revision feedback timeline:**
[When will you provide notes? 48 hours? Same day?]

**Final delivery date:**
[Hard deadline for finals]

## 9. APPROVAL WORKFLOW

**Who reviews?**
[Editor, Anthony, Client, etc.]

**Approval path:**
[Internal → Client? Or just internal?]

**What requires client approval?**
[Color grade? Music choice? All revisions?]

---

## 10. QUESTIONS / CONTACT

**Contact:**
[Name, email, Slack handle]

**Response time expected:**
[24 hours? Same day?]

**Availability during revision rounds:**
[When can editor ask for clarification?]

## SIGN-OFF

- [ ] Editor understands direction and has all footage
- [ ] Approval workflow confirmed
- [ ] Technical specs locked
- [ ] Due dates aligned

Date brief finalized: _____
Approved by: _____
```

## Video Editing Best Practices

1. **Show Don't Tell:** Use actual reference edits (linked YouTube videos, previous projects) instead of just describing
2. **Specificity on Pacing:** "Quick cuts every 1–2 seconds" > "Fast-paced"; "Longer holds on emotional moments" > "Slow it down"
3. **Music Matters:** Always specify music style and placement; it drives pacing more than cuts
4. **Footage Clarity:** Organize raw footage clearly; label keepers; note any technical issues upfront
5. **Platform Awareness:** Vertical vs. horizontal changes the edit entirely; specify
6. **Approval Path:** Who has final say? This prevents endless feedback loops
7. **Revision Limits:** Cap revisions; unlimited notes kill productivity
8. **Delivery Format:** Wrong codec/resolution wastes time; lock specs early

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Vague direction:** "Make it more dynamic" > "Use faster cuts and more B-roll transitions"
- **No reference:** Expecting editor to guess style without examples
- **Footage chaos:** Disorganized takes or incomplete shot notes
- **Multiple voices:** Client + internal team + designer all giving different notes; choose one approval voice
- **Wrong aspect ratio:** Editing in 16:9 when it needs 9:16 (time-consuming fix)
- **Music conflicts:** Saying "find something" when you need specific licensing or brand approval
- **Endless revisions:** No cap on rounds; brief should state max rounds included
