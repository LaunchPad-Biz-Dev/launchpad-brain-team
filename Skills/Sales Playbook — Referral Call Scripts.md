# Sales Playbook — Referral Call Scripts

**Purpose:** Scripts Anthony uses for the 5 Quintet client referral calls in Week 1 of the Phase 1 sales build. Based on Hormozi's 7-ways-to-ask-for-referrals framework + BAMFAM close.

**Duration:** 8–12 minutes per call.
**Goal per call:** Ask for 2–3 warm introductions + book a 15-min BAMFAM check-in 2 weeks out.

---

## Master Script Structure

1. **Warm opener** (30s) — reference something specific about their recent work/life (NOT business).
2. **Appreciation** (30s) — name a specific outcome we've delivered. No generic thanks.
3. **The ask frame** (60s) — *"We're opening 5 spots this quarter for businesses like yours. We work best with [specific profile]. Can you think of 2–3 business owners who fit?"*
4. **The incentive** (30s) — *"$500 cash when they sign AND stay 3 months, plus a bottle of [their preference]. Their friend gets 25% off month one + a free Growth Audit."*
5. **The next step** (30s) — *"Want me to send you a short text template you can forward? Or would you rather make the intro three-way?"*
6. **BAMFAM close** (15s) — *"Can I put a 15-min check-in on our calendar two weeks from today? I want to follow up on the referrals and show you what we're doing in [their vertical] this quarter."*
7. **Close warm** — *"Either way, thank you — we wouldn't be where we are without you."*

## Incentive Language (Use Verbatim)

> "Here's what we're doing. You send us a warm introduction — text, email, three-way call, whatever's easiest. If they sign with us AND stay 3 months, I personally send you **$500 cash** and a bottle of [their preference]. For them, we knock **25% off their first month** and run them through a **free Growth Audit** — basically a 30-minute look at what's leaking money in their marketing.
>
> It's a genuine thank-you. I'm not trying to turn you into a salesperson. Just letting you know if you ever want to send someone our way, there's something real in it for you."

## Fine Print to Know (Don't Read Out Loud, But Be Clear If Asked)

- Payout fires on **signed AND retained 3 months** — not just signed. Protects against short-tenure deals.
- Referred business must be **in-market** (real budget, real decision authority, real timeline).
- We need a **warm intro** — not a list of names. Launchpad handles all follow-up after the intro happens.
- Anthony's discretion on whether the referral qualifies.

## What to AVOID Saying
- Don't name specific Launchpad revenue numbers, client fees, or ROI percentages without confirming with Anthony. CCS's 3 years of documented growth = fine to reference qualitatively; don't cite exact figures unless the client has publicly.
- **Do NOT cite [[Wamen Garage Doors]] as a success story** (outcome was not great).
- Don't overpromise close rate ("I'll get you 5 clients easy") — the offer is the incentive, not a projection.
- Don't ask for referrals without first acknowledging what the client has given us.

## BAMFAM Calendar Logic
End every call with the 15-min check-in scheduled 2 weeks out. Purpose:
1. Accountability loop — they're more likely to make the intro if a follow-up is booked
2. Vertical-specific update (shows we're working for them beyond just their account)
3. Natural second ask if first round produced 0 intros
4. Relationship deepener

Put these 5 calls on Anthony's calendar in Week 1 of Phase 1 build (2026-04-21 → 2026-04-28).
