# Scheduling and Calendar Management

## Purpose
Scheduling ensures clients are locked in for quarterly planning meetings and monthly content shoots, eliminating back-and-forth delays. Calendar management keeps the team aligned on timing and prevents double-bookings or missed commitments.

**Core Philosophy:** One month prior, lock everything. No surprises, no chaos.

---

## Scheduling Process Overview

### Timeline
- **One month in advance:** Send scheduling email blast to all clients
- **Two weeks before month:** Reminders for non-responders
- **One week before:** Final confirmation
- **Week of meetings/shoots:** Calendar invites sent, final prep

### Standard Meeting Types

**Quarterly Planning Meetings**
- Frequency: Every 3 months (Jan, April, July, Oct for most clients)
- Duration: 1–1.5 hours
- Format: In-person or virtual (client choice)
- Attendees: Client stakeholder, LaunchPad strategist, optional: client team

**Monthly Review Meetings**
- Frequency: If not on quarterly schedule, monthly checkpoints happen
- Duration: 45 minutes – 1 hour
- Format: Virtual or in-person
- Attendees: Client POC, Launchpad team lead

**Monthly Content Shoots**
- Frequency: One per month (or as contracted)
- Duration: 2–6 hours (depends on scope)
- Format: In-person only
- Attendees: Client, LaunchPad production team

## Scheduling Email Template

### Default Combined Template (Both Meeting + Shoot)

```
**Subject:** Planning Ahead for [MONTH] — Meeting + Content Shoot Scheduling

Hi the [Company Name] team,

As we prepare for **[MONTH]**, we're getting both our **[quarterly/monthly] planning meeting** and **monthly content shoot** scheduled in advance. These are typically held **within the first two weeks of the month** to ensure a strong and organized flow.

To make scheduling easy, please use the link below to book both your **meeting** and **content shoot**:

**[INSERT BOOKING LINK HERE]**

When booking, you'll be prompted to:
- Select a **date and time** within the first two weeks of [MONTH]
- Confirm whether the meeting is **in-person or virtual**
- Provide the **meeting and shoot location(s)** (if applicable)
- Share any **key focus areas or priorities** for [MONTH]'s content

Once your bookings are submitted, we'll review the details and follow up with calendar invites and next steps.

Looking forward to [kicking off the month / planning ahead] strong.

Best,
Emigen Lagman
LaunchPad Business Development
```

### Important Notes

- **Booking links:** Create one link per client (using GoHighLevel or similar) or group link for multiple clients if generic
- **Timing:** Send exactly one month prior to the month being scheduled (e.g., December 1st for January shoots)
- **No back-and-forth:** Booking link eliminates email chains. Clients click, select, done.
- **Signed by:** Emigen Lagman (Admin Assistant) or assigned team member

## Follow-Up for Non-Responders

### First Reminder (1 week after initial email)

```
**Subject:** Quick Follow-Up — [MONTH] Scheduling

Hi the [Company Name] team,

Just following up as we haven't seen a booking come through yet for **[MONTH]**, and we don't currently have your **[meeting type]** and **content shoot** on the calendar.

Since these are typically scheduled **within the first two weeks of the month**, we'd like to get everything locked in as soon as possible.

You can either use the booking link below **or** simply reply to this email with the details:

**Booking link:** [INSERT LINK]

**If replying directly, please include:**

**[Meeting Type] Meeting**
- Preferred **date(s)** within the first two weeks of [MONTH]
- Preferred **time**
- **In-person or virtual**
- If in-person, the **meeting location**

**Monthly Content Shoot**
- Preferred **date(s)** within the first two weeks of [MONTH]
- Preferred **time**
- **Shoot location(s)** and address(es)
- Any **key focus areas or priorities** for [MONTH]'s content

Once we have this information, we'll get everything confirmed and send over calendar invites and next steps.

Thanks, and let us know if you have any questions.

Best,
Emigen Lagman
LaunchPad Business Development
```

### Second Reminder (3–4 days before deadline)

```
**Subject:** URGENT — [MONTH] Scheduling Deadline Approaching

Hi the [Company Name] team,

We still haven't received your booking for **[MONTH]'s content shoot and meeting**. We want to make sure we get you locked in before our production calendar fills up.

Please respond ASAP (ideally by EOD [DATE]) so we can finalize your schedule.

[Same booking link + reply-with-details option]

Thanks,
Emigen
```

## Managing Schedule Conflicts & Changes

### If Client Requests Date Change After Initial Lock

```
Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for reaching out. We currently have your **[meeting/shoot]** locked for **[DATE]** at **[TIME]**.

We can explore moving to **[ALTERNATIVE DATE]**, but it would require us to adjust our production schedule and potentially impact other clients.

Can we jump on a quick call to discuss? Here are a few alternative dates we have available:
- [Option 1]
- [Option 2]
- [Option 3]

Let me know what works best.

Best,
[Team Member]
```

### If Internal Conflict (LaunchPad Team Unavailable)

1. **Notify client immediately** (don't wait)
2. **Offer alternatives** (usually 3 options)
3. **Escalate to leadership** if needed
4. **Document the change** in calendar and client notes

## Calendar Management Tools & Systems

### What Launchpad Uses

**For client scheduling:** GoHighLevel booking calendar (link sent to clients)
**For team management:** Google Calendar (shared calendars by role/project)
**For tracking:** Notion database with status tracking

### Calendar Best Practices

1. **Block time immediately** when client confirms (don't wait for final calendar invite)
2. **Buffer time:** Add 30 min before/after shoots for setup and breakdown
3. **Travel time:** Account for travel between locations
4. **Backup teams:** Identify backup person for every commitment
5. **Notes:** Add details directly in calendar (location, contact person, special requirements)

### Google Calendar Event Template

```
TITLE: [CLIENT NAME] – [TYPE: Quarterly Meeting / Content Shoot]

DESCRIPTION:
—Client Contact: [Name, phone, email]
—Location: [Full address]
—Team Lead: [Name]
—Other attendees: [Names]
—Parking/Access Notes: [Details]
—Special Requirements: [Anything unusual]
—Deliverables: [What we're producing]
—Backup Contact: [If lead is unavailable]

REMINDERS:
—1 week before: Email client to confirm
—2 days before: Final production meeting (internal)
—Day before: Last-minute check-in
—1 hour before: Arrive early for setup
```

## Monthly Cadence Template

Use this template to establish predictable patterns for clients:

```
LAUNCHPAD STANDARD MONTHLY CADENCE

WEEK 1 (First two weeks):
- **Tuesday–Friday:** Quarterly meetings or monthly review meetings
- **Tuesday–Saturday:** Content shoots (if scheduled)
- Goal: Plan and capture early in month

WEEK 2:
- Client meetings & shoots wrap
- Internal team reviews footage/notes

WEEK 3:
- Post-production begins (editing, design)
- Client receives early drafts if applicable

WEEK 4:
- Final content delivery
- Schedule next month's meetings/shoots
- Monthly review completed

This creates predictable rhythm for both LaunchPad and clients.
```

## Seasonal Adjustments

### Holiday Scheduling

**November (prepare for Dec/Jan)**
- Schedule December meetings + shoots by early November
- Note: Clients may be less available Thanksgiving week

**December (prepare for Jan/Feb)**
- Heavy booking period (everyone planning for new year)
- Offer extra time slots to accommodate demand
- Note: Jan 1–2 may be unavailable; adjust scheduling

**Summer (June–August)**
- Clients may be traveling; offer flexibility
- But also demand spikes (summer campaigns)
- Book early; vacations change plans

## Communication Checklist

Use this to ensure nothing falls through cracks:

```
[ ] Initial scheduling email sent (month in advance)
[ ] Client booking received and processed
[ ] Booking confirmed in internal calendar
[ ] Location verified and confirmed with client
[ ] Team lead assigned
[ ] Production briefing created
[ ] Team briefed on specifics
[ ] 1-week reminder email sent to client
[ ] Internal 2-day-before meeting held
[ ] Final confirmation with client (day before)
[ ] All team members confirmed attendance (day of)
[ ] Production day completed
[ ] Footage backed up
[ ] Next month's scheduling begins
```
