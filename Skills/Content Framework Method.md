# Content Framework Method

**This is how Launchpad builds content for every client. Final as of 2026-10-07.** The vocabulary below is the vocabulary; the team and clients use it the same way. Full client-facing template: Claude Docs "Launchpad Content Strategy — Master Template." Client applications live in Intelligence/ until approved, then Projects/.

Sits under [[Core Framework]] (Content → Leads → Systems) as the Content layer. [[Content Strategy Framework]] is the companion note for running and measuring a strategy; this note is what the system is.

---

## Vocabulary

Eight terms. If a word isn't on this list, it isn't part of the system.

| Term | Definition | Test | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pillar** | What we talk about. A product, service, audience or theme tied to a business goal. Long-standing: 6–24 months. Three to five per client. | "Will this still be true next year?" | First Colony: Direct to Consumer · LO Recruiting · Culture and Community. A Mi Manera: The Craft · The Culture · The People · The Origin · Bookings |
| **Bucket** | What the content does in the buyer's journey. Fixed across every client, **never renamed**: Awareness · Desire · Story · Choice. | "Where in the journey does this land?" | Same four for every client |
| **Job** | The intent of one piece. One of six: Educate · Entertain · Inspire · Empathize · Prove · Convert. | "What should the viewer feel or do?" | First Colony Desire content is mostly job Educate; A Mi Manera Desire content is mostly Entertain / Inspire |
| **Series** | A named, repeatable piece living in one pillar × bucket cell. The unit briefs plan with. | "Can we shoot it again next month without a meeting?" | The Anti-Rush (Craft × Desire); Meet the LO (Recruiting × Story) |
| **Media** | One of three: Video · Photo · Graphic. Deliverables are counted by media. Photo feeds graphic. | "Which team makes it?" | |
| **Format** | The production shape inside a media type. Video: reel, talking head, skit. Photo: hero, portrait, lifestyle. Graphic: carousel, functional, static ad. See [[Content Types and Formats]]. | "What does the editor or designer open?" | |
| **Theme** | A time-bound campaign or seasonal angle layered over the pillars. Monthly or quarterly. Has an end date. | "When does it end?" | November launch; Día de los Muertos; Block Party |
| **Framework** | The whole system: pillars × buckets with jobs, series and formats inside. Never just the four buckets. | | |

**Not vocabulary:** "stage" (say bucket), "content type" (say series or format), "segment" (a shoot-day grouping in a production brief only).

### The six jobs

| Job | The piece exists to… | Lives mostly in |
|---|---|---|
| Educate | explain, clarify, answer the question they already have | Desire, Choice |
| Entertain | stop the scroll, make them stay or laugh | Awareness, Desire |
| Inspire | show what's possible, what it could feel like | Awareness, Desire, Story |
| Empathize | show we understand their situation or fear | Story |
| Prove | show evidence: testimonial, result, the process itself | Story, Choice |
| Convert | ask for the next step | Choice |

### The brief tag

Every line item in any brief: **Pillar · Bucket · Job · Series (Media: Format)**

> Direct to Consumer · Desire · Educate · Top 3 signs a reverse mortgage could make sense (carousel)
> The Craft · Desire · Entertain · The Anti-Rush: Café de Olla (reel)
> LO Recruiting · Story · Prove · LO Voices: what changed when I joined (talking head)
> The People · Story · Empathize · Window Portraits: the Sunday tía (photo: portrait)
> Bookings · Choice · Convert · Find Us (graphic: functional)

**What we got wrong before 2026-10-07:** we called pillars "buckets" (Notion: "Bucket 1: Direct to Consumer"), called the four buckets "the framework" or "stages," and renamed Desire to Educate for service clients. All three stop. Buckets keep their names; the job carries the intent.

## The framework

Adapted from the Taco Bell playbook: their four categories are our four buckets. We add the pillar layer so the same four buckets work for a lender and a coffee trailer.

### The four buckets

| Bucket | Purpose | Default jobs | Qualities (example) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Make the brand and its hero product or people recognizable | Entertain, Inspire | Bold, Iconic, Distinctive, Consistent |
| **Desire** | Create want. Food: crave. Mortgage: confidence. Services: proof. Bucket stays Desire; the job changes. | Educate, Inspire, Entertain | Fresh, Tempting (food) / Clear, Reassuring (service) |
| **Story** | Build the world around the product: people, craft, culture, community | Empathize, Prove, Inspire | Real, Vibrant, First-person |
| **Choice** | Enable the decision: menu, comparison, hours, booking, reaching a human | Convert, Educate, Prove | Direct, Honest, Functional, Repeatable |

### Five slots per bucket (per client)
1. **Purpose** — one sentence on what this bucket does for this client
2. **Qualities** — 4–5 adjectives as the filter; opposite buckets get opposite adjectives
3. **Jobs** — which of the six this bucket carries for this client
4. **Placement** — exactly which channels, and by omission where not (Arby's Food Truck: no skits or trends on the Facebook page)
5. **Reference** — a visual example of each series once it exists

### The grid
Pillars down the side, buckets across the top. Every cell is a place content can live; not every cell needs filling. Series live in cells (2–4 per pillar). Share of volume is set by pillar; bucket mix is checked against the funnel (every bucket fed, none over half the month).

### Guardrails
Compliance (NMLS, RESPA, no rates) or authenticity (Chicano pride, not costume). A checklist in the approval step.

### Recurring messages
Three to five lines the brand repeats until people can finish the sentence ("Luxury belongs to everyone." "Just looking is fine."). Listed in the strategy's voice section, pulled into briefs as hooks, captions, end cards and ad copy.

### It starts with an insight
Taco Bell: "All Culture, Little Crave." First Colony: "information, not trust." A Mi Manera: "identity without a system." If the gap can't be named in one sentence, discovery isn't done.

## Media types: video, photo, graphic

Three media types. Every client strategy says which formats it uses in each; every brief counts deliverables in each.

- **Video** carries most of the reach; where Desire and Story get fed.
- **Photo** is content on its own (hero, portraits, lifestyle) and the raw material for graphics. Every shoot day captures a photo list for the graphics team.
- **Graphic** carries Choice (menus, comparisons, offers, how-to-book), ads, and anything seasonal. Fastest way to fill a calendar; its photo needs are planned a month ahead.

### Video formats

|---|---|---|---|
| Hero product / object | Awareness | Inspire | The taco; the A Mi Manera trailer |
| Process / B-roll | Desire, Story | Prove, Inspire | BME tech on an AC unit; the Anti-Rush |
| Detail / macro | Desire | Inspire | Honeycomb, crema |
| Talking head | Desire, Story | Educate, Empathize | Ben Nadig myth-busting; First Colony explainers |
| Testimonial / UGC | Story, Choice | Prove | BME customer; client stories (no figures) |

| Story (24h) | Desire, Choice | Entertain, Convert | Flavor Drops; Block Party countdown |
| Video ad | All | Any | Any of the above cut for paid |

### Photo list on every shoot day
Graphics hands production a photo list for next month's graphics (close-ups in one light, portraits, environment, textures, seasonal set). It's its own segment in the production brief. Photos are filed by pillar and photo format.

### Mood boards
One canonical mood board per client (Pinterest, or Canva where the team prefers), linked in section 7 of the strategy and in the client's brain note. Sections: one per pillar, one per media (video look, photo look, graphic look), plus seasonal. It is the visual reference for the bucket qualities at review. The 2026-04-04 client audit found mood boards missing for most clients; this is where that gets fixed.

### Seasonal and themed graphics
Themes are planned a quarter out so photo needs land on a shoot day in time. A seasonal set = theme, pillar, dates, photo set needed, formats (single-image, Story, countdown, recap), recurring message carried. Holidays that matter to the brand get a set; the rest don't.

## The Client Content Strategy: ten sections

Client-facing. Under ten pages. Client's language.

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One page up front | Brand in a sentence, goal, audience, the pillars named |
| 2 | The insight | The gap, named, one paragraph |
| 3 | Audiences | Up to three: who, what they need to believe, what gets in the way |
| 4 | Voice, positioning, taglines | How it sounds, what it never says, approved/banned language, 3–5 recurring messages |
| 5 | Pillars | 3–5, each with share of volume and the goal it serves |
| 6 | Buckets | The four with five slots filled; the pillar × bucket grid with series in cells |
| 7 | Media, formats and mood board | Video, photo and graphic formats for this client; photo library plan; seasonal sets; link to the canonical mood board |
| 8 | Channels and cadence | Where, how often, posting mix by pillar and by media; matches contract |
| 9 | Guardrails | Checklist; copied into every brief |
| 10 | Measurement | Reported monthly by bucket; what would change the pillar mix |
| — | Appendix: idea bank | 30–40 ideas by pillar, each tagged |

Writing rules: first sentence of every section is the point; specifics over adjectives; the client's terminology; tables for anything compared; no dashes in anything that becomes a caption; under ten pages.

## Briefs

The ten brief types in [[Brief Creation]] don't change. Every line item carries the four-part tag. Production briefs group the shoot by pillar (segments), each series listed with bucket and job.

**Monthly Content Strategy Brief, in order:** goal + theme · pillar mix vs baseline · video list (tagged) · photo list (tagged; content vs for-graphics) · graphic list (tagged; seasonal set if any) · ad angles (tagged, bucket visible) · series continuing / new / resurfaced · recurring messages in play · photo needs for next month's graphics (a shoot segment) · approval checklist · deliverable count by media vs [[Contracted Monthly Deliverables]].

Check on a finished brief: every pillar appears, every bucket appears at least once, no bucket over half the month.

## Process

**Building (once, then quarterly)**
1. Discovery: everything the client has, plus founder and approver interviews
2. Insight: the gap in one sentence
3. Pillars: 3–5, confirmed with the client first
4. Buckets: five slots filled; the grid; guardrails; recurring messages in the client's words
5. Idea bank: 30–40 tagged ideas; flag the ones needing buy-in
6. Client review: sections 1, 2, 4, 5, 9 live; client edits guardrails and messages
7. Lock and file: brain (Intelligence/ → Projects/), client Drive, pillar list + grid into their Notion page

**Running (Notion CYCLE EXPECTATIONS, June 2026; see [[Weekly and Monthly Cycles]])**

| Cycle week | What the strategy adds |
|---|---|
| Week 3: Preproduction | Team brainstorm → Preproduction intake → Apollo drafts the five Monthly Briefs (tagged) → Creative Director approves by end of week |
| Week 4: Production | Shot lists grouped by pillar (segments); series set the cut, jobs set the tone |
| Week 1: Production + Post | Guardrails checklist is the review gate; bucket qualities are the review question; all production complete |
| Week 2: Post + Scheduling | Posting per [[SMM Posting Calendar Patterns]]; mix checked vs pillar split and bucket check; brainstorm for next cycle |

**Templates and routing**

| Thing | Template | Where it lives in Notion | Apollo skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client Content Strategy | `Resources/Templates/Content Strategy — Master Template.md` (+ PDF) | Client Portals → {Client} → Resources → Content Overview → **Content Strategy** (portal template already carries the structure) | `content-strategy-builder` |

The **Client** relation routes to the portal. | `shoot-brief-builder` (manual fire: team assigns the Preproduction task to Apollo after the brainstorm) |

**Quarterly:** move the pillar mix, retire or add series, refresh the theme. A pillar that changes every quarter was a theme.
