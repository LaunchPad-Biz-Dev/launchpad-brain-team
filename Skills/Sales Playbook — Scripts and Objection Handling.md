# Sales Playbook — Scripts, FAQs & Objection Handling

*Source: LPD Sales Manual (Google Drive: Sales Team / Sales Resources / 1.2 LPD Sales Manual)*

This note provides AI sales agents and the sales team with ready-to-use conversation frameworks, FAQ responses, objection handling strategies, and email templates.

---

## Sales Philosophy

**Sales as a Service** — Sales at LaunchPad is about providing genuine solutions, not just closing deals. The approach is to understand the client's pain points and offer solutions that actually fit.

**Core Principles:**
- **Active Listening** — Understand the prospect's challenges and vision before recommending anything
- **Honesty in Proposition** — Present services truthfully, no unnecessary embellishments
- **Education-Based Selling** — Let the value speak; no high-pressure tactics
- **Leverage Success** — Use case studies and testimonials to build trust and demonstrate expertise

---

## The Two-Meeting Sales Process

LaunchPad uses a **two-meeting system** that differentiates it from other agencies:

### Meeting 1: Discovery Call
- **Purpose:** Deep understanding of the prospect's business, challenges, and goals
- **Approach:** Listen more than talk — ask about their current marketing, what's working, what's not, their growth goals, timeline, and budget
- **Key Questions to Ask:**
  - What does your current marketing look like?
  - What's working and what isn't?
  - What are your growth goals for the next 6-12 months?
  - Have you worked with a marketing agency before? What was that experience like?
  - What's your timeline for getting started?
  - Who else is involved in this decision?

### Meeting 2: Tailored Presentation
- **Purpose:** Present a customized solution based on what was learned in Meeting 1
- **Approach:** Show the prospect you listened — reference their specific pain points, recommend a package that fits, walk through deliverables and timeline
- **Key Actions:**
  - Present the recommended package with clear deliverables
  - Show relevant portfolio examples from their industry
  - Walk through the onboarding timeline so they know what to expect
  - Address any questions or concerns
  - If ready, send the PandaDoc proposal for signing

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

These are the most common questions prospects ask, with recommended responses:

### About LaunchPad
**"How are you different from other marketing agencies?"**
We provide a personalized approach with a two-meeting system — the first meeting to deeply understand your needs, the second to present a tailored solution. We're not a cookie-cutter agency. Plus, we're full-service under one roof: ads, content, video, social, CRM.

**"I've never heard of LaunchPad before. How do I know you're credible?"**
We're happy to share case studies, testimonials, and references from satisfied clients. We've helped businesses across multiple industries — law firms, home services, franchises, restaurants — grow their revenue through strategic marketing.

### About Packages & Pricing
**"How flexible are your packages? Can they be customized?"**
Our standard packages (Essential Growth, Comprehensive Growth, Ultimate Business Builder) cover most needs, but we absolutely customize based on your specific goals. We also offer Ads Only, Creative Only, and A La Carte options.

**"What's included in each package?"**
See [[Packages and Pricing]] for the full breakdown. In short: Essential ($2,500/mo) covers FB/IG ads, 4 videos, 8 graphics, social media, and CRM. Each tier up adds more platforms, more content, and more strategic depth.

**"Do you require long-term contracts?"**
We aim to build long-term relationships, but we understand the need for flexibility. Contract terms can be discussed — we also offer a Pilot Program for prospects who want to test us first with a smaller engagement.

### About Results
**"What kind of results can I expect?"**
Results vary by industry, budget, and starting point, but we track everything — ad performance, lead generation, engagement, conversions — and optimize monthly. We never promise specific numbers, but we do promise transparency and continuous improvement.

**"How soon will I see results?"**
Typically 60-90 days for meaningful traction, depending on the services. Ads can show results within the first month. Content and social media build over time. We set realistic expectations during onboarding.

## Objection Handling

For each objection, the pattern is: **acknowledge → reframe → respond with value**.

### Price Objections

**"It's too expensive / We can't afford it"**
- **Strategy:** Break down cost per deliverable to show value
- **Response:** We understand budget is a concern. When you break down what's included — videos, graphics, ad management, social media, CRM — the per-item cost is well below market rate. We also have Ads Only and Creative Only options if you want to start smaller and scale up.

**"We're just a small business; I don't think we can afford your services"**
- **Strategy:** Highlight scalability and growth potential
- **Response:** We work with businesses at every stage, which is why we offer scalable packages. Essential Growth at $2,500/mo is designed for growing businesses — and the ROI from professional marketing typically pays for itself.

### Trust / Credibility Objections

**"I've never heard of LaunchPad before"**
- **Strategy:** Provide evidence of past success and credibility
- **Response:** We'd be happy to share case studies, testimonials, and references from our satisfied clients across multiple industries.

**"We already have someone doing our marketing"**
- **Strategy:** Differentiate by showing the full-service advantage
- **Response:** That's great — are they handling ads, video production, CRM, social media, and reputation management too? Most businesses use 3-5 different vendors. We consolidate everything under one roof, which means better coordination and lower total cost.

### Commitment Objections

**"The contract terms seem too long. What if things don't work out?"**
- **Strategy:** Address flexibility and build trust
- **Response:** We aim to build long-term relationships, but we understand the need for flexibility. We can discuss terms that work for both sides. We also offer a Pilot Program so you can experience our work before committing to a full package.

**"We need to think about it / talk to my partner"**
- **Strategy:** Respect the process, set a follow-up
- **Response:** Absolutely, take the time you need. I'll send over a summary of what we discussed so you have everything in front of you. Can I follow up with you on [specific day]?

**"Can we try before committing?"**
- **Strategy:** Offer the Pilot Program
- **Response:** That's exactly what our Pilot Program is for — a smaller engagement so you can see our quality and process firsthand before committing to a full package.

## Sales Email Templates

### 1. Initial Outreach Email
**Subject:** Elevate Your Business with LaunchPad
**Purpose:** First contact with a new prospect
**Key elements:** Personal touch, reference their business, brief value prop, CTA to schedule call

### 2. Follow-Up Email
**Subject:** Following Up — LaunchPad Business Development
**Purpose:** Re-engaging a prospect who hasn't responded
**Key elements:** Reference previous message, make it easy to respond, offer flexibility on timing

### 3. Discovery Call Confirmation
**Subject:** Confirming Our Discovery Call
**Purpose:** Confirmation and details of the scheduled discovery call
**Key elements:** Time/date confirmation, what to expect, light prep work, easy reschedule option

### 4. Post Discovery Call Thank You
**Subject:** Thank You and Next Steps
**Purpose:** Thanking the client and providing a brief overview of next steps
**Key elements:** Recap of conversation, what you learned, timeline for proposal, enthusiasm

### 5. Proposal Follow-Up
**Subject:** Your Custom Marketing Proposal [Client Name]
**Purpose:** Following up after sending the PandaDoc proposal
**Key elements:** Confirm receipt, highlight key sections, offer to answer questions, schedule follow-up call

**Email structure for post-proposal follow-up:**
1. Personalized greeting with client name
2. "Your proposal is attached" — make it clear
3. Summary of what's included (brief, 2-3 bullets)
4. "Looking forward to talking through this together"
5. Specific follow-up call time (e.g., "tomorrow at 9:00 AM")
6. "Send over any questions you have"
7. Warm closing with signature

### 6. Proposal Follow-Up with Bonus/Discount Offer
**Purpose:** Adding value or incentives during proposal review
**Key elements:** Explain the additional items being offered, reinforce value, mention affiliate partnerships or discounts being passed along, reiterate excitement about working together

Example language:
"Attached, you'll also find invoices for [design work] which will come with revisions, and [additional service] normally at $X but we'd love to offer you [discount]% with [partnership] discount. The design is complimentary if you order through [partner platform]."

## Custom Package Sales Strategy

For startup, multi-location, or high-value prospects, don't default to standard packages. Build custom proposals that address specific needs.

### When to Propose Custom Packages
- New startup with branding needs ($3,000+ onboarding)
- Business wanting to expand into new markets/locations
- Franchise or scalable business model
- High ad spend potential ($5,000+/month)
- Need for specialized integrations (call centers, voicemail drops, etc.)
- Partnership opportunities requiring custom pricing

### Custom Package Components
1. **Onboarding fee** — Upfront cost for brand buildout ($3,000-$5,000)
   - Covers: logos, brand kit, taglines, brand strategy, content strategy, mockups, graphics, full buildout

2. **Service fee** — Monthly recurring (based on content + ads + CRM)
   - Can be a custom tier (e.g., 16 videos + 24 graphics + ads)
   - Or combination of standard package + add-ons

3. **Ad spend minimum** — Separate line item ($500-$5,000+/month)
   - Client decides spend amount
   - Launchpad optimizes across platforms

4. **Add-ons & specialized services**
   - Call center integration + voicemail drops
   - Talent hiring/management (client pays talent; Launchpad manages)
   - Multi-location coordination
   - Quarterly shoots for additional locations
   - Data/lead buying budget
   - CRM customization beyond standard

5. **Strategic discounts**
   - Multi-quarter commits ("lock in rate for 6 months")
   - Partnership passes (LBEM affiliate, etc.)
   - Referral discounts

### Custom Package Proposal Structure
Position it clearly so client understands investment:
1. **Onboarding Phase** (Months 1-3)
   - Deliverable: Full brand identity + content strategy
   - Fee: $X (one-time)
   - Timeline: 30-60 days to completion

2. **Ongoing Service** (Months 1+)
   - Deliverable: X videos, X graphics, X posts, X shoots per month
   - Fee: $X/month (recurring)
   - Includes: Ad management, CRM, social media, reporting

3. **Paid Advertising** (Ongoing)
   - Budget: $X/month (client's ad spend)
   - Launchpad manages setup, optimization, reporting
   - Platforms: [specific platforms based on goals]

4. **Add-Ons** (Optional)
   - Call center integration: $X/month
   - Talent hiring: [cost passed through]
   - Quarterly shoots in additional locations: $X/shoot

### Startup Special Strategy
For brand-new businesses:
- Position as "3-Month Sprint" package
- $3,000+ onboarding fee (worth the full brand buildout)
- Premium service tier (e.g., Ultimate level content)
- Higher minimum ad spend ($5,000-$10,000/month)
- Emphasize "flood social media" strategy
- Include Gen Z or trendy positioning if relevant
- Offer Gen-Z-specific content guidance
