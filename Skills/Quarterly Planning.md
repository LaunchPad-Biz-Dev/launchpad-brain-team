# Quarterly Planning

*How to create 90-day strategic roadmaps and monthly execution plans for clients*

---

## Overview

Quarterly Planning is the foundation of Launchpad's service delivery. Instead of billing monthly without strategy, we create a **90-day roadmap** that shows clients exactly what will be delivered, why, and how it connects to their business goals.

**Two-Stage Process:**
1. **Stage 1: Quarterly Plan (QP)** — Strategic, client-facing, 90-day roadmap
2. **Stage 2: Monthly Execution Plans (MEPs)** — Tactical, team-facing, monthly breakdown

---

## Why Quarterly Planning Matters

### Problem Without It
- Clients see monthly deliverables but don't understand direction
- No strategic flow (January's content doesn't build on December)
- Missed seasonal opportunities
- Hard to adjust mid-quarter without surprises

### Solution With It
- Clients see full 90-day strategy upfront
- Clear themes, offers, and promotions across all three months
- Holiday tie-ins and CRM campaigns aligned with content
- Flexibility to adjust within the plan if needed

---

## Stage 1: Quarterly Plan (QP)

**Purpose:** Create a 90-day strategic roadmap that aligns client and Launchpad on offers, services, campaigns, and deliverables.

**Audience:** Client-facing (sent to decision-maker and stakeholders)

### QP Components

#### 1. Client Overview
- **Client name** and context
- **Package level** (Essential, Comprehensive, Ultimate, Custom)
- **Monthly deliverables** (X videos, X graphics, X posts, etc.)
- **Quarter dates** (e.g., Q1 2026 = Jan 1 – Mar 31)
- **Main objectives** (lead generation, conversions, brand awareness, retention, etc.)

#### 2. Review & Insights
**Wins from past work:**
- What content themes resonated?
- Which platforms drove the best engagement or leads?
- Any successful campaigns or offers to replicate?
- Client feedback on deliverable quality or process?

**Challenges to overcome:**
- What underperformed?
- Where are there gaps (e.g., fragmented messaging, seasonal dips)?
- Budget or resource constraints?
- Market or seasonal dynamics?

#### 3. Quarterly Focus Areas
**Services to emphasize:**
- Primary service (FX, DMV, AC repair, legal services, etc.)
- Secondary service (support offers, upsells)
- Any new services or product launches?

**Monthly campaigns:**
- October: What offers, themes, or pain points are we tackling?
- November: Seasonal angle (holidays, Black Friday, etc.)?
- December: Year-end wrap-up, gift-giving, resolution themes?

**Holiday tie-ins:**
- Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, etc.
- How do they connect to the client's business?

**CRM focus:**
- Lead gen campaigns (capturing new prospects)
- Nurture sequences (warming up engaged leads)
- Review/referral campaigns (converting customers to advocates)
- Greetings/retention (keeping existing customers engaged)

#### 4. Narrative Arc (Monthly Themes)
Create a progression across the three months:
- Month 1: Hook (address a pain or opportunity)
- Month 2: Build (educate, show proof, build trust)
- Month 3: Convert (final push, urgency, call to action)

**Example (Solar Sales, Q4 2025):**
- October: "Don't wait until the airport" (FX + travel prep)
- November: "Holiday travel + Thanksgiving is expensive" (FX, gifts for family)
- December: "New year, new savings" (Year-end decision-making, resolutions)

#### 5. Content Buckets & Themes
Define how content will be distributed across themes:
- Educational/How-to
- Service/Offer Promos
- Testimonials/Social Proof
- Behind-the-Scenes/Culture
- Seasonal/Promotional

**Example:** For Ultimate package (16 videos/month):
- 6 educational
- 4 service promos
- 3 testimonials/case studies
- 2 behind-the-scenes
- 1 seasonal hero

#### 6. Deliverables Summary
**Hard totals, not ranges:**
- Videos: X per month × 3 = X for quarter
- Graphics: X per month × 3 = X for quarter
- Posts: X per month × 3 = X for quarter
- Shoots: X per quarter
- CRM campaigns: List specific campaigns and sequences
- Ad spend recommendations: Budget by platform

**Example (Ultimate Package, Q4 2025 CCS):**
- Videos: 16/month × 3 = 48 total
- Graphics: 24/month × 3 = 72 total
- Organic posts: TBD per platform
- Shoots: 2 (Vegas in October, LA in December)
- CRM: 4 lead gen campaigns, 2 nurture sequences, ongoing review prompts
- Ads: $3,500-4,500/month Meta primary, minimal Google keywords

#### 7. Success Metrics
How will we measure success?
- **Paid:** Cost per lead, ROAS, ad spend efficiency
- **Organic:** Engagement rate, reach, follower growth
- **CRM:** Opt-in rate, email open rate, conversion rate
- **Overall:** Revenue impact, customer acquisition cost

## Stage 2: Monthly Execution Plans (MEPs)

**Purpose:** Turn the Quarterly Plan into a tactical breakdown for the team to execute.

**Audience:** Team-facing (creative, editors, ad manager, social media manager)

**Created After:** QP is approved by client

### MEP Components

#### 1. Executive Summary
- **Month focus** and connection to quarterly plan
- **Primary goal** (e.g., "Drive FX volume for holiday travel")
- **Secondary goal** (e.g., "Build R-Check enrollments")
- **Themes for the month** (e.g., "Don't get tricked + early pay emphasis")

#### 2. Sales Objectives (for Media Buyer)
- **Primary objective:** What are we optimizing for? (leads, conversions, awareness)
- **Secondary objective:** Any supporting metrics?
- **Ad mix:** Platform breakdown (e.g., 60% Meta, 30% Google, 10% testing)
- **Budget allocation:** How much per platform?

#### 3. Deliverables Breakdown
**Videos (with brief descriptions):**
1. Don't get tricked by airport rates (FX educational)
2. Leftover cash buyback (FX service promo)
3. ... (continue for all videos)

**Graphics (by type):**
- 8 service/offer promos
- 6 educational/FAQ carousels
- 6 reviews/before-after
- 4 culture/BTS

**Organic posts:** List by platform and week

**CRM campaigns:** Specific campaigns launching this month, what they're targeting, copy templates

#### 4. Weekly Cycle
- **Week 1:** Shoot day (if applicable), pre-production, scriptwriting
- **Week 2:** Edit sprints, first-round reviews
- **Week 3:** Revisions, approvals, ad creative finalization
- **Week 4:** Posting schedule, ad launch, optimization begins

#### 5. Team Handoffs
Clear ownership:
- **Videography/Photography:** Anthony or Kyle (shoot day)
- **Editing:** Munzelle, Ral, or freelancers
- **Graphic Design:** Arija, David, or in-house
- **Ad Management:** Amber (or Anthony if split)
- **Social Media:** Cynthia (or Amber if multi-tasking)
- **CRM:** Amber or systems team

#### 6. Creative Direction Notes
- Tone, style, or messaging unique to this month
- Any client-specific requirements
- Visual style or brand guidelines to refresh

## Master Prompt for QP/MEP Creation

Launchpad uses AI (Claude, ChatGPT) to collaboratively build QPs and MEPs. The master prompt includes:

### Core Rules
1. Research client context (past work, brand, deliverables, goals)
2. Make it collaborative (ask questions, test assumptions, involve client)
3. Separate into two stages (QP first, MEP second after approval)
4. Be detailed and actionable (tie to sales: Better Content → Better Leads → Better Systems)
5. Use package levels to calculate deliverables (multiply monthly × 3 for quarter)
6. Format for audience (client-facing vs. team-facing)
7. Always include holiday tie-ins and CRM campaigns
8. Align content buckets with Launchpad's 3 Es (Entertain, Educate, Empathize) + brand/service promos

### Process
- Stage 1: Draft QP with client input → finalize together
- Stage 2: Build MEP for first month only after QP approved → remaining months follow same structure
- Always ask: "What other wins/challenges should we add?"

## Common Templates & Examples

### Narrative Arc Structures
**Problem → Solution → Results:**
- Month 1: Identify problem (e.g., "Airport exchange rates are hidden")
- Month 2: Show solution (e.g., "We offer 80 currencies, fair rates")
- Month 3: Create urgency (e.g., "Book your consultation before travel")

**Education → Proof → Conversion:**
- Month 1: Teach (e.g., "How to choose solar panels")
- Month 2: Show proof (e.g., "Customer savings case studies")
- Month 3: Close (e.g., "Schedule your free solar assessment")

### CRM Campaign Templates
**Lead Gen:**
- Offer: Free guide, consultation, audit, assessment
- Landing page: Captures email/phone
- Follow-up: Automated emails → sales sequence

**Nurture:**
- Educational content sent weekly
- Addresses objections
- Re-engages after 30 days of no action

**Review/Referral:**
- Request for reviews (text, email, SMS)
- Referral incentives
- Social proof content
