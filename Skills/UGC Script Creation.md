# UGC Script Creation

## Purpose
UGC (User-Generated Content) scripts guide creators in producing authentic, on-brand videos for advertising and social platforms. Unlike traditional commercials, UGC feels raw, relatable, and non-polished—while still hitting campaign goals and brand messaging.

**Philosophy:** UGC is the intersection of entertainment + education + CTA.

---

## UGC Discovery Questions

Before writing scripts, always clarify these fundamentals:

### 1. **Client/Brand Overview**
- Who is the client? (name, industry, core product/service)
- What is their unique selling point?
- What should people know about this brand?

### 2. **Campaign Goals**
- Primary objective (drive sales, awareness, signups, engagement, etc.)
- KPIs being measured (CTR, conversion rate, engagement)
- What does success look like?

### 3. **Target Audience**
- Age range, gender, interests, pain points
- Mindset when they see this (casually scrolling vs. ready to buy)
- What problems do they have that this product solves?

### 4. **Platform & Format**
- Which platform(s)? (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, etc.)
- Length requirements? (15s, 30s, 60s)
- Vertical (9:16) or horizontal? (16:9)

### 5. **Use Case / Context**
- Where will viewers discover this? (in-feed, ads, hashtag challenge, etc.)
- What action should they take immediately? (click link, follow, use code, comment)

### 6. **Brand Voice & Guidelines**
- Tone? (authentic/raw, polished, humorous, heartfelt, aspirational, etc.)
- Mandatory mentions? (brand name, tagline, features, disclaimer)
- Anything to avoid?

### 7. **Assets & Inspiration**
- Previous UGC examples or competitor videos that work
- Visual assets the creator should feature (product, branding, etc.)
- Any props, locations, or specific scenarios?

## UGC Script Template

Use this structure for all UGC scripts:

```
## UGC SCRIPT: [Title]

**Platform:** [TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts / etc.]
**Length:** [15s / 30s / 45s]
**Format:** [Vertical 9:16 / Horizontal 16:9]
**Tone:** [Authentic, Humorous, Heartfelt, Educational, Urgent, etc.]

---

### On-Screen Directions
1. **Setting:** [Where is creator? What's visible? Lighting?]
2. **Props/Wardrobe:** [What does creator have/wear?]
3. **Visual Style:** [Professional? Raw? Casual? Branded?]
4. **Camera Movement:** [Static, gimbal, handheld, POV?]

---

### Script (Timestamped)

**[0–3s] HOOK**
[Opening line/visual that stops the scroll]

**[3–20s] PROBLEM + SOLUTION**
[What's the pain point? How does product help?]

**[20–40s] BENEFITS / DEMO**
[Show product in action or explain why it's great]

**[40–45s] CTA + EMOTIONAL CLOSE**
[What should they do? Why should they care?]

---

### Notes for Creator
[Any special instructions, alt takes, tone adjustments]

```

## UGC Script Types

### 1. **Problem → Solution**
Most common UGC format. Identify pain point, reveal how product solves it.

**Example:** "Struggling with energy bills? These smart HVAC systems use AI to reduce costs by 40%."

### 2. **Testimonial / Story**
Creator shares personal experience with the product (works for services, SaaS, products).

**Example:** "I hired this team for my legal case and within a week I had a settlement check. Here's what happened..."

**Key Elements:**
- Specific problem they had
- How they found the solution
- Results/outcome
- Why they recommend it
- CTA

### 3. **Educational / How-To**
Creator teaches a quick skill or concept while mentioning the brand/product.

**Example:** "3 mistakes you're making with your AC (and how Blue Mountain Energy fixes them)"

### 4. **Trend-Based / Entertainment**
Creator uses trending audio, dance, or format while weaving in brand message.

**Example:** "POV: You found a secret Taco Bell Cantina..." (using trending POV format)

### 5. **Comparison**
Creator shows before/after, wrong way/right way, etc.

**Example:** "What the insurance company wanted me to do vs. what my lawyer actually did..."

### 6. **Myth-Busting**
Creator debunks common misconceptions while promoting product/service.

**Example:** "'You can't sue if you were texting too.' Actually, here's how comparative negligence works..."

## Real-World Examples

### Example 1: Law Firm (Testimonial)

```
## UGC SCRIPT: Settlement Story

**Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels
**Length:** 35–45s
**Tone:** Authentic, relief/gratitude

### On-Screen Directions
- Selfie-style, natural lighting, home or car setting
- Casual attire, relatable vibe
- Warm, genuine expression

### Script

**[0–3s] HOOK**
"Hey everyone, I just had to share my experience with this team..."

**[3–18s] PROBLEM + DISCOVERY**
"I was in a car accident last month and honestly didn't know where to start. I left a voicemail at 8 AM, and by 10 AM I already had a call back—same day. That kind of quick response made all the difference."

**[18–35s] BENEFITS**
"From day one, they kept me in the loop. Anytime I had a question about medical bills or paperwork, they answered right away. No waiting forever for updates. They literally handled everything so I didn't have to stress. I got my settlement check last week and it was more than I expected."

**[35–45s] CTA + CLOSE**
"If you need someone who actually picks up the phone and gets the job done, these guys are it. Seriously, can't recommend them enough!"

### Notes for Creator
- Keep it conversational, not scripted-sounding
- Let genuine relief/gratitude show through
- Don't name-drop the firm; use "this team," "my guys," etc. (compliance)
```

### Example 2: Energy Company (Education + Entertainment)

```
## UGC SCRIPT: Vegas Heat Reality Check

**Platform:** TikTok
**Length:** 30–40s
**Tone:** Urgent, relatable, Vegas-specific

### On-Screen Directions
- Shot in home or near window showing bright Vegas sun
- Casual summertime attire
- Optional: fan going in background, ice water visible
- Natural lighting (bright Vegas light)

### Script

**[0–4s] HOOK**
"Okay, Las Vegas friends—110-degree summers hit different, and our AC died at the worst possible time."

**[4–18s] PROBLEM**
"We called a big company in town and they wanted $15K to replace the whole system. I was about to bite the bullet until I got a second opinion and called Blue Mountain Energy."

**[18–32s] SOLUTION + BENEFIT**
"They actually explained what was wrong, walked me through options, and saved us thousands. Turns out we didn't need to replace it—just fix it. Now? Home is cool, we saved money, and they're actually honest about what we need vs. what they want to sell."

**[32–40s] CTA**
"If you're in Vegas and your AC is acting up, don't get ripped off. Call Blue Mountain Energy first. Free estimate, no pressure."

### Notes for Creator
- Emphasize the "honest business" angle (they don't just upsell)
- Make it relatable to Vegas summer struggle
- Use conversational, slightly urgent tone
```

### Example 3: Mattress/Bedding (Screen Recording Format)

```
## UGC SCRIPT: Cruise Sleep at Home

**Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Length:** 30–45s
**Tone:** Aspirational, luxury, relaxed

### On-Screen Directions
- Screen recording of website/product pages
- Voice-over (smooth, calm tone)
- Soft background music (low volume)
- Text overlays highlighting key products

### Script

**[0–3s] HOOK**
"Remember that cruise-ship sleep? So good you never wanted to leave your cabin? *[pause]* You can have that at home."

**[3–15s] PRODUCT SHOWCASE**
"Carnival Home has pillows, bedding, towels, and robes that feel EXACTLY like ship-quality luxury. *[scroll to pillows]* These are plush but supportive. *[scroll to sheets]* Temperature-regulating. *[scroll to towels]* Melt-in-your-hands softness."

**[15–35s] EMOTIONAL BENEFIT**
"Honestly, every night feels like a mini-vacation. No more waking up with a crick in your neck. No scratchy towels. No sad bathrobe. Just that feeling of being wrapped in clouds after your shower."

**[35–45s] CTA**
"If you want that cruise-cabin comfort at home, tap the link. Bring the cruise home. Your body will thank you."

### Notes for Creator
- Keep voice calm, aspirational (not salesy)
- Use screen pauses to let viewers absorb products
- Music should evoke relaxation, not urgency
```

## Writing Tips for Strong UGC

1. **Hook First (0–3 seconds):** Stop the scroll immediately. Ask a question, make a bold statement, or show something surprising.

2. **Be Specific:** "They answered in 2 hours" > "They're responsive"

3. **Use Story Structure:** Setup (problem) → development (how we solved it) → payoff (result)

4. **Show, Don't Just Say:** If possible, demonstrate the product or show before/after

5. **Keep It Conversational:** People should hear a friend talking, not an ad

6. **One CTA:** Don't ask them to do 3 things. One clear action.

7. **Compliance Check:** No name-dropping (use "this team," "my guys" for law/medical). No false claims. No unlicensed testimonials for regulated industries.

8. **Platform Optimization:**
   - **TikTok:** Trending audio, jump cuts, fast pacing
   - **Reels:** Mix of cuts + holds, trend-aware, upbeat
   - **YouTube Shorts:** Slightly more polished, can be longer form

## Approval & Revisions

**Creator → Launchpad**
Creator submits raw footage/first edit.

**Launchpad Review**
- Does it hit brand messaging?
- Is compliance safe?
- Does it match tone/style?
- Are CTAs clear?

**Notes to Creator**
Keep feedback specific: tone, pacing, specific phrases, re-records needed.

**Re-submission & Final Approval**
Lock in final version before posting to paid/organic.
