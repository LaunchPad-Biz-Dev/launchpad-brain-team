# Media Buying

*Strategy, platform selection, ad copy, campaign setup, and optimization for lead generation and brand awareness*

---

## Overview

Media buying is the execution engine behind content. Great creative doesn't matter without proper targeting, platform selection, and budget allocation. Launchpad's media buying approach is:
- **Integrated:** Paired with content strategy
- **Data-driven:** Testing and optimization based on performance
- **Platform-focused:** Master 2-3 platforms instead of spreading budget thin
- **Results-oriented:** Tied to client business outcomes (leads, conversions, ROAS)

---

## Platform Strategy & Selection

### Primary Platforms by Use Case

#### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
**Best for:** Lead generation, conversions, retargeting, broad demographics
- **Strengths:** Largest audience, detailed targeting options, lead gen format, remarketing
- **Ad formats:** Lead Gen, Traffic, Conversions, Collection, Carousel, Video
- **Audience:** 18-65+, all income levels, broad interest-based targeting
- **Typical spend:** 50-70% of total media budget for lead-heavy businesses

**Example campaigns:**
- Solar sales (high-ticket): Lead Gen form → consultation booking
- Legal services: Lead Gen form → case evaluation
- HVAC: Lead Gen form → free estimate request
- Restaurants: Traffic → reservations or location clicks

#### Google Ads
**Best for:** Intent-driven campaigns, local search, keywords, bottom-of-funnel
- **Strengths:** High-intent traffic, keyword targeting, local pack visibility, budget control
- **Ad formats:** Search (text), Local Services, Display, YouTube pre-roll
- **Audience:** Users actively searching for your service
- **Typical spend:** 20-40% of total media budget for local/service-based

**Example campaigns:**
- "Currency exchange near me" (CCS)
- "HVAC repair [city]" (AC companies)
- "Lawyer near me [practice area]" (legal)
- Local Services Ads (high visibility, pay-per-lead for some verticals)

#### TikTok
**Best for:** Brand awareness, viral potential, younger demographics, organic-feel content
- **Strengths:** High engagement, trend participation, younger audience, low CPM
- **Ad formats:** In-feed ads, branded hashtag challenges, creator partnerships
- **Audience:** 13-40 (peak 18-24), trend-conscious, entertainment-first
- **Typical spend:** 5-15% of budget (supplementary, especially for B2C)

**Note:** TikTok is still proving ROI for lead gen; better for awareness and top-of-funnel engagement.

#### LinkedIn
**Best for:** B2B, professional services, recruitment, high-ticket offers
- **Strengths:** Professional targeting, job title/company filtering, decision-maker access
- **Ad formats:** Sponsored content, InMail, Conversation ads, Lead Gen forms
- **Audience:** Professionals, executives, business owners
- **Typical spend:** 5-20% for B2B; rarely used for B2C

#### YouTube
**Best for:** Long-form storytelling, educational content, pre-roll on niche channels
- **Strengths:** Contextual targeting, in-stream ads, brand-safe placements
- **Ad formats:** TrueView (skippable), Non-skippable, Bumper, Masthead
- **Audience:** Varies by channel, typically older demographics for longer-form content
- **Typical spend:** 10-20% for educational/awareness campaigns

### Platform Selection Framework
**Questions to Ask:**
1. Where does the ideal customer spend time?
2. What's the customer journey? (awareness → consideration → decision)
3. What's the budget? (Some platforms have higher minimum CPM)
4. What's the business model? (B2B = LinkedIn; local service = Google Local; B2C = Meta + TikTok)
5. What type of content do we have? (Video = YouTube/TikTok; static = Meta; long-form = LinkedIn)

**Rule:** Choose 2-3 platforms max. Spreading budget thin leads to poor optimization.

## Audience Targeting Strategy

### Demographic Targeting
**Essential demographics:**
- Age range (e.g., 25-65 for home services; 18-35 for trendy products)
- Gender (if product-specific, otherwise both)
- Income level (affects buying power)
- Geographic location (local business focus)

### Psychographic & Behavioral Targeting
**Interest-based:**
- Topics the audience cares about (e.g., "home improvement" for solar)
- Competitor interest (people interested in competitor pages)
- Lifestyle and aspirations

**Behavioral:**
- Purchase history (high-income spenders)
- Recently moved (relevant for home services)
- Life events (engaged, new parents, etc.)
- Device usage (mobile vs. desktop)

**Custom audiences:**
- Website visitors (retargeting, pixel-based)
- Email list (lookalike audiences)
- Customer lifetime value targets (upsell campaigns)

### Ideal Audience Profile Example (Solar Sales, Las Vegas)
- **Age:** 30-55
- **Gender:** Both
- **Income:** $50,000+
- **Location:** Las Vegas metro + 50-mile radius
- **Interests:** Home improvement, energy efficiency, environmental causes
- **Behaviors:** Recently purchased home or made home improvement, high engagement with solar content
- **Life events:** Homeowner, interested in energy savings, high electric bills

## Campaign Types & Strategy

### Lead Generation Campaigns
**Objective:** Collect qualified leads for sales follow-up
**Platform:** Meta Lead Gen format (primary)
**Ad structure:**
- Hook (headline/image/video that stops the scroll)
- Body copy (benefit statement, address pain)
- CTA (Call-to-Action button: "Get Free Consultation," "Download Guide," etc.)
- Lead form (name, email, phone, optional: custom questions)

**Best practices:**
- **Hook first:** 2-3 seconds to grab attention
- **Clear value:** What do they get? (free guide, consultation, estimate)
- **Short forms:** Name + email minimum; add 1-2 custom questions if needed
- **Follow-up:** Auto-reply email + sales call within 24 hours

**Example (Solar):**
```
Headline: "Skip the Airport Exchange Rates — Get Fair Rates in Vegas"
Body: "Stop overpaying for currency. We offer 80+ currencies with rates updated hourly."
CTA: "Get a Free Rate Quote"
Form fields: Name, Email, Phone, "Which currencies are you exchanging?"
```

### Conversion Campaigns
**Objective:** Drive immediate action (purchase, booking, sign-up)
**Platform:** Meta Conversions, Google Shopping/Search
**When to use:** You have a clear offer, product, or booking system

### Awareness/Reach Campaigns
**Objective:** Build brand awareness, reach large audience
**Platform:** Meta Reach, TikTok, YouTube pre-roll
**When to use:** New brand launch, seasonal campaigns, low initial budget

### Retargeting Campaigns
**Objective:** Re-engage users who visited website or engaged with content
**Platform:** Meta, Google Display
**Audience:** Website visitors (pixel-based), video viewers, post engagers
**Messaging:** Different from cold traffic (assume some familiarity)

**Example funnel:**
- Audience 1: Visited website but didn't convert → "Still thinking? Here's why we're different"
- Audience 2: Viewed video but didn't click → "Watch how [client] saved $X with [service]"
- Audience 3: Abandoned cart/didn't complete form → "Forgot something? Get your [offer]"

## Ad Copy & Creative Strategy

### Hook Formulas (What Stops the Scroll)
**Problem-focused:**
- "Stop wasting $X/month on [problem]"
- "Before you [action], know this..."
- "Your [authority] just changed"

**Curiosity-focused:**
- "I found a way to [benefit] in [time]"
- "This [industry] secret..."
- "One weird trick..."

**Value-focused:**
- "Get [benefit] for $X/month"
- "Free [resource] inside"
- "Limited time: [offer]"

**Story-focused:**
- "[Customer name] had [problem]. Here's what changed..."
- "We tried [X], then discovered [Y]..."

### Body Copy Best Practices
1. **Lead with benefit** — What's in it for them?
2. **Address objection** — What's holding them back?
3. **Show proof** — Social proof, testimonials, numbers
4. **Create urgency** — Limited time, limited spots
5. **Clear CTA** — What should they do next?

**Example (CCS FX ad):**
```
Headline: "Don't Get Tricked By Airport Rates ✈️"

Body:
"Airport currency exchanges charge 10-20% more than fair rates.

At Continental Currency Services, we offer 80+ currencies with live rates updated hourly.

✓ Fair prices
✓ Friendly service
✓ Fast exchanges
✓ Open weekends

Traveling soon? Get your currency before the airport."

CTA: "Get a Free Rate Quote" (form)
```

### Testing Creative Variations
**Always test multiple angles:**
- 2-3 different hooks
- 2 different value propositions
- 2 different CTAs
- 2 different images/videos

**Optimize by:** Pause underperformers, scale winners. Refresh creative every 2-4 weeks (audience fatigue).

## Budget Allocation & Optimization

### Monthly Budget Breakdown (Example: $5,000/month)
- **Prospecting (cold traffic):** 50% = $2,500
- **Retargeting (warm traffic):** 30% = $1,500
- **Testing/new creatives:** 20% = $1,000

### Budget by Platform (Example: $5,000 Meta primary, Google secondary)
- **Meta (70%):** $3,500 → $1,750 prospecting, $1,050 retargeting, $700 testing
- **Google (20%):** $1,000 → keyword campaigns
- **Testing (10%):** $500 → new platforms or creative angles

### Bidding Strategies
**Cost Per Click (CPC):** Best for traffic, awareness
**Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Best for lead gen (optimize for form fills)
**Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM):** Best for brand awareness
**Target Cost Per Action (tCPA):** Automated bidding for conversions

### Performance Optimization Process
1. **Monitor daily:** Check ad performance, pause underperformers
2. **Adjust bids:** Increase bids on high-performing ads, decrease on low performers
3. **Refresh creative:** Every 2-4 weeks to prevent fatigue
4. **A/B test:** Always have 2-3 variations running
5. **Scale winners:** Once CPL/ROAS is proven, increase budget
6. **Weekly reports:** Monitor trends, adjust strategy

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Advertising KPIs
- **Cost Per Click (CPC):** How much does each click cost? (lower is better)
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** % of impressions that result in clicks (industry avg: 1-3%)
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Total spend ÷ number of leads (varies by industry)
- **Conversion Rate:** % of leads that convert to customers
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):** Total spend ÷ customers acquired
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Revenue ÷ ad spend (breakeven = 1:1, profitable = 2:1+)
- **Frequency:** How often the average person sees your ad (optimal: 2-5x per week)

### Industry Benchmarks
| Industry | Target CPL | Conversion Rate | Breakeven ROAS |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| Solar | $100-150 | 5-10% | 2:1 |
| Legal | $50-100 | 3-5% | 2:1 |
| HVAC | $30-50 | 8-15% | 1.5:1 |
| Real Estate | $25-50 | 10-20% | 1.5:1 |
| Restaurants | $5-15 | 20-40% | 1:1 |

### CRM Integration
Once ads drive leads into the CRM:
- **Email open rate:** Should be 25-40% (measure engagement)
- **Click rate:** Should be 5-10% (measured engagement)
- **Conversion rate:** % of leads that become customers (tracked manually)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total marketing spend ÷ new customers acquired
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Average revenue per customer (used to calculate ROAS targets)

## Platform-Specific Tactics

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Tips
- Use **video** when possible (higher engagement than static)
- Test **carousel ads** (multiple images, different offers)
- Retarget **video viewers** (people who watched 25%, 50%, 75% of video)
- Use **custom audiences** (email lists, website visitors)
- Schedule ads for **peak times** (lunch, evening commute for most verticals)

### Google Ads Tips
- Use **exact match** keywords (most qualified, higher cost)
- Add **negative keywords** (exclude irrelevant searches)
- Create **location extensions** (show address, driving distance)
- Use **call extensions** (add phone number, click-to-call)
- Test **different landing pages** (specific service vs. homepage)

### TikTok Tips
- Use **native TikTok style** (polished ads underperform here)
- Leverage **trends and sounds** (algorithm loves trending audio)
- Test **creator partnerships** (authentic endorsements work better)
- Use **longer video content** (15-60 sec outperforms 6-15 sec)

### YouTube Tips
- Create **skippable pre-roll ads** (people skip after 5 seconds; get hook in first 3)
- Target **specific channels** (contextual match is powerful)
- Use **end screens and cards** (drive clicks at video end)
- Retarget **video viewers** (people who watched your content)

## Common Mistakes & How to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Spreading budget too thin
**Problem:** Using all 5 platforms with $1,000 each = no optimization
**Solution:** Pick 2-3 platforms, go deep, optimize

### Mistake 2: No testing
**Problem:** Running one ad and wondering why it's not working
**Solution:** Always have 2-3 ad variations running simultaneously

### Mistake 3: Wrong audience targeting
**Problem:** Showing solar ads to renters who can't install panels
**Solution:** Define ideal customer first, then reverse-engineer targeting

### Mistake 4: Ignoring retargeting
**Problem:** Only prospecting, ignoring warm audience (much cheaper to convert)
**Solution:** Allocate 30-40% of budget to retargeting

### Mistake 5: Poor CTA or form
**Problem:** Great ad, but form is too long or CTA is unclear
**Solution:** Test short forms (name + email), clear CTAs (specific benefit, not just "click here")

### Mistake 6: No follow-up system
**Problem:** Driving leads but no one calls them within 24 hours
**Solution:** Set up CRM automation, sales alerts, follow-up emails before sending traffic
