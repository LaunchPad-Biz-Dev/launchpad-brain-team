# Skill: Social Media Posting Workflow

Owner: Cynthia Hernandez (Social Media Manager / Art Director)

## Responsibilities
- Manages brand aesthetics and overall look/feel for all client accounts
- Schedules and publishes posts across all platforms
- Works closely with Kyle to ensure content aligns with social strategy
- Maintains consistent tone of voice and on-brand aesthetic per client

## Platforms Managed
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- LinkedIn
- Google My Business

## Tools Used
- Sprout Social — scheduling and publishing
- Canva — graphics and client approvals
- Adobe Creative Suite — advanced editing

## Process
1. Receive approved content from post-production
2. Write captions aligned with client brand voice
3. Schedule posts in Sprout Social
4. Confirm posting calendar with Abril
5. Monitor engagement after posting

## Notes
- Cynthia should be notified when content is fully approved and ready to post
- Currently there is a gap where completion confirmations don't always reach her
- Works heavily with Kyle — their content must align strategically
