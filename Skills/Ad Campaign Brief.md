# Ad Campaign Brief

## Purpose
An ad campaign brief is the strategic and tactical blueprint for running paid advertising across platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.). It outlines campaign goals, audience strategy, creative direction, messaging, and expected outcomes. A solid campaign brief ensures alignment between creative, strategy, and performance goals.

**Golden Rule:** Every campaign should tie directly back to business objectives (lead generation, brand awareness, sales, etc.) and fit into a layered funnel strategy.

---

## When to Use
- Launching new ad campaigns
- Multi-platform advertising (Meta + Google + TikTok)
- Lead generation or sales-focused campaigns
- Brand awareness initiatives
- Retargeting campaigns
- Client deliverables and strategy documents

**Campaign Name:** BME Spring HVAC Lead Generation
**Objective:** Lead Generation (Tune-ups, Estimates, Replacements)

**Success Metrics:**
- CPL: $87.50 or less

- Goal: Remind and educate

- Goal: Drive form submissions

- Age: 28–65

## Ad Campaign Best Practices

1. **Layer Your Funnel:** Cold → Warm → Hot audiences require different messaging and budgets
2. **One Business Metric:** Define what "success" means (CPL, ROAS, lead volume); everything else supports that
3. **Platform Focus:** Pick 1–2 primary platforms; master them before expanding
4. **Audience Specificity:** "Homeowners interested in HVAC" beats "Everyone in Las Vegas"
5. **Clear Conversion Path:** Know exactly what happens after the click (where does the lead go?)
6. **Budget Discipline:** Stick to cost-per-lead targets; pause underperformers aggressively
7. **Messaging Matters:** A-M-M framework ensures relevance to each audience
8. **Test Early:** Launch with 3–5 audience/creative combos; identify winners by week 2

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Unclear objective:** Mixing awareness with conversion campaigns wastes budget
- **No cold audience:** Jumping straight to retargeting limits scale
- **Vague targeting:** "Interested in home improvement" (too broad) vs. "Recently searched AC repair + homeowner"
- **No conversion tracking:** Flying blind on what actually converts
- **Wrong landing page:** Cold audience video traffic lands on a form (friction) instead of educational LP
- **Budget confusion:** Not knowing where the money is actually going or what ROI is
- **Approval delays:** Starting campaigns before landing page/creative is finalized
