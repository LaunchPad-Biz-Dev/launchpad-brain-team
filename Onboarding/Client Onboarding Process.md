# Client Onboarding Process — Context Extraction Framework

> **Scope:** This note defines the **context extraction framework** — what information to pull from a client during onboarding and how to structure it into a client snapshot brief. It is NOT the execution timeline.
>
> For the execution SOP (phases, meetings, 14-day timeline), see the canonical [[New Client Onboarding Process]].
> For Abril's specific automation wishlist, see [[Client Onboarding Process - Abril]].

## Overview
The client onboarding process is the first touchpoint where we establish alignment, understand business goals, extract key context, and prepare for execution. This process transforms raw discovery into actionable strategy.

**Core Philosophy:** Better upfront context = better execution + fewer revisions.

---

## Initial Chat Setup — New Client Onboarding Prompt

When starting a new client engagement in ChatGPT, use this structured prompt to seed the AI with all necessary context:

### Required Fields (Copy + Paste)

```
Client Name:
Industry / Service Type:
Website:
Primary Locations / Markets:

Main Goal for the First 90 Days:
(Examples: lead gen, brand positioning, content system, ads, CRM, etc.)

What LaunchPad Is Handling:
(Select all that apply)
- Content
- Ads
- CRM / Systems
- Branding / Strategy
- Website
- Photo / Video
- Consulting Only

Package Level / Scope:
(Essential / Comprehensive / Custom / Sprint)

Key Offers / Services to Prioritize:

Target Audience(s):

Brand Voice / Archetype (if known):

Platforms in Play:
(FB / IG / TikTok / Google / YouTube / LinkedIn / Other)

Current Assets Available:
(Logos, brand kit, past content, drive links, photos, etc.)

Known Constraints or Risks:
(Budget, bandwidth, timing, approvals, legal, etc.)

Anything We Should Avoid or Be Careful About:

Notes From Sales / Discovery Call:
```

### What Happens After Prompt Is Complete

1. **Generate client snapshot brief** — Synthesize all data into 1-2 page overview
2. **Identify quick-win priorities** — What can we win on first?
3. **Map work** under **Better Content → Better Leads → Better Systems**
4. **Move into execution** — Choose path:
   - Quarterly plan development
   - Media / content planning
   - CRM + ads build-out
   - Branding / strategy refinement

## Information Gathering Phase

### Sources to Mine
- Discovery call notes
- Email correspondence
- Internal emails
- Meeting recordings / transcripts
- Past proposals
- Client's existing materials (website, social, brand guidelines)
- Compliance documents (if relevant, like healthcare or legal)

### What to Extract From All Sources

**Organizational Info**
- Company structure, history, evolution
- Leadership team + decision-makers
- Departments + stakeholders
- Roles and approval workflows

**Business Model & Operations**
- Services / products offered
- Current capacity / volume
- Growth targets
- Expansion plans
- Operating model (team, tools, processes)

**Brand & Positioning**
- Mission, vision, values
- How they describe themselves
- Core differentiators
- Brand voice / tone
- Competitive positioning
- Brand archetypes (use [[Kay Putnam Brand Archetypes]])

**Market & Audience**
- Primary target audience (for content focus)
- Secondary audiences
- Pain points they solve
- Where leads actually come from (different from content audience)
- Geographic focus

**Goals & Constraints**
- 90-day goals and key results
- Long-term vision
- Budget realities
- Timeline constraints
- Approval processes (who says yes to what)
- Legal / compliance guardrails
- Risk areas (things to avoid)

**Service Specifics**
- If multiple service lines, understand each:
  - Customer journey
  - Operational flow
  - Go-to-market strategy
  - Pricing model
  - Capacity

## Client Snapshot Brief (Deliverable)

After information gathering, synthesize into a **Client Snapshot** that covers:

1. **Company Overview**
   - What they do, how they do it, where they're going
   - Leadership + core team
   - Evolution / history relevant to strategy

2. **Business Model & Capacity**
   - Current operations + volume
   - Target capacity / growth
   - Service lines being prioritized

3. **Brand Identity**
   - Core mission + positioning
   - Brand archetypes
   - Voice and visual direction (if locked)

4. **Primary Audiences**
   - Who content is actually created for
   - Their pain points and needs
   - What messaging resonates
   - How they make decisions

5. **Market & Competition**
   - Where customers come from (leads, referrals, organic)
   - Competitive landscape
   - Positioning advantage

6. **Constraints & Guardrails**
   - Budget reality
   - Timeline reality
   - Approval workflows
   - Compliance / legal considerations
   - Things to avoid or be careful about

## Working With External Partners

If the client works with external partners (PR firms, design agencies, etc.):

- Document each partner's role and responsibilities
- Map communication workflows
- Identify approval flows and decision-makers
- Note areas of integration vs. separation
- Ensure alignment on messaging and timeline

Example: [[Upbeat Health]] works with [[84 Comms]] for PR. LaunchPad handles content, CRM, lead workflows. Integration points: messaging alignment, spokesperson prep, narrative consistency.

---

## Next Steps After Onboarding

Once snapshot is locked and approved by client:

1. **Choose execution path** based on main goal for first 90 days
2. **Create working documents** (briefs, content calendars, CRM maps)
3. **Assign team members** to client account
4. **Set up communication cadence** ([[Scheduling and Calendar Management]])
5. **Begin execution** under appropriate framework ([[Content Workflow — Shoot to Post]], [[GHL Automation Setup]], etc.)
