# New Client Onboarding Process

> **Canonical onboarding SOP.** This is the execution timeline — the phased 14-day process from contract signed to launch. For related angles on onboarding, see:
> - [[Client Onboarding Process]] — context extraction framework (what info to pull from the client)
> - [[Client Onboarding Process - Abril]] — Abril's automation wishlist + Phase 1C build decisions
> - [[Client Onboarding Workflow]] — skill definition for the AI onboarding agent

Source: Notion — Client Onboarding Stages
Owner: Abril Rodriguez (Creative Director)
Standard Timeline: 14 business days

## Overview
Client onboarding begins after a client has signed and paid their proposal. They are handed from sales to the onboarding team. A client is not considered fully "active" until campaigns are launched and generating leads.

## Phase 1: Pre-Onboarding Setup
- Create Google Shared Drive with standard folders:
  - Brand assets
  - Graphics
  - Videos
  - Photos
  - Login credentials
- Grant access to all team members and the client
- Create client portal in Notion with contact details and resource links
- Draft and send welcome email (Abril sends, CC Emigen)

## Phase 2: Onboarding Meetings

### Meeting 1 — Onboarding Meeting
- Process overview and setting expectations
- Review client portal and Google Drive
- Connect to client platforms (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
- Collect login credentials
- Overview of monthly process after onboarding
- Basic CRM overview

### Meeting 2 — Discovery Meeting
- Brand backstory, mission, goals
- Ideal client and messaging
- Tonality and communication style
- Short-term and long-term vision

## Phase 3: Create Client Assets & Strategy
- Build brand guide (mood board, brand story, voice, visual identity)
- Develop quarterly plan
- Brief the full team on the client

## Phase 4: Implementation (Content → Leads → Systems)

### Content
- Create social media and brand kits
- Audit and optimize all social media platforms (TT, FB, IG, YT, GMB)
- Update profile pictures, cover photos, bios
- Develop initial graphics
- Produce initial videos

### Leads
- Build brand awareness campaigns
- Set up lead generation campaigns
- Set up retargeting for lead generation

### Systems
- Create GHL CRM sub-account
- Set up A2P messaging registration
- Build lead follow-up sequences:
  - Fast Five (days 1-5)
  - Long-term nurture (10, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days)

## Phase 5: Launch
- First content shoot
- Assign video editors
- Approve content
- Launch ads
- Client is now officially ACTIVE

## Tracking Phases in Notion
1. Onboarding — initial meetings and info gathering
2. Optimization — updating assets and profiles
3. Ready to Launch — all assets prepared
4. Launched — campaigns running
