# New Employee Onboarding

Source: Notion — Team Onboarding Dashboard
Owner: Antonia Andrade (HR)

## Overview
Standard process for bringing new team members into Launchpad. Covers both in-house and remote team members. Designed to assess skills, identify strengths, and place people in roles that fit their growth path. Takes approximately 30-90 days depending on role.

## Core Hiring Philosophy
New hires are selected on:
1. **Willingness to learn** — Mindset matters more than experience
2. **Culture fit** — Collaborative, growth-oriented, interested in quality
3. **Hands-on experience** — We teach through doing, not training modules
4. **Potential for growth** — Where can this person go in 6-12 months?

## Pre-Onboarding (Before Start Date)

**Logistics:**
- Confirm start date and orientation schedule
- Send welcome email with detailed onboarding plan and 30/60/90-day goals
- Prepare and send contractor agreement and NDA for signature
- Set up company email account and provide email signature instructions
- Grant access to necessary software and tools
- Share login credentials for shared drives and communication platforms
- Arrange data security and privacy protocol training

**Communications:**
- Welcome message from [[Anthony Mendoza]] (sets tone, excitement about their arrival)
- Brief intro from their direct manager explaining what they'll be doing
- Team introduction if applicable
- Timezone considerations for remote team members

## First Day

**Morning (Welcome & Setup):**
- Virtual welcome meeting with team and CEO ([[Anthony Mendoza]])
- Verify all tool access is working
- Overview of company mission, values, and culture
- Introduction to direct manager and key team members
- Finalize any pending paperwork

**Afternoon (Context Building):**
- Overview of current projects and client portfolio
- Walkthrough of Notion workspace and key documents
- Introduction to communication tools (Slack, WhatsApp for quick updates)
- Overview of weekly meeting structure and expectations
- Set up 1-on-1 schedule with direct manager

## First Week

**Daily Standups:**
- Join daily huddles (even if just listening initially)
- Get a feel for how the team communicates
- See what priority items look like

**Training & Context:**
- Deeper training on tools relevant to the role
- SOPs and processes walkthrough
- [[Anthony Mendoza]] or direct manager provides initial mentorship
- Start on small, supervised tasks to learn workflow
- Ask lots of questions — this is the time

**Deliverables:**
- Complete security/privacy training
- Review onboarding checklist with manager
- Identify blockers or confusion
- Set up 30/60/90-day goal framework

## First Month (Days 1-30)

**What They're Doing:**
- Learning the systems and processes
- Building relationships with team
- Completing initial assignments under supervision
- Getting feedback on work quality and approach
- Exploring tools and workflows

**What Managers Are Doing:**
- Providing clear direction and context
- Giving constructive feedback (what's working, what to adjust)
- Checking in daily for blockers
- Gradually increasing responsibility
- Assessing skill level and growth potential

**Mid-Month Check-In (Day 15):**
- How is onboarding going?
- Any blockers or confusion?
- Adjustment of goals if needed
- Feedback on performance and fit

**30-Day Assessment:**
- Can they handle tasks independently?
- Do they understand the culture and processes?
- Where are their strengths and growth areas?
- Are they in the right role?

## Months 2-3 (Days 31-90)

**Increasing Responsibility:**
- Assigned to client projects with growing autonomy
- Expected to contribute ideas in meetings
- Providing feedback to peers
- Building deeper relationships across departments

**Path Assessment:**
- Is this person suited for their current role?
- Are they Content, Leads, or Systems focused?
- Do they have potential for leadership?
- What training/development are they ready for?

**90-Day Evaluation:**
- Full performance review with direct manager
- Decision on permanent role/path
- Compensation discussion if applicable
- 6-month growth goals set

## LaunchPad University (Internal Training)

All new employees should complete LA University training to understand:
- **The Content → Leads → Systems Framework**
  - Content: How we create and share brand stories
  - Leads: How we generate and manage customer acquisition
  - Systems: How we build automations and processes
- **How Launchpad Works with Clients**
  - Onboarding process and expectations
  - Client communication and feedback loops
  - Monthly cadence and deliverables
- **Team Processes and Expectations**
  - Daily huddles and weekly kickoffs
  - How feedback works
  - Quality standards and revision process

Courses are taught by Kyle Fuller and documented in Descript transcripts.

## Department-Specific Onboarding Paths

### Creative / Content Track
**Owner:** [[Abril Rodriguez]]

**Role Possibilities:** Content creator, graphic designer, social media manager

**First Month Focus:**
- Understand brand archetyping and StoryBrand (foundational frameworks)
- Learn how [[Abril Rodriguez]] reviews content (quality standards)
- Shadow on client calls to understand context
- Study past campaigns and why they worked
- Start on small design/content tasks

**Skills Assessed:**
- Eye for design and storytelling
- Communication and collaboration
- Ability to take feedback
- Quality attention to detail

**Tools Setup:**
- Adobe Creative Suite access
- Canva access
- Opus.ai (if applicable)
- Sprout Social (if social media role)
- Google Drive access to brand kits and templates

### Systems / Leads Track
**Owner:** [[Anthony Mendoza]] (strategy), [[Amber Nardo]] (CRM/Ads)

**Role Possibilities:** CRM specialist, media buyer, automation builder

**First Month Focus:**
- Understand GoHighLevel architecture and workflows
- Learn how customer journeys are designed
- Study email/SMS copy templates
- Get familiar with ad platforms and performance metrics
- Start with CRM setup or simple automations under supervision

**Skills Assessed:**
- Attention to detail (critical for ad accounts)
- Systems thinking and problem-solving
- Communication ability (copy writing, client updates)
- Speed of learning new platforms

**Tools Setup:**
- GoHighLevel access
- Relevant ad platform access (Meta, Google, TikTok)
- Email marketing tools
- Analytics and reporting tools

### Video Editing / Production Track
**Owner:** [[Robert Quiantanace]] (creative lead), [[Kyle Fuller]] (day-to-day)

**Role Possibilities:** Video editor, content producer, videographer

**First Month Focus:**
- Learn Launchpad's editing style and workflow
- Study client deliverables to understand quality standards
- Complete CapCut and Premiere Pro training basics
- Start with simple edits under supervision
- Shadow on shoots to understand production

**Skills Assessed:**
- Technical proficiency with editing software
- Creative eye for pacing and storytelling
- Attention to detail (color, audio, consistency)
- Ability to interpret creative briefs

**Tools Setup:**
- CapCut (primary)
- Premiere Pro access
- Google Drive access for raw footage
- Adobe Creative Suite (for graphics, color, effects)

## Expectations & Culture

**What We Expect:**
- Willingness to learn and ask questions
- Showing up on time and being reliable
- Quality work, not rushed work
- Collaborative spirit and open feedback
- Ownership of your projects
- Communication about blockers early

**What We Provide:**
- Clear direction and expectations
- Regular feedback and mentorship
- Growth opportunities and expanded responsibility
- A team that cares about your development
- Time to learn the craft properly

**Red Flags (Things That Don't Work Here):**
- Not asking for help or feedback
- Defensive about constructive criticism
- Blaming others instead of solving problems
- Not showing up or communicating delays
- Rushing through work without quality focus

## 30/60/90-Day Goals Template

Use this for onboarding and ongoing development:

**30 Days (Foundation):**
- Goal 1: Master basic tools and workflows
- Goal 2: Understand team processes and culture
- Goal 3: Complete first assignment with feedback

**60 Days (Growing Capability):**
- Goal 1: Handle projects with minimal supervision
- Goal 2: Contribute ideas in team meetings
- Goal 3: [Role-specific skill development]

**90 Days (Independent Contributor):**
- Goal 1: Own projects from start to finish
- Goal 2: Mentor or help onboard others
- Goal 3: [Demonstrate mastery of role]
