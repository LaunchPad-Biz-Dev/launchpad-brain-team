Format guide

Every hero setup gets shot in more than one ratio. Frame loose enough to crop — decide the format at the camera, not in post.

Vertical 9:16
Reels · TikTok · Stories

Handheld POV walk-ups to the counter, pour and dispense close-ups, single-subject portraits, drink raised toward camera. Subject dead center, headroom at the top for the Stories UI to sit over.

Feed 4:5 — primary
IG feed · 1080×1350

The TBC house format — most of the shoot should be composed for this. Populated storefront, crew lineup, drink in hand. Keep a dark band top or bottom for typography.

Square 1:1
Feed carousel · grid

Centered product beauty, macro condensation and swirl detail, overhead flat-lay of cups. Symmetry does the work — put the freeze on the center axis and leave even margins.

Horizontal 16:9
Website header · PR · press

Ultra-wide storefront hero, concourse establishing, group behind the counter. Shoot these wider than feels right — web headers crop hard top and bottom, and press will re-crop again. Worth setting up deliberately, since nothing else on the day produces a true landscape frame.

Technical notes

Lighting and color — this is the hard part

  • Allegiant is a domed stadium. The concourse is 100% artificial light. Expect a nasty mix — LED menu boards, purple neon, overhead fluorescent, and TV screens, all different color temperatures in one frame.
  • Shoot RAW. Set a custom white balance off a neutral surface at the counter, then check it again out in the concourse. Don't trust auto WB.
  • Expose for the storefront glow and let the concourse fall off dark. The dark surround is on-brand and it gives design room for type.

Flash

  • TBC's approved visual system is direct-flash nightlife. Bring on-camera flash and use it — it isn't a compromise, it's the look.
  • Flash also kills the mixed-temperature problem on people. Use it across all of Block C and Block E.

Glass

  • 24–70 f/2.8 for coverage — Blocks A, B, C.
  • Fast prime, 35 f/1.4, for lifestyle and low-light crowd — Blocks E, F.
  • Something longer for candid guest shots without invading anyone's space.

Framing for downstream use

  • Every hero gets a horizontal, a 4:5, and a 9:16 version. Feed is 1080×1350, Stories and Reels are 9:16, the website needs 16:9.
  • Leave negative space. Our approved TBC system places 15–25% text in the photo's natural dark zones. If every frame is edge-to-edge busy, design can't use it — consciously compose empty areas into the top or bottom third of hero frames.
  • Volume target: 300–500 frames across three hours. Cover A, C and D ruthlessly — those are the ask.

Do not shoot

  • ✕No food. No tacos, no trays, no food in hero frames. Menu boards showing food are fine.
  • ✕No empty or dirty tables, trash cans, spills, or back-of-house clutter in frame.
  • ✕No competitor concession brands legible in the background.
  • ✕No minors in the same frame as an alcoholic Twisted Freeze.
  • ✕No guests who decline or look uncomfortable. Move on immediately.

Clearances Confirm tomorrow AM

These are the things that kill an asset in post if we don't handle them on the day.

  • 1Early access. Badged in by 1:30–2:00 PM for clean plates. Highest priority.
  • 2Alcohol depiction. Twisted Freezes are spiked. Taco Bell corporate and the NFL both have rules on alcohol imagery — typically everyone visible with an alcoholic drink must appear 25+. Confirm which SKU we're shooting and whether alcohol can appear in public-facing assets at all.
  • 3Talent releases. Signed releases for every employee. Bring a stack of guest releases too, or keep guest shots non-identifiable.
  • 4NFL / Raiders / Allegiant trademark clearance. The Shield is strict about commercial use of team marks and stadium IP. Get in writing what we can show and where it can run — organic social, paid, website and print are all different permissions.
  • 5Shooting credentials. Where can we set up? Tripod or light stand allowed on the concourse? Media escort required?
  • 6On-site wrangler. Who from DRG walks with us to pull employees and clear guests? Get a name and a cell number.
  • 7Camera-approved crew. Which employees are cleared, and will they be in fresh uniform?
  • 8Clean souvenir cups pulled from unused stock for product shots.
  • 9Deliverable date. When do they need first selects? Grand opening content is time-sensitive.

Post direction — approved TBC system

  • Warm, moody, nightlife grade. Dark base, saturated purple, punchy highlights.
  • Photo is the hero. Text enhances, never competes — 15–25% text, placed in the photo's actual negative space.
  • Typography: GT America Bold, all caps, white, 48–68pt. Small TBC lockup, bottom corner, white.
  • Feed format 1080×1350. Captions: one or two sentences, lowercase, casual, zero brand speak.
