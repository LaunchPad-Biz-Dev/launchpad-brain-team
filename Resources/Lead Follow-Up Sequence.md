# Resource: Lead Follow-Up Sequence (Standard)

This is the standard CRM follow-up sequence built in GoHighLevel for all clients.

## Sequence Timeline

| Day | Action | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Day 1-5 | Fast Five | Immediate high-touch follow-up |
| Day 10 | Follow-up | Check in |
| Day 30 | Follow-up | 1 month nurture |
| Day 60 | Follow-up | 2 month nurture |
| Day 90 | Follow-up | Quarterly check-in |
| Day 180 | Follow-up | 6 month nurture |
| Day 365 | Follow-up | Annual nurture |

## Fast Five (Days 1-5)
High-frequency immediate response sequence designed to make contact quickly after a lead comes in. Speed to lead is critical for conversion.

## Long-Term Nurture (10-365 days)
Keeps leads warm over time. Many clients convert months after first inquiry — this sequence ensures no lead goes cold.

## Built In
GoHighLevel (GHL) — sub-account per client
