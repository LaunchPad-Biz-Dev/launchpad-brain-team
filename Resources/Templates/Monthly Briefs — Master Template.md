# [CLIENT] — [Month Year] Briefs

> **How to use this template.** Five briefs, one month, one client. They are written in Week 3 of the cycle (Preproduction) and approved by the Creative Director before the end of that week. Everything in them traces to the client's Content Strategy (portal → Content Overview → Content Strategy). **A brief never introduces anything the strategy doesn't already contain**; new ideas go into the strategy's idea bank first.
>
> **Where each one lives in Notion**
> - Content Brief + Media Day + Scripts → *Project Boards → Video Production Management → Video Post Production Board*. Set **Client** (relation), **Film Date**, **Delivery Date**, **Videographer**, **Editor**, **Project Type**, **Aspect Ratio**, **Deliverables**, **Deliverables Tracker**. The Client relation is what routes it to the client's portal.
> - Graphics Brief → *Project Boards → Graphic Management → Graphic Design Board*. Set **Client**, **Month**, **Deliverables**, **Designer**, **Date Range**, **Aspect Ratios / Sizing**, **Deliverables Tracker**.
> - Ads Brief → the ads board, same Client relation.
>
> **The tag on every line item:** Pillar · Bucket · Job · Series (media: format). If a line can't be tagged with all four, it isn't ready.
>
> Replace every `[bracket]`. Delete the italic instructions before sharing with the client.

## Vocabulary (fixed; do not rename)
