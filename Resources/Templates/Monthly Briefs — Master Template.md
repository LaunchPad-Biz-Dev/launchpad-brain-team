# [CLIENT] — [Month Year] Briefs

> **How to use this template.** Three briefs, one month, one client. Written in Week 3 of the cycle (Preproduction); Creative Director approves by end of that week. Everything traces to the client's Content Strategy (portal → Content Overview → Content Strategy). **A brief never introduces anything the strategy doesn't contain**; new ideas go into the strategy's idea bank first.
>
> **Where each one lives in Notion**
> - **Shoot Brief** (this is the one the client reads) → the **Preproduction subtask** of the month's Video Task on the Deliverable Tracker. The **Client** on the parent task routes it to the portal.
> - **Graphics Brief** → *Graphic Design Board*; set Client, Month, Deliverables, Designer, Date Range, Sizing, Deliverables Tracker.
> - **Ads Brief** → the ads board (or the month's Ads task), same Client relation.
>
> **What is deliberately not in these briefs:** gear, lighting, camera choices, crew roles, call times (the callsheet, built after approval); edit notes (the Edit Brief, after the shoot); posting plan and dates (the Content Calendar); pillar percentages and bucket math (one line is enough). Each piece is said **once**, in shooting order, with its idea, script and shots together.
>
> **The tag on every piece:** Pillar · Bucket · Job · Series. Replace every `[bracket]`; delete these instructions before sharing.

## Vocabulary (fixed; do not rename)

Pillar (what we talk about) · Bucket (Awareness · Desire · Story · Choice; never renamed) · Job (Educate · Entertain · Inspire · Empathize · Prove · Convert) · Series (named, repeatable, one pillar × bucket cell) · Media (Video · Photo · Graphic) · Format · Theme (time-bound) · Segment (a shoot-day grouping, shoot briefs only).
