## 6. Buckets: what the content does

Every piece, in every pillar, lands in one of four buckets. The bucket names never change from client to client; the purpose, qualities and placement do.

| Bucket | Purpose for [Client] | Qualities (the filter) | Main jobs | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** | [make the brand and its hero product or people recognizable: how, for this client] | [4–5 adjectives] | [Inspire, Entertain…] | [channels] |
| **Desire** | [create want: crave for food, confidence for services, proof for contractors] | [4–5 adjectives] | [Educate, Inspire, Entertain…] | [channels] |
| **Story** | [build the world: people, craft, culture, community] | [4–5 adjectives] | [Empathize, Prove…] | [channels] |
| **Choice** | [enable the decision: menu, comparison, hours, how to book, how to reach a human] | [4–5 adjectives] | [Convert, Educate…] | [channels] |

**The qualities are the review question.** Before a piece is approved, the reviewer asks whether it feels like its bucket's adjectives. If it doesn't, it isn't finished.

## 10. Measurement: what we report and what would change the plan

| Bucket | What we watch | What good looks like in the first 90 days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** | reach, impressions, new followers, profile visits | |
| **Desire** | watch time, saves, shares, carousel completion | |
| **Story** | comments, DMs, shares | |
| **Choice** | [conversations, bookings, calls, form fills, cost per X] | |

**By pillar:** share of posts delivered vs planned; which series are compounding.
**By media:** video vs photo vs graphic engagement.

**What would change the plan**

- If [Desire is saved but Choice isn't converting] → [rework the path to the next step]
- If [Awareness grows outside the target area] → [tighten targeting]
- If a series underperforms two months running → retire it and replace from the idea bank
- Pillars don't change for at least two quarters. If one keeps needing to change, it was a theme.

## Vocabulary (fixed; do not rename)

| Term | Means | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pillar** | What we talk about. Product, service, audience or theme tied to a goal. 6–24 months. | "Still true next year?" |
| **Bucket** | What the content does: Awareness · Desire · Story · Choice. Never renamed. | "Where in the journey?" |
| **Job** | The piece's intent: Educate · Entertain · Inspire · Empathize · Prove · Convert. | "What should the viewer feel or do?" |
| **Series** | A named, repeatable piece in one pillar × bucket cell. | "Again next month without a meeting?" |
| **Media** | Video · Photo · Graphic. Deliverables are counted by media. | "Which team makes it?" |
| **Format** | The production shape inside a media type. | "What does the editor or designer open?" |
| **Theme** | A time-bound campaign or seasonal angle. Has an end date. | "When does it end?" |

Brief tag for every piece: **Pillar · Bucket · Job · Series (media: format)**
