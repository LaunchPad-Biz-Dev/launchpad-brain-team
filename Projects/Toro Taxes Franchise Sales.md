## CRM Project Status

### GoHighLevel Agency Account Setup
**Project Goal:** Build comprehensive franchise CRM system for Toro Taxes to manage 250+ franchisee sub-accounts across 30+ states

**Architecture:**
- Main agency account: Toro Taxes central management
- Sub-accounts template: Standardized snapshot for franchisees to customize locally
- 250 franchisee sub-accounts: One per franchisee location for local lead/client management

**Deadline:** December 2, 2025

**Key Team Members:**
- [[Abril Rodriguez]] — Technical CRM setup and workflows
- [[Anthony Mendoza]] — CRM architecture and technical oversight
- Antonia Andrade (Toro Taxes internal) — Brand expert, content alignment

### Core Workflows Being Built
1. **Lead Nurturing** — Automated sequences to engage and qualify leads post-inquiry
2. **Appointment Management** — Booking confirmations, reminders, rescheduling
3. **Review Request System** — Follow-ups after service completion
4. **Client Re-engagement** — Targeted campaigns to reactivate lapsed clients
5. **Pipeline Stages** — New Lead → Contact Made → Consultation Booked → Client Active → Follow-Up → Closed

### Sub-Account Template Features
- Standardized workflows (customizable for franchisee needs)
- Messaging templates aligned to brand voice
- Pre-built automation sequences
- Lead capture forms with qualification questions
- Client segmentation and tagging system
- Reporting and performance dashboards
