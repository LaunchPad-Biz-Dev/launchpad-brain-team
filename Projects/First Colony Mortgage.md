## Ad Spend & Strategy

- **Starting budget:** $3,000/month — 70% ($2,100) lead gen / 30% ($900) brand awareness + recruiting
- **Primary services:** Reverse mortgages (55–62+), cash-out refis, debt consolidation
- **Test campaign:** General home purchase — low spend, 1–2 month measurement window (market share expected mostly through realtor relationships)
- **Long-term:** When rates drop from high-6s to mid-5s (~18mo out from Jan 2026), scale to ~$20K/month for anticipated refi volume surge
- **Cash-out refi & debt consolidation** currently outperforming first-time-buyer campaigns given rate environment

## Messaging & Positioning

- **Stigma issue:** "Reverse mortgage" carries a negative historical association (scams) despite being FHA-backed today
- **Preferred language:** "Home equity conversion mortgage" (FHA-backed) or "Equity Advantage" (FCM's proprietary product name)
- **Audience insight:** Adult children (40s–50s) increasingly research reverse mortgages on behalf of parents — not just the 62+ demographic directly
- **Value prop:** Freedom from monthly mortgage payments, stay in home, stress-free retirement, avoid bankruptcy via cash access
- **Differentiator:** FCM's "Equity Advantage" extends eligibility down to age 55 vs. the standard FHA 62+ floor

## Compliance Considerations

- Avoid specific rate quotes / loan terms (TILA violations)
- Age targeting must use indirect language ("your parent") rather than "62 and older" — fair housing / age discrimination risk
- NMLS license 557156 must appear on all pages and ads
- Meta may suppress mortgage content — needs ongoing compliance monitoring

## Recruiting Strategy

- Indirect brand-visibility approach so target loan officers reach out organically (not cold outreach)
- ~200 loan officer "avatar" contacts to be uploaded as a GHL CRM smart list, targeted with recruiting/brand-awareness campaigns
- Cross-leverage Matt Nye's 12k–17k LinkedIn connections for national reach

## Brand & Social Setup (from scratch)

- Assets needed: logo, sub-logos, profile images, color palette
- Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok business pages — none existed at discovery
- Cross-tag individual team member pages into the business accounts
