## Brand Guidelines
**Source:** [365 Plumbing Brand Overview in Notion](https://www.notion.so/24db01f77d6d8025a851d6bbb18a8804)

### Brand Identity
- **Archetype:** Guy/Girl Next Door (primary), Hero (secondary)
- **Voice:** Real, respectful, relatable — no sales gimmicks, always honest
- **Tone:** Neighborly, approachable, trustworthy, with a playful edge
- **Core Values:** Repair before replace, fair & transparent pricing, family-owned local-first, community support (veterans, first responders, schools)
- **Visual Vibe:** Bold, clean, approachable. Modern but not corporate. Branded uniforms, tools, local Vegas backdrops.

### USP (Unique Selling Points)
- Repair > replace philosophy
- Honest pricing — same across all neighborhoods
- Bilingual (English & Spanish)
- 24/7 emergency availability
- Licensed, background-checked techs
- Free estimates

### Content Strategy
**Pillars:** Educate, Entertain, Empathize, Authority, Urgency
**Buckets:**
1. Richard: Unplunged — CEO's mix of authority & humor
2. On the Job — techs, day-in-the-life, rapid-fire
3. Client Testimonials — social proof, customer trust
4. Trends/Skits — funny, seasonal, shareable
5. Community — veterans, local causes, employee families
6. Promos/Specials — seasonal offers, urgent CTAs
7. Homeowner Plumbing 101 — tutorials, myth-busting, quick tips

### Do's & Don'ts
**Do:** Use branded pics as hero images, use gradients with text and icons, mix up carousel photos, use solid backgrounds with cutouts/emojis/icons, define text hierarchy with color and sizing
**Don't:** Use big color blocks (looks too infomercial), use generic stock-style graphics, repeat the same photo across every carousel slide
