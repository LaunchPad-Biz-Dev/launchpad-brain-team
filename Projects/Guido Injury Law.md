# Guido Injury Law

## Status
**Active**

## Industry
Personal Injury Law / Car Accident Claims

## Location
Las Vegas, Nevada

## Key Contact
- **Scott Guido** — Managing member, founder, and primary legal representative

## Attorney Background
Scott Guido, the managing member and founder of Guido Injury Law, brings over 20 years of personal injury experience with deep expertise in the insurance industry:
- 20+ years handling personal injury claims
- 6+ years in automobile insurance claims industry (insider perspective on claims process)
- 15 years as personal injury attorney at one of Las Vegas's largest and most prestigious personal injury law firms
- Former managing attorney of firm's Nevada offices
- Expert in property damage appraisal, claims coverage issues, policy analysis, and personal injury resolution

## Core Services
- Personal injury claims handling
- Car accident claims (immediate steps & guidance)
- Property damage claims
- Insurance claim denials (specializes in overturning rejected/denied cases)
- Free initial case consultations

## Practice Areas (Content Pillars)
1. **Car Accident Response** — What to do after an accident (3-step framework: call police, take photos, call Scott Guido)
2. **Property Damage Claims** — Specialized guidance on property damage assessments and claims
3. **Denied/Rejected Case Revival** — Expertise in taking on cases that have been denied or rejected by other firms
4. **Insurance Industry Knowledge** — Leveraging insider knowledge of insurance industry practices

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Primary Trait:** Compassionate and family-oriented (treats clients like family)
- **Secondary Trait:** Empathetic and professional
- **Tone:** Warm, approachable, knowledgeable
- **Value Proposition:** Personal touch combined with decades of expertise; client well-being prioritized over profit
- **Communication Style:** Direct but caring; educational without being condescending

## Brand Archetypes
- **Guide/Mentor** — Expert advisor who takes clients through complex process
- **Caregiver** — Shows genuine concern for client's well-being and stress levels
- **Hero** — Fights for clients and turns denials into victories
