## Brand Guidelines
- **Tagline**: "We listen to the beat of your life—to keep you upbeat through every stage of your health journey."
- **Content Pillars**: Education, Entertainment, Empathy
- **Content Buckets**: Primary Care, Wound Care, Team, Patient Care, Skits/Trends/Memes, Brand Support, Seasonal
- **Content Focus Split**: Wound Care 60%, Primary Care 20%, Other Services 20%
- **Monthly Deliverables**: 30 videos + 24 graphics + 5 carousels
- **Platform Strategy**:
  - Facebook: Educational/informational, no emojis, more text, serious and evergreen
  - Instagram: SEO hashtags, emojis, playful and informational, modern aesthetic
  - TikTok: Gen Z, short and humorous, only funny skits + trends
- **Caption Rules (Cynthia authoritative — 2026-06-18):**
  1. Instagram + TikTok: emojis YES (tasteful, wellness context). Facebook: NO emojis.
  2. BOTH (FB+IG) posts: no emojis (FB rule wins since one caption serves both platforms).
  3. Hashtags go INSIDE the caption body (summary field). Never in followUpComment.
  4. 2–3 engaging sentences. Topic-relevant. No filler. No AI cadence.
  5. Max 4 hashtags, relevant to topic. Examples: #health #wellness #healthcare #ivtherapy #woundcare #woundhealing #weightloss #lasvegas
- **Patient Experience Values**: Radiant touch, uplifting atmosphere, time/attention/intention. Patients should feel calm, confident, and cared for — not rushed or processed
- **Design Notes**: No gradients, all first letters must be capitalized, no JCPenney-style images (organic feeling preferred), use real shoot photos
- **Q1 Goals**: 1) Increase booked wound care visits, 2) Reinforce brand trust and awareness
