## Brand Guidelines
**Source:** [BME Brand Style Guide in Notion](https://www.notion.so/24cb01f77d6d80518506e8de8013eca1)

### Brand Essence
- **Tagline:** "Keep It Cool. Preserve Before You Replace."
- **Mascot:** Juanito (must appear with "Keep It Cool" slogan in all major assets)
- **Tone:** Friendly, professional, preservation-first

### Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Primary Blue #2A5D84 (headers, large text), Preservation Orange #F5A623 (CTAs, highlights, pricing), Secondary Blues #0047AB / #4A90E2 (gradients), Charcoal #333333 (body text), Soft Gray #F2F2F2 (backgrounds only)
- **Photography:** Bright, clean, community-friendly. Include AC units, technicians, thermostats, homes. NO AI-generated photos. NO grayscale. NO brick walls or muted tones.
- **Graphics:** Simple line icons with rounded edges in BME blue. Bright open space with bold text. Gradients or solid brand colors only.

### Do's & Don'ts
**Do:** Keep Juanito + "Keep It Cool" together, highlight comfort and savings, use bright gradients with blue and orange pops, center logo placement
**Don't:** Use black & white or grayscale imagery, show AC unit brand names, use AI stock photos, use brick walls or dull backdrops, stack photos on solid backgrounds, crop or misplace the logo

## Content & Social Media Strategy

### Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Friendly, professional, preservation-first
- **Key Message:** "Preserve Before You Replace" — honest service, no upsells
- **Audience:** Vegas homeowners concerned about AC costs and reliability
- **Values:** Transparency, long-term solutions, local expertise

### Social Media Content Pillars
1. **Educational** - HVAC maintenance tips, what to watch for, summer prep
2. **Trust-Building** - Team credentials, licensing, background checks, process transparency
3. **Local Authority** - Vegas-specific heat challenges, community focus
4. **Promotional** - Seasonal checklist, special offers, tune-up scheduling
5. **Engagement** - Problem identification, quick fixes homeowners can do

### Recent Caption Themes & Examples

#### "We Preserve Before We Replace"
Focus: Honest service positioning, no unnecessary upsells
- Captions emphasize "if it can be fixed, we fix it"
- Position against industry norm of automatic replacement

#### "3 Signs Your HVAC System Might Be Failing"
Focus: Educational, attention-grabbing carousel
- Signs: loud noises, weak cooling, energy bill spikes
- CTA: Schedule service check before it breaks

#### "A Team You Can Trust in Your Home"
Focus: Trust & credibility (licensed, background-checked, local)
- Highlight professional vetting, peace of mind
- Emphasize local presence

#### "5 Things You Can Do Right Now to Help Your AC"
Focus: Value-add, empowerment
- Homeowner actions: filter replacement, clearing debris, thermostat adjustment
- Ends with "we'll handle the rest"

#### "We Don't Guess — We Diagnose"
Focus: Expert positioning, differentiation from competitors
- Real tools, real tests, real answers
- No guesswork approach

#### "Local vs. National HVAC Companies"
Focus: Competitive positioning, community advantage
- Big chains: call centers, long waits, overpriced
- BME: local team, fast service, no corporate scripts

#### "Las Vegas Heat Is No Joke"
Focus: Urgency, seasonal relevance
- Vegas-specific heat challenges
- Prep system before summer hits full force

#### "Tired of High Power Bills?"
Focus: Value/savings angle
- Inefficient system = higher costs
- BME can diagnose and lower monthly expenses

#### "Your Summer HVAC Checklist"
Focus: Helpful resource, shareability
- Actionable steps: filter replacement, unit clearing, thermostat check, airflow test, tune-up booking

#### "What We Do at BME"
Focus: Service overview, credibility
- Repairs, tune-ups, honest work
- From quick fixes to full-system care
