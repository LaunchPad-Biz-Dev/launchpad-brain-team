## Company Info

Ben Nadig Law is a personal injury and criminal defense law firm operating across Nevada and expanding to Arizona. The firm emphasizes client-first values, educational content, and transparent legal guidance. Recent podcast series focused on California wildfire issues, legal rights, and insurance claims.

## Brand Guidelines

### Brand Essence
- **Tagline:** "We get you fixed. Then we get you paid."
- **Tone:** Empathetic, educational, transparent, advocacy-focused
- **Values:** Client-first, transparency, community support, fighting for fair compensation
- **Position:** Advocates for victims against insurance companies and legal complexity

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Educate** - Help clients understand legal rights, insurance claims, accident response
2. **Empathize** - Show support for victims, especially during crises (wildfires, accidents)
3. **Advocate** - Position firm as fighting against insurance companies for fair payouts
4. **Empower** - Help people take action and protect themselves

### Content Buckets
- **Wildfire-related claims** (high priority in 2025)
- **Car accident response & rights**
- **Insurance company tactics & how to counter them**
- **Personal stories & case studies**
- **New office announcements** (Phoenix expansion)
- **Team introductions & credibility**

### Visual Identity
- **Style:** Professional but approachable - clean talking heads, office environments, team collaboration
- **Photography:** Day-to-day work, professional attire, minimal lifestyle staging
- **Graphics:** Clean, educational infographics on legal process, rights, timelines
