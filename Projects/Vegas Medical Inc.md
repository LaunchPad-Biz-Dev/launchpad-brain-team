## Company Overview
Vegas Medical Inc is a multi-service medical and cosmetic center offering cash-based medical treatments, non-surgical cosmetic procedures, and surgical services across multiple Las Vegas locations.

## Services & Product Lines

### Cash-Based Medical Services
- Anti-aging treatments
- Medical weight loss (with hCG hormone, semaglutide, diet plans: Paleo & Keto)
- Hormone replacement therapy (HRT)
- IV drip therapies
- Doctor home services ($100/month subscription model)

### Cosmetic & Surgical Services
- Non-surgical: Botox, fillers, BBLs
- Surgical: Breast implants, liposuction (Lipo 360/body contouring), fat transfer, tummy tucks
