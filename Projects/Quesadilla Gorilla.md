## Company Info
Quesadilla Gorilla is the fastest-growing quesadilla company in the nation, founded in 2013 by Miguel Reyes and Mikayla Reyes. Named the 3rd Fastest Growing Inner City Business in America by Fortune Magazine (2019). The brand is built on "Peace, Love, and Dillas" philosophy emphasizing family, community involvement, and social responsibility at each location.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Fastest-growing quesadilla company nationally
- Founded on "Peace, Love, and Dillas" core values
- Unique, customizable quesadillas with authentic family recipes
- House-made salsas and sauces
- Strong community involvement and local franchisee partnerships
- App-based loyalty program and rewards

- Young, vibrant brand culture

## Key Campaigns & Strategies
### Grand Opening Campaign
**Goal:** Launch new location with maximum visibility and generate buzz

**Shot List for Video Production:**
1. **Opening/Closing Shots** — Sunrise/sunset exterior, signage, branding
2. **Building Wide Shots** — Full structure angles, entrance with traffic
3. **Logo & Branding** — Close-ups of logo, branded elements, uniforms
4. **Kitchen Footage** — Wide and tight shots of food prep, ingredient selection, quesadilla assembly, cheese pulls (detail shots)
5. **Bar Footage** — Bartender craft shots, signature drink preparation
6. **Food Plating** — High-detail shots of finished dishes, quesadillas, plated items
7. **People Action** — Servers delivering food, customer reactions, cheers moments, general atmosphere
8. **Interview Footage** — Founders/operations team answers to franchise sales questions, customer testimonials

**Interview Questions for Franchise Sales Focus:**
- Why should someone start a Quesadilla Gorilla franchise?
- What separates Quesadilla Gorilla from another Mexican restaurant?
- What's the first step someone should take if interested in franchising?
- How does Quesadilla Gorilla contribute to and engage with the local community?
- What unique experiences do you aim to provide for customers?

**Founders/Operations Team Questions (Franchise-Focused):**
1. What unique advantages does a Quesadilla Gorilla franchise offer compared to other food industry opportunities?
2. Can you elaborate on the support and training provided to franchisees for success?
3. Could you share success stories of current franchisees and Quesadilla Gorilla's contribution to their journey?
4. What is the vision for Quesadilla Gorilla's growth and expansion? How does a new franchisee fit in?

**Customer Interview Questions:**
1. What was your first impression of Quesadilla Gorilla?
2. Do you have a favorite menu item and what makes it stand out?
3. How would you describe your overall experience (ambiance, service, food quality)?
4. Would you recommend Quesadilla Gorilla to others?
