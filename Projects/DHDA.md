## Company Info
Las Vegas-based emotional healing and personal growth organization founded by Angelica Curiel. Started as a support group for young people and has grown into a healing and transformation community for all ages. Offers individual, couple, and family therapy sessions plus support groups focused on emotional healing and self-knowledge. Team is composed of young adults on their own healing journey. Provides services in Spanish, serving the Latino community. Safe, inclusive space for people to find tools to heal and grow.

## Brand Guidelines
- **Language**: Spanish-primary (all ad copy and content in Spanish)
- **Overall Vibe**: Clean, modern, therapeutic — emotionally grounded and personally disruptive
- **Goal Feeling**: "¿cómo saben lo que estoy sintiendo?" (How do they know what I'm feeling?)
- **Visual Style**: Texture and depth essential — light grain overlays, paper feel, layered warm/handcrafted elements
- **Color Palette**: Warm pinks, cream, sage, terracotta, mustard yellow, earthy neutral backgrounds
- **Typography**: Bold readable serif or sans-serif for hooks; lighter sans-serif for info/CTA. Feel: Human, not robotic
- **Imagery**: Organic line art, real photos of hands/faces/people in motion, stylized with texture, Hispanic-presenting people. No clinical stock photos or overly posed imagery
- **Content Structure**: Hook → Info → CTA for all pieces
- **Services Promoted**: Haz Las Paces Contigo workshops (relationships, parenting), Group therapy (Tuesdays 6:30 PM), Kids & Teens groups, Individual consultations
- **CTA Style**: "Agenda tu consulta gratuita" (Schedule your free consultation), "Primera sesión gratuita"

## Brand Archetype
**Primary:** Magician
**Secondary:** Sage

**Goal:** Facilitate emotional and spiritual transformation through safe, therapeutic spaces

**Strategy:** Create transformative experiences that help individuals recognize their inner power and capacity for change; position as guide in healing journey

**Fear:** Being perceived as superficial or ineffective in creating real transformation; losing credibility in mental health space

**Brand Voice:** Warm, inviting, empathetic, knowledgeable, spiritual but grounded; speaks in Spanish primarily

**Customers Feel:** Safe, understood, empowered, hopeful; recognized for their struggles and potential

## Origin Story
DHDA was initiated when a teenager who was particularly important to Angelica was going through a difficult situation. This personal experience inspired the creation of a teen support group to help other adolescents facing similar challenges. From this seed grew Dejando Huellas De Amor—a comprehensive healing and transformation organization serving all ages.

**Key Message:** "Un adolescente especial para mí estaba enfrentando una etapa complicada. De esa experiencia, nació nuestro grupo de apoyo para jóvenes, y con él, Dejando Huellas De Amor."
