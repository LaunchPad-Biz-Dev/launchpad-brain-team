### What Wylees Actually Does

**The school in one sentence:** Wylees is a tuition-free public charter middle school (grades 6–8) occupying 46,000 sq ft of the former Macy's at Boulevard Mall, teaching through project-based learning, student-designed electives, and civic engagement — not lectures and rows of desks.

**Grade levels:** 6–8 only. Goal is to eventually expand to include high school (stated by founder Mike Taack).

**Schedule:** 4-day school week, 8:15 AM – 4:00 PM. This is a major enrollment differentiator — parents with working schedules or kids who hate traditional structure respond strongly to this.

**Location / facility:**
- 3634 Maryland Parkway, Suite 300, Las Vegas NV 89169 (inside Boulevard Mall, third floor of former Macy's)
- 46,000 sq ft — open, bright, deliberately un-school-like
- Windows face the northern Las Vegas Strip
- Security-controlled ground-floor entrance — no direct mall connection (important for parent safety concerns)
- Accessible via ground-floor side doors
- Described by founder as "like Hogwarts" — feels unlike any school students have attended

**Core learning model — 4 pillars:**
1. Project-Based Learning (PBL) — real projects co-created with students and families, tied to community problems
2. Service Learning and Civic Engagement — students create tangible impact in their communities
3. Personalized Learning Pathways (PLPs) — each student gets an individualized academic journey; mastery-based portfolio assessment, not traditional grades/credits
4. Student Agency / "Exploration" elective — students literally tell the school what they want to study; teachers help them plot a course to their goals

**Signature programs and features (confirmed):**
- **Hydroponics lab** — the undisputed hero visual asset; students grow plants and herbs indoors; founder has plans for a full wall of hydroponic plants; functions as both science curriculum AND food-production education ("bigger-picture look at food production" per Taack)
- **"Water to Table" Pathway** — the formal program name for the hydroponics/food education pathway (confirmed on the school's What-Is-WYLEES page)
- **Explorations elective** — student-proposed study areas; designed by Taack himself during his CCSD years, now the school's signature
- **Personalized Learning Pathways** — mastery portfolio track, not course-completion credits
- **Free lunch program** — temporary sack lunches initially; full kitchen planned
- **Podcast studio** — planned/in progress (referenced in press coverage)

**NOTE on welding:** The May 2026 shoot plan referenced welding as a trade program. No welding or dedicated trades program was confirmed on the Wylees website or in any press coverage as of this research date. The "Water to Table" hydroponics pathway is the confirmed hands-on/career pathway. ### Competitive Landscape

Las Vegas charter school landscape for middle-grade students (grades 6–8). Wylees competes with these schools for enrollment in the same age band.

**1. Explore Academy — Las Vegas (SPCSA-authorized)**
- Grades: 6–12
- Enrollment trajectory: 150 → 198 → 275 → 285 (2020–2024); approved cap 450 students
- Focus: STEM-leaning, project-based elements
- Positioned as college-prep alternative
- Differentiation gap vs. Wylees: Explore is larger and more established but less community-embedded; Wylees wins on uniqueness of environment and student agency

**2. Young Women's Leadership Academy of Las Vegas**
- Grades: 6–12 (ramping up)
- Population: 100% female enrollment (any student may apply; curriculum geared toward girls)
- Not a direct competitor for students who don't self-select into a single-gender environment
- Mentions: actively enrolling 6th–10th grade

**3. Legacy Traditional School — Southwest Las Vegas**
- K–8, tuition-free charter
- More traditional/structured model — direct contrast to Wylees' flexible approach
- Parents who want rigor and structure choose Legacy; parents who want flexibility choose Wylees
- Wylees should position against this by leaning into flexibility and student ownership

**4. Nevada Learning Academy at CCSD (online)**
- Teacher-led online instruction; PBL elements
- Not a physical school — competes for the "flexible schedule" parent who might consider it
- Wylees differentiator: community, physical space, the hydroponics/hands-on experience

**5. CCSD Traditional Middle Schools (broad competition)**
- The biggest "competitor" is inertia — families defaulting to their zoned CCSD middle school
- Wylees' enrollment messaging needs to give parents permission to choose a different path ("you can switch, here's how")

**Positioning summary:** Wylees is strongest vs. the field on: (1) physical environment (mall school is genuinely memorable), (2) 4-day week, (3) student agency / Explorations elective, (4) hydroponics and hands-on learning, (5) founder credibility (former CCSD teacher who knows the system from inside). Weakest on: (1) track record (opened August 2025 — no graduation class yet), (2) name recognition vs. established schools.

### Enrollment Funnel

How a family moves from awareness to enrolled student:

**Stage 1 — Awareness** (Social + Paid ads)
- Touch points: Facebook organic post / paid ad, Instagram reel, TikTok video, word-of-mouth from neighbor
- Goal: stop the scroll, make them say "wait, what is this?"
- Best content: the hydroponics lab, the "school inside a mall," 4-day week callout, tuition-free
- Winning hook: "There's a school inside the Boulevard Mall and your kid gets Fridays off"

**Stage 2 — Consideration** (Website visit + interest form)
- Touch point: wylees.org/now-enrolling
- CTA: Interest form (name, email, phone, grade, zip, language preference)
- Key messaging at this stage: safety, outcomes, "is it a real school" reassurance, free lunch
- Funnel gap: no confirmed open house dates found — this is a high-converting event that should be visible on social

**Stage 3 — Application / Lottery**
- Students apply through enrollment portal
- Lottery system (SPCSA schools use random selection when oversubscribed)
- Enrollment and Lottery Policy 2026–27 exists as a Google Drive document — link on their site
- Key action: confirm WYLEES 2026–27 lottery date and push it hard on social before the deadline

**Stage 4 — Enrollment confirmation**
- Families accept their spot
- Orientation / summer institute (school runs a "Summer Institute" — referenced on FB content)

**Social media's role in this funnel:**
- Organic posts (IG/FB) → Stage 1 awareness, warm audience for retargeting
- Paid Meta ads (starting June 2026, $1.5K/mo) → Stage 1 cold awareness in target ZIP codes
- Retargeting pool: website visitors + video viewers → Stage 2 consideration push
- Enrollment-urgency content → Stage 3 application push (seasonal)
