## Brand Guidelines
**Source:** [BWU Brand Kit in Notion](https://www.notion.so/1bab01f77d6d8194a75af2a5bbd23a2c)

### Brand Essence
- **Archetype:** The Creator — innovative, expressive, motivated by new ideas
- **Tone:** Confident, friendly, resourceful — like a knowledgeable partner in your corner
- **Values:** Reliability, Creativity, Collaboration, Community, Integrity
- **Inspiration:** Canva (empowering language), Printful (professional), Apple (sleek UX)

### Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Red #e82e29 (primary accent/CTAs), Gray #656464 (secondary/backgrounds), White #FFFFFF (main background), Black #000000 (headlines/text)
- **Typography:** Montserrat/Poppins (headlines), Lato/Merriweather (body)
- **Photography:** Bright, clean shots of real people, behind-the-scenes print processes, diverse subjects
- **Style:** Simple, uncluttered — let product images and brand colors take center stage

### Target Audience
1. Small & medium businesses (local shops, growing brands)
2. Event planners, schools, nonprofits, churches (English/Spanish)
3. Corporations & franchises needing e-commerce integrations

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Educate** — printing process, file prep tips, behind-the-scenes
2. **Inspire** — dream big, if you can imagine it BWU can print it
3. **Showcase** — final products, success stories, quality difference

### Taglines
- "Turning Ideas into Impressions"
- "Print with Purpose, Create with Confidence"
- "Your Vision, Our Craft"

### Do's & Don'ts
**Do:** Emphasize creativity and collaboration, share behind-the-scenes content, keep color usage consistent, celebrate local LV and bilingual capabilities
**Don't:** Use low-quality stock images, be overly pushy or gimmicky, crowd designs with too much text, neglect Spanish-speaking audiences

## Content & Marketing Strategy

### Brand Positioning
- **Core Value:** "Your Vision, Our Craft" — empowering entrepreneurs and businesses
- **Tagline Options:**
  - "Turning Ideas into Impressions"
  - "Print with Purpose, Create with Confidence"
- **Tone:** Friendly, professional, supportive — like a knowledgeable partner in your corner
- **Approach:** Customer-facing benefits, not just products

### Video Content Strategy

#### Interview/Talking Head Videos
- **Format:** Colloquial, engaging responses from team members
- **Focus:** Service showcases + brand personality
- **Example Question:** "Can you describe a time when you went the extra mile to show commitment to excellent service?"
- **Best For:** Building trust, showing behind-the-scenes, highlighting quality difference

#### Promotional Scripts (Generic Format)

**Promo 1: 10 Full Color Print Shirts for $135**
- Scene: Person wearing vibrant custom-printed shirt, high-energy vibe
- Message: Customize options, perfect for events/teams/personal use
- CTA: Order now, wear brand with pride
- Platform: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok

**Promo 2: 10 Full Color Print Snapbacks for $119**
- Scene: Person wearing custom snapback, stylish setting
- Message: Perfect for promotions, teams, unique gifts
- CTA: Visit website, "cap off your style"
- Platform: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok

**Promo 3: Combo (Shirts + Snapbacks)**
- Both products shown, celebratory group setting
- Message: Mix & match, maximum impact
- CTA: Visit website, place order

### Landing Page Strategy

**Headshot Happy Hour Campaign**
- **Goal:** Capture leads interested in custom business cards + print items
- **Hook:** "Turn Your Fresh Headshot into Stunning Business Cards"
- **Alternative:** "New Headshots, New Business Cards"
- **Partner:** Launchpad Productions (headshot shoot) + Brand With Us (card printing)
- **Form Fields:** Name, email, phone, card quantity, design preferences
- **Thank You Page:** Follow-up messaging on value of professional cards, next steps

### Inquiry Form Strategy

**Custom Print Order Form**
- **Purpose:** Capture details for banners, signs, trade show materials (NOT apparel)
- **Key Questions:** Design upload, quantity, size specifications, deadline, budget
- **Removed Questions:** Clothing sizes (apparel handled separately)
- **Flow:** Progressive disclosure based on product type
