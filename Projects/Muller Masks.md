## Company Info
US-manufactured protective mask company. The Muller Mask features a four-layer filtration system providing high filtration efficiency with easier breathing. Each mask has a flexible nose piece insert and head straps for a tight seal. Sold in boxes of 20 individually wrapped masks. Offers custom logo options for large orders (1,500+). Focused on serving individuals and occupations requiring high protection from airborne particles and contaminants.

## Brand Guidelines
**Source:** [Muller Masks Brand Overview in Notion](https://www.notion.so/306b01f77d6d8016aca3ca072140fe41)

### Brand Identity
- **Full Name:** Muller Construction Manufacturing (CMMC) — Muller Masks is the mask division
- **Archetype:** Everyday Man (primary) — grounded, practical, reliable. Hero (secondary) — duty-driven protector
- **Tone:** Confident, direct, professional, minimal, industrial, structured
- **Supporting Tone:** Engineer mindset — precise, certified, tested, controlled
- **Inspiration:** Milwaukee Tool, DeWalt — work-ready durability meets professional-grade credibility
- **Gold Narrative:** "The Gold Standard of Protection" — gold signals authority and standard, not decoration

### Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Gold #FAC63D (emphasis/accents), Black #231F20 (heavy base), Dark Gray #333333, Deep Navy #04203E
- **Typography:** Print: Georgia. Digital Headlines: Playfair Display or Merriweather. Body: Inter
- **Photography:** Metallic textures, close-up product details, industrial lighting. NO medical blue tones, healthcare teal, or consumer white branding.
- **Design Rules:** Heavy black base, gold for emphasis, clean structured layouts, no color deviations

### Positioning
For institutions, PPE distributors, resellers, construction firms, and disaster response organizations. NIOSH certified, American made N95 respirators. Competes with 3M on reliability, compliance, and direct manufacturer access — not on price.

### Target Market
1. PPE distributors and resellers
2. Construction and demolition companies
3. Disaster recovery and emergency response organizations
4. Specialized healthcare facilities
**Decision Makers:** Operations Directors, Procurement Managers, Supply Chain Directors, Safety Compliance Officers

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Work Ready Protection** — NIOSH certified, secure seal, real-world conditions
2. **Engineered and Certified** — tested, compliant, controlled, USA manufactured
3. **Bulk Built** — minimum 5K units, 30-35K weekly capacity
4. **American Manufacturing** — Las Vegas produced, supply chain control
5. **Certified Business Partner** — minority enterprise certified, supplier diversity eligible

### Do's & Don'ts
**Do:** Lead with quality and capability, use short/strong/clear copy, maintain industrial protection positioning
**Don't:** Use emotional pandemic fear messaging, discount-driven copy, political tone, over-explaining, consumer wellness branding
