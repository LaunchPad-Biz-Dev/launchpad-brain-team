# HombreX

## Status
**Active**

## Industry
Telemedicine / Men's Health (Hair Loss, ED, Weight Loss)

## Website
https://hombrex.com

## Service Model
Virtual/Telehealth with compounded medications

## Services & Products

### Core Treatment Areas
1. **Hair Loss Treatment** — Prescription solutions for male pattern baldness
2. **Erectile Dysfunction (ED) Treatment** — Prescription and topical solutions
3. **Weight Loss Treatment** — Compounded semaglutide (similar to Ozempic); similar efficacy to major brands at competitive pricing

### Target Market & Expansion
- **Primary Market:** Nevada (current operations)
- **Expansion States:** California, New Mexico, Arizona, Texas, Florida, New York
- **Secondary Market:** 55-60% of weight loss treatments target women
- **Primary Focus:** Men (particularly Hispanic men)

## Brand Guidelines

### Brand Archetypes
- **Primary:** Hero (empowers clients to overcome health challenges, inspires confidence)
- **Secondary:** Guy/Girl Next Door (relatable, authentic, trustworthy, approachable)

### Brand Voice & Tone
- **Emotional Register:** Empathetic and educational
- **Tone:** Upbeat, optimistic, hopeful
- **Communication Style:** Friendly and casual (like two equals talking); balance between legitimacy and approachability
- **Approach:** Educational without being preachy or condescending; address emotional pain points authentically
- **Cultural Sensitivity:** Acknowledge and respect cultural barriers men (especially Hispanic men) face discussing these topics; no stereotyping or condescension

### Content Format Preferences
- **Primary Format:** Talking head videos (founder/clinician speaking directly to audience)
- **Strategy:** Create longer talking head content, then clip into shorter digestible social media clips
- **Focus:** Pain point-driven content, educational content

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
- **Competitive Pricing:** Among the lowest pricing in the market for similar treatments (versus HIMS, Roman, other telemedicine platforms)
- **Flexibility:** Willing to work with clients if pricing is an issue
- **Cultural Alignment:** First major telemedicine player offering these services in Spanish at scale on national level
- **Cultural Competency:** Addresses specific barriers Hispanic and Latin American men face (family stigma, shame, cultural conservatism)

## Target Audience Persona
- **Primary:** Men, 30-60+, particularly Hispanic/Latino men
- **Secondary:** Women (weight loss products, 55-60% of that segment)
- **Geographic:** Initially Nevada, expanding nationally
- **Pain Points:** Hair loss causing self-consciousness; ED causing relationship strain; weight loss for health and appearance; cultural taboos preventing seeking treatment

## Business Metrics & Goals
- **Short-term Goal:** Acquire 2,000-3,000 clients by end of year
- **Product Evolution:** Moving toward virtual product offerings (beyond medication/treatment)
- **Sales Timeline:** Aggressive but realistic; rush to sales while maintaining credibility

## Marketing Strategy

### Content Creation Approach
1. **Talking Head Videos**
   - Develop scripts addressing key pain points (hair loss, ED, weight loss)
   - Clinician/founder as primary speaker
   - Clipping strategy: Long-form content → 15-30 second social clips

2. **Content Themes**
   - Pain points (emotional vulnerability, cultural shame)
   - Educational information (how treatments work, efficacy)
   - Cultural barriers and how HombreX addresses them
   - Authenticity and relatability

3. **Graphic Design & Ad Copy**
   - Hook (emotional pain point) + Information (solution) + CTA (call to action)
   - Culturally relevant imagery and language
   - Spanish language content capabilities

4. **Platform Strategy**
   - Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
   - TikTok
   - Google Ads
   - Potentially YouTube (for longer-form talking head content)

### CRM & Lead Generation
- Lead capture with: First name, last name, email, phone number + 2-3 qualifying questions
- GoHighLevel white-labeled CRM for lead nurturing and conversion
- High LTV customer model (ongoing medication/treatment subscriptions)

## Key Decisions & Positioning
- Focus on authentic, empathetic messaging that addresses cultural barriers
- No "bro culture" or stereotyping; respectful treatment of sensitive health topics
- Position as accessible, affordable alternative to competitors
- Leverage Spanish language and cultural expertise as competitive advantage
- Balance aggressive sales goals with trust-building through education
