## Company Info
Arby's only food truck in the entire system, operated by DRG (Diversified Restaurant Group) in Las Vegas. Approximately 35 feet end to end with a custom Vegas-themed wrap featuring an Arby's poker chip and "Viva Las Arby's" branding with 10 LED bulbs. Operates like a restaurant throughout the day, popping up at various locations. Available for weddings, holiday parties, school lunches, and fundraisers. Serves the classic Arby's menu.

- **Tagline**: "We Have The Meats"

  - Fire (Red): #F50023 (PMS 2035)
  - Marinade Maroon: #500619 (PMS 7421)
  - Butcher Paper Beige: #FCED2 (PMS 9184)
  - Charcoal Black: #000000
  - White: #FFFFFF
- **Typography**: Custom font family of 5 bespoke typefaces unique to Arby's:
  - Arby's Chuck — heavy sans serif with sign painter influences (headlines)
  - Arby's Shortrib — bold condensed, reminiscent of old butcher shop signage (headlines)
  - Arby's Flank — display font
  - Arby's Sirloin — body/versatile (+ italic variant)
  - Arby's Sauce — script accent font
- **Typography Rules**: Fonts are proprietary and owned by Arby's. Meant to be paired together to evoke butcher shop craft and detail. Not to be disseminated publicly.
- **Photography Style**: Rooted in analog quality — authentic and real, slight grain reminiscent of film photography. Natural approach, never polished, cold, sterile, or high contrast. Food shot in full focus to emphasize quality and detail. Unique style inspires hunger through craveable closeups, tactile textures, and authentic imperfections.
- **Design Direction**: Captures the spirit of the butcher shop through carefully considered typography, bold red-dominant compositions, and meat-forward imagery

## Media Day Production (July 2025)

### Goal & Overview
Full-day video content shoot designed to stockpile short-form video assets for Gen-Z audiences. Content is fun, absurd, comedic with physical humor and visual hooks. Focus on viral, highly shareable content across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.

### Skit Content Concepts (8 Total)

#### 1. "Fall Into Flavor" (Skateboard Skit)
- **Setup:** POV - Person on skateboard trips but catches themselves, keeps falling
- **Action:** Multiple falling scenarios → person ends up at Arby's truck → "accidentally" gets served food
- **Payoff:** Person eating satisfied, wide shot of truck
- **Tone:** Absurd, comedic, unexpected salvation
- **Runtime:** 15-30 seconds

#### 2. "Greatest Transition Ever" (Throw & Catch)
- **POV Concept:** Something gets thrown → pans to another person waiting → someone runs to give Arby's burger
- **Visual:** Cinematic truck shots (back, sides, front, inside, drone shot if available)
- **Execution:** Smooth transition from chaos to burger delivery
- **Tone:** Satisfying, smooth, visually impressive
- **Runtime:** 15-30 seconds

#### 3. "Fit Check Gone Arby's" (Almost Hit Scenario)
- **POV Setup:** "You almost get ran over"
- **Action:** Person doing "fit check" (showing off outfit), truck passes by, person grabs camera to catch license plate
- **Payoff:** Cinematic food truck sequence (back → sides → front → inside → possibly drone)
- **Tone:** Humorous, self-aware, unexpected pivot to product
- **Runtime:** 20-30 seconds

#### 4. "The Box Trend" (Hit with Object)
- **Setup:** Someone gets hit in head with ball/box
- **Response:** Person responds, "Did you think that box was gonna hit me?"
- **Punchline:** "The only thing that's gonna hit is this meat in your mouth. ARBYS WE HAVE THE MEAT"
- **Tone:** Punchy, unexpected punchline, product centric
- **Runtime:** 15-20 seconds

#### 5. "Durability Challenge" (Burger Comparison)
- **Setup:** Side-by-side comparison, shot in front of food truck
- **Regular Burger Tests:**
  - Water test - soak the burger
  - Rip test - rip the burger apart
  - Slam test - destroy it
- **Arby's Burger Tests:**
  - Water test - drop water behind burger (stays intact)
  - Rip test - pretend it's indestructible
  - Slam test - "slam" to table, cinematic reaction shot
- **Tone:** Educational comparison, quality showcase
- **Runtime:** 30-45 seconds

#### 6. "Run Over Skit" (Overlay Effect)
- **Setup:** Person giving review of burger, truck visible coming from road in background
- **Action:** Truck passes by, person "runs through" (overlay effect, shot separately)
- **Execution:** Shoot review and truck footage separately, composite in edit
- **Tone:** Visual trick, humorous framing
- **Runtime:** 15-20 seconds

#### 7. "GTA 5 Style" (Gaming Reference)
- **Setup:** Person wearing Insta 360 (or overhead shot), "spawning" like video game
- **Action:** Subtle movements (jump, jump, walk), person struggles to sit in Arby's truck setting
- **Movement:** Circles truck to find seating
- **Tone:** Gaming reference, absurd physical comedy
- **Runtime:** 15-30 seconds

#### 8. "Real Review (Staged)" (Testimonial)
- **Setup:** Person strapped in chair with spatula-wielding team around them
- **Content:** Person gives enthusiastic review of Arby's food truck
- **Script Example:** "Arby's food truck is the best. So convenient. [More specific praise]"
- **Tone:** Humorous, over-the-top commitment to review, authentic-feeling
- **Runtime:** 15-30 seconds

### B-Roll & Supplementary Content
- **Cinematic food shots:** Close-ups of sandwiches, fries, sauces
- **Truck exterior:** Vegas wrap, LED lights, food truck setup
- **Truck interior:** Cooking process, staff preparation
- **Process footage:** Sandwich assembly, food plating
- **Environmental:** Crowd reactions, event setup, customer interactions

### Shoot Specifications

**Equipment Needs:**
- Primary camera (4K capable)
- Insta 360 camera (for GTA-style content)
- Drone (if available, for aerial truck shots, transitions)
- Tripod(s)
- Slow-motion capable camera for dramatic moments

**Editing Specifications:**
- Quick cuts, pacing optimized for TikTok/Reels (15-30 second primary format)
- Trending audio integration (trendjacking when applicable)
- Visual effects (overlays, transitions, text overlays)
- Lower-third graphics with Arby's branding
- Film-grain analog aesthetic (per brand guidelines)

### Distribution Strategy

**Primary Platforms:**
- **TikTok:** Trending format, quick-cut heavy, audio-focused, algorithm boost
- **Instagram Reels:** 15-30 seconds, high engagement, shareable format
- **YouTube Shorts:** Same format, additional algorithm reach
- **Facebook:** Longer versions, community engagement

**Content Calendar:**
- **Frequency:** 4-5 posts per week (minimum)
- **Timing:** Peak engagement times for Gen-Z (evening, late night)
- **Variety:** Mix skit types, keep audience engaged with different formats
- **Hashtags:** Trending Gen-Z hashtags, food/comedy, local/regional, Arby's official
