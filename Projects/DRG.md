## Company Info
Diversified Restaurant Group franchises approximately 360 Taco Bell and Arby's units across Nevada, Northern California, Southern California, Alaska, and Kansas City. Founded in 2012 by David Grieve and SG Ellison, starting with 30 Taco Bell units — now a 360+ unit, multi-brand operation. QSR Magazine's 2025 Franchisee of the Year. Innovations include themed Cantinas, Live Mas Cafe, Go Mobile restaurants, AI drive-thru ordering, EV charging stations, and Arby's only food truck.
