# RB Wellness Med Spa

## Client Overview
**Brand Name:** RB Wellness Clinic & Med Spa
**Location:** Las Vegas, NV
**Leader:** Leanne Lee
**Industry:** Medical Spa, Wellness & Aesthetic Services
**Website:** https://rbwellnessmedspa.com/

## Business Model
- High-end luxury wellness experiences
- **Focus:** Packages and memberships (primary sales strategy)
- Target market: Men and women aged 30+, high-income earners willing to invest in health and wellness

## Brand Positioning

### Brand Archetypes
- **Primary:** Lover (intimate, pampering, personal connection)
- **Secondary:** Royalty (luxury, exclusivity, premium positioning)

### Brand Voice & Aesthetic
- **Style:** LaserAway/Buzzfeed/Celebrity-Style, Hollywood luxury
- **Vibe:** Intimate, pampering, luxe, experiential
- **Visual Theme:** Music video, film, aesthetic, cinema—red carpet energy
- **Environment:** Aesthetically pleasing space designed as an experience, not just a service location

### Core Values
- Excellence in service and care
- Personalization of every experience
- Luxury and indulgence
- Integrity and transparency
- Community building with clients

## Services Offered
1. **IV Infusion Therapy** (including Mobile IV)
   - Rehydration, vitamin drips, glutathione skin glow, NAD+ therapy

2. **Non-Surgical Procedures**
   - Microneedling, mesotherapy, hair restoration, facial toning, tightening

3. **Laser Therapy & Rejuvenation**
   - Rejuran (skin booster—only clinic in Nevada offering this)
   - Tattoo removal, laser hair removal
   - Advanced skin boosters

4. **Medical Weight Loss & Laser Sculpting**
   - Laser Lipo 360

5. **Laser Hair Removal**
   - Full body, legs, intimate areas (vajacial)

6. **PRP Hair Restoration**
   - Comprehensive treatment with professional care

## Target Audience
- **Demographics:** Men and women 30+, high-income professionals
- **Psychographics:** Luxury seekers, wellness-focused, willing to invest in self-care
- **Geographic:** Las Vegas and surrounding areas

## Marketing Strategy

### Content Production
- 5 primary promotional videos (30 sec–1 min)
- Purpose: Organic social posting + paid ads
- Style: High-production, cinematic, luxury aesthetic
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, YouTube

### Video Content Themes
1. **Laser Lipo 360** — VIP experience with wine/champagne, Netflix, ambient lighting
2. **PRP Hair Restoration** — Focus on customer service, safe procedures, gift bag experience
3. **Vaginal Laser Hair Removal** — Prepping, procedure, numbing, vajacial finale
4. **Leg Laser Hair Removal** — Complete preparation and treatment protocol
5. **Rejuran Skin Booster** — Unique offering (only in Nevada), closing with gift bags

### Marketing Initiatives
- Influencer partnerships (UGC) for brand awareness
- Focus on local Las Vegas/regional influencers
- Lead form follow-ups via SMS and email
- Instagram Story highlights with service education and FAQs

### Ad Copy Structure
- Headline/Hook
- Informative content (not overcrowded)
- CTA aligned with caption

## Design Briefs & Brand Assets
- Brand Kit creation in progress
- Graphic Design Requests: 10+ carousel and single graphics
- Color palette similar to brand, modern aesthetic, minimal black (avoid dark/moody feel)
- Typography: Modern, on-brand fonts (avoid extremes)

## Key Messaging
- Luxury experience, not just a service
- Pampering and self-care as investment
- Professional, safe, and personalized treatments
- Memberships and packages as value proposition
