## Company Info
MH Collisions is a local auto body and collision repair shop with 8+ years of experience. They specialize in full collision and impact repair. Current expansion plans include opening additional locations in Arizona and California. The team has three main departments: Body, Prepping, and Paint. They offer comprehensive services including deductible coverage, towing, rental car provision, and legal contact references for clients.

## Brand Identity & Voice
- **Tone:** Raw, urban, real, and authentic (avoid stereotypical/corny branding)
- **Brand Core:** Car culture enthusiast shop; relationship-focused approach
- **Key Differentiators:**
  - Quality of work (relationship-centric, pay deductible for clients to keep them happy)
  - City-wide service (out-of-town accidents welcome)
  - Expansion mindset (Texas, California, Arizona)
  - Transparency in communication
  - Quick turnaround times
  - Relationship building
