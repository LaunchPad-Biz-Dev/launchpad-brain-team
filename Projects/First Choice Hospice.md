## Company Info

First Choice Hospice provides compassionate end-of-life care and hospice services. The organization focuses on bringing hospital-level care directly to patients' homes, emphasizing professional expertise combined with emotional and spiritual support for patients and families.

### Key Values
- Compassion + Professional Expertise
- Family-focused care
- Comfort and dignity in final days
- Complete end-of-life support (medical + logistics)

## Services Offered

### Core Hospice Services
- **Nursing Care:** 24/7 round-the-clock nursing support
- **Medical Management:** Pain control, symptom management, medication management
- **Interdisciplinary Team:**
  - Nurses
  - Doctors
  - Social Workers
  - Chaplains (spiritual support)
- **Daily Needs Management:** Bathing, grooming, personal care
- **Comfort & Dignity:** Focus on comfort care over curative treatment

### Financial Coverage
- **Medicare Coverage:** Fully covered (for qualifying patients)
  - No copays
  - No out-of-pocket expenses
  - Medicare Part A qualifies automatically
- **Veterans Benefits:** VA covers hospice care at no cost
- **Help Applying:** If you don't have Medicare Part A, First Choice Hospice assists with application process

### End-of-Life Services
- Death certificate issuance
- Funeral home coordination
- Family support during and after patient's passing
- Post-bereavement follow-up support

### Eligibility Requirements
- **Medical:** Diagnosis of life-limiting illness with prognosis of 6 months or less (confirmed by physician)
- **Coverage:** Medicare Part A (automatic qualification) OR assistance with application
- **Timeline:** Available for patients at any point in terminal diagnosis

## Key Messaging Insights

### Pain Points Addressed
1. **Financial Anxiety:** Script emphasizes 100% Medicare coverage, no hidden costs
2. **Fear of Unknown:** Scripts explain exactly what to expect (24/7 support, team composition)
3. **Guilt/Burden:** Messaging focuses on relief - "we handle the logistics"
4. **Spiritual/Emotional Needs:** Chaplains specifically mentioned alongside medical team
5. **Logistical Overwhelm:** Death certificates, funeral coordination are covered

### Conversion Strategy
- **Educational First:** Scripts teach before selling
- **Relief-Focused:** Emphasize burden lifted, not service sold
- **Family-Centered:** All copy addresses "you and your family"
- **Professional Authority:** Emphasize team credentials and expertise
- **No Pressure:** Tone is supportive, not urgent
