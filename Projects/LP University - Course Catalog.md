## CHAPTER 1: STRATEGY & PLANNING
**Tier:** Core Course (Low-ticket, $97-$147)
**Instructor:** Anthony Mendoza (CEO, LaunchPad)
**Duration:** 7 modules, 3-5 hours total video
**Audience:** Business owners, operators, new hires, junior strategists, anyone overwhelmed by marketing
**Prerequisite:** None (entry-level to this framework)
**Certification Available:** No (but completion enables enrollment in Tier 2/3)

### Course Overview
Foundation of everything LaunchPad teaches. Students learn to build a quarterly marketing plan that creates focus and eliminates chaos. Not tactics. Strategy. The LaunchPad way.

**Core Philosophy:** Better Content → Better Leads → Better Systems. If any piece is missing, growth breaks.

**Learning Outcomes:**
By the end, students can:
- Build a clear, realistic quarterly marketing plan
- Understand what matters vs what's noise
- Align content, ads, and systems under one plan
- Speak the same strategic language as LaunchPad
- Identify their audience with emotional + demographic depth
- Craft a single, focused message statement
- Define systems needed to convert leads into revenue
- Select 1-3 primary platforms (not spread thin across 10)

**Module Breakdown:**

### **Module 1: Quarterly Planning Setup & Goal Alignment (45 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Why quarterly planning beats monthly thinking
- The difference between business goals and marketing goals
- Why most marketing fails before it starts (no alignment)
- How to set goals that actually drive business

**Key Takeaway:** A quarterly plan is a decision-making framework, not a calendar.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Quarterly setup with clear business + marketing goals

---

### **Module 2: Choosing the Right Objective (30 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Four core marketing objectives (Awareness, Lead Gen, Conversion, Retention)
- How to identify your current bottleneck
- Why choosing all four kills momentum
- The power of focused execution

**Key Takeaway:** Focus creates results. Trying to do everything achieves nothing.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Primary objective selected with reasoning

---

### **Module 3: Audience First Thinking (50 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Beyond demographics: Understanding emotional state
- Real problems vs surface-level problems
- Why specificity creates clarity
- Brand archetype framework (Hero, Sage, Creator, Mentor, etc.)
- How your audience determines your messaging

**Key Takeaway:** Your customer is the hero. Your brand is the guide.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Audience profile + emotional transformation + archetype

---

### **Module 4: Messaging Strategy (45 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Creating a single, focused message statement
- Supporting angles that build depth without distraction
- Why consistency beats cleverness
- How to align messaging with goals + audience

**Key Takeaway:** One clear sentence + 2-3 supporting angles = authority

**Examples:**
- "This quarter, our marketing is about luxury real estate for busy business owners who want streamlined buying"
- "This quarter, our marketing is about LaunchPad University — educating on structured marketing systems"

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Core message statement + 3 supporting angles

---

### **Module 5: Systems & Metrics (40 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Why systems convert attention into revenue
- CRM as foundation (capture, nurture, convert)
- Lead forms, email, SMS sequences
- Success metrics vs vanity metrics
- What systems must exist this quarter

**Key Takeaway:** Marketing creates attention. Systems convert attention into money.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Systems audit + metrics selected

---

### **Module 6: Channel & Distribution Strategy (35 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Where your audience actually spends time
- Why dominance in one channel beats mediocrity in five
- Organic vs paid vs owned channels
- Platform-specific content requirements
- Resource allocation per platform

**Key Takeaway:** Pick 1-3 platforms, not all 10.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Channel selection + resource plan

---

### **Module 7: Quarterly Plan Alignment Check (30 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Reviewing all sections for execution readiness
- Ensuring goal alignment across all pieces
- Can we actually do this?
- Final sign-off process

**Key Takeaway:** Every piece must support the business goal. If it doesn't, remove it.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Completed quarterly plan, approved and ready to execute

## CHAPTER 2: CONTENT CREATION
**Tier:** Core Course (Low-ticket, $197-$297)
**Instructor:** [[Kyle Fuller]] (@vcy on TikTok/Instagram, 2.5M+ followers on TikTok)
**Duration:** 5 modules, 4-6 hours total video
**Audience:** Content creators, marketing teams, anyone creating social content, filmmakers, editors
**Prerequisite:** Chapter 1 recommended (understanding quarterly strategy helps contextualize content)
**Certification Available:** No (but completion enables Tier 2 "Content Creation Specialist" certification)

### Course Overview
Execute the strategy. Production workflow from research through posting. Not theory. Process. The repeatable system Kyle uses daily for personal brand (2.5M followers on TikTok) and client work at LaunchPad.

**Learning Outcomes:**
By the end, students can:
- Research content intentionally (reverse-engineer high-performers)
- Plan shoots efficiently (shot lists, briefs, timelines)
- Capture video with minimal equipment
- Edit for maximum impact (hook-first, pacing, platform optimization)
- Optimize for each platform (TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Stories)
- Produce consistent, engaging content on a sustainable schedule

---

### **Module 1: Pre-Production — Research (50 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Intentional vs mindless scrolling
- How to reverse-engineer why content works
- Pattern recognition in high-performing videos
- Adaptation framework (trends are transferable)
- Detailed breakdown framework

**Key Takeaway:** Understanding the "why" lets you adapt it to your message.

**Breakdown Elements:**
- Hook (why it stops the scroll)
- Structure (narrative arc)
- Camera work (angles, movement)
- Transitions (cuts, fades, wipes)
- Text/captions (placement, duration, function)
- Audio (music, VO, pacing)
- CTA (what does creator ask you to do?)
- Pacing (fast, slow, when it changes)

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Analysis of 5 high-performers + patterns identified + adaptation ideas

---

### **Module 2: Pre-Production — Planning (45 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Translating message into content concepts
- Shot list creation (what specific shots?)
- Content brief development (idea, why, emotional goal)
- Role assignment (who does what)
- Timeline and scheduling (how long, who, when)
- Location scouting

**Key Takeaway:** Bad planning = chaos on set. Good planning = efficient shoots and better creative.

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Content concept + emotional goal + full shot list + brief + timeline

---

### **Module 3: Production — Filming (55 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- Minimal equipment mindset (you don't need $5K gear)
- Essential equipment (lighting, sound, stability)
- Setting the scene (establishing shots)
- Capturing detail (close-ups, specific angles)
- Getting multiple takes (safety net approach)
- Audio best practices
- On-set professionalism and safety
- Directing talent

**Key Takeaway:** You don't need expensive equipment. You need good light, good sound, and intentionality.

**Equipment Recommendations:**
- Camera: iPhone 12+ or equivalent (or DSLR/mirrorless)
- Stabilization: Smartphone gimbal or simple tripod
- Audio: Lav mic or shotgun mic (don't skip this)
- Lighting: 2-3 simple lights or reflectors
- Backdrop: Clean, uncluttered

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Location scouting notes + equipment checklist + shot count + backup confirmation

---

### **Module 4: Post-Production — Editing (60 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- File organization and naming (critical for future you)
- Editing software basics (CapCut as primary, Premiere as secondary)
- Hook-first editing (strongest moment in first 2-3 seconds)
- Pacing and rhythm (when to cut fast, when to hold)
- Text overlays and captions (hierarchy, timing, readability)
- Color grading and consistency (create a "look")
- CTA placement (clear call to action)
- Export settings (format/quality per platform)

**Key Takeaway:** Editing is where content gets its power. First 3 seconds determine if viewers stay.

**Editing Structure:**
1. Hook (0-3 sec) — Strongest visual/sound
2. Story (3-30 sec) — Setup, problem, curiosity
3. Peak (Peak moment) — Emotional turning point or reveal
4. Resolution/CTA (Last 5-10 sec) — Close loop, ask for action

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Edited video file + captions/text + color grade reference + export settings per platform

---

### **Module 5: Platform Optimization (55 min)**
**What It Teaches:**
- TikTok/Reels specifics (fast, punchy, sound-on, algorithm)
- YouTube optimization (length, SEO, thumbnails, pattern interrupts)
- Facebook/LinkedIn (conversational, captions, slower pacing)
- Story formats (ephemeral, different aspect ratio)
- Hashtag strategy per platform
- Posting times and frequency
- Algorithm considerations

**Platform Deep-Dives:**

**TikTok/Instagram Reels:**
- Aspect: 9:16 (vertical)
- Length: 15-60 sec (sweet spot 15-30)
- Sound: Critical (trending audio)
- Hook: First 1 second
- Captions: ALL dialogue on screen
- Pacing: Fast cuts, interrupt every 2-3 sec
- CTA: Hook for follow, not always "buy"

**YouTube:**
- Aspect: 16:9 (landscape)
- Length: 8+ minutes (longer = algorithm favor)
- Thumbnail: Custom, high-contrast, face/emotion
- SEO: Title, description, tags
- Captions: Full transcription
- Interrupts: Every 15-30 sec (b-roll, text, visual change)
- CTA: End screen for subscribe, playlist, next video

**Facebook/LinkedIn:**
- Aspect: 1:1 or 4:5
- Length: 2-3 min optimal
- Sound: Many watch muted, captions essential
- Pacing: Slower, conversational
- Captions: Natural reading
- CTA: Link in comments, bio link
- Engagement: Questions, replies matter

**Worksheet Deliverable:** Platform-specific exports + descriptions + hashtag research + 30-day posting calendar
