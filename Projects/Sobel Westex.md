## Brand Guidelines — Travel + Leisure Home
- **Tagline**: "Experience Travel Comfort at Home"
- **Tone & Feel**: Inspiring, adventurous, aspirational — evokes the thrill of travel and discovery
- **Look**: Clean and modern with elements of wanderlust and adventure
- **Primary Colors**:
  - Explorer Blue: #005B96
  - Earthy Green: #7BA05B
  - Travel Beige: #D9CBA7
- **Typography**: Raleway (headings), Pacifico (accent highlights)
- **Imagery**: Stunning visuals of bedding in travel-inspired settings — cozy mountain cabins, beachside retreats, urban lofts
- **Design Direction**: Use adventure/discovery imagery, incorporate travel-themed elements subtly (maps, compasses), keep modern and clean, focus on aspirational
- **Creative Examples**: Ads use immersive storytelling; organic posts share travel tips alongside product features
- **Parent Company Socials**: @travelandleisure on IG, FB, Twitter, Pinterest, YouTube

## Brand Guidelines — Princess Luxury Home
- **Tagline**: "Sleep Like Royalty"
- **Tone & Feel**: Elegant, sophisticated, luxurious — brings the royal experience of Princess Cruise Lines into the home
- **Look**: Classic, refined, upscale — focus on premium materials and craftsmanship
- **Primary Colors**:
  - Royal Blue: #002E5D
  - Gold: #C5A300
  - Elegant White: #F0F0F0
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (headings — elegant serif), Lato (body text — clean sans-serif)
- **Imagery**: High-resolution bedding in luxurious, well-appointed rooms. Highlight textures and fine details to convey premium quality
- **Design Direction**: Sophisticated palette, elegant design elements, focus on fine details and textures, beautifully styled rooms suggesting high-end cruise experience
- **Parent Company Socials**: @princesscruises on IG, FB, Twitter, Pinterest, YouTube

## Brand Guidelines — Carnival Home
- **Tagline**: "Bring Cruise Comfort Home"
- **Tone & Feel**: Energetic, vibrant, joyful — brings the lively atmosphere of Carnival Cruise into the home, focuses on fun and relaxation
- **Look**: Bright, colorful, playful with a focus on comfort and joy
- **Primary Colors**:
  - Bright Blue: #0073CF
  - Carnival Red: #FF3D00
  - Sunshine Yellow: #FFD700
  - Pure White: #FFFFFF
- **Typography**: Montserrat Bold (headings — bold/playful), Open Sans (body text — clean sans-serif)
- **Imagery**: High-quality bedding photos in bright, well-lit rooms with nautical themes or beach accessories
- **Design Direction**: Vibrant colors, playful elements, high-energy and joyful imagery, highlight luxurious comfort replicating cruise experience at home
- **Parent Company Socials**: @carnival on IG, FB, Twitter, Pinterest, YouTube
