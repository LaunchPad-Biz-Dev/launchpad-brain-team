# Welding School of Nevada

## Client Overview
**Official Name:** Welding School of Nevada
**Location:** Nevada
**Leadership:** Greg Gilbert (Owner/Founder)
**Industry:** Vocational Education, Welding Training
**Website:** https://www.weldingschoolofnevada.com/

## Services & Offerings

### Core Program
- **Duration:** 10 weeks (intensive)
- **Hours:** 180-hour comprehensive course
- **Certification:** AWS SENSE Program certified
- **Outcome:** Career pathway entry into welding industry

### Welding Techniques Covered
- MIG (Metal Inert Gas) welding
- TIG (Tungsten Inert Gas) welding
- Stick welding
- Industry standards and best practices

### Program Advantage
- Hands-on, immersive industry training
- AWS SENSE Program standards (industry-recognized)
- Fast-track to employment
- Real-world challenge preparation

## Target Audience
- Career changers and first-time learners
- People seeking hands-on work
- High-earning potential seekers
- Those valuing job stability and industry demand
- Individuals looking for career growth in manufacturing/construction

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Tone
- **Direct and confident** (not overly playful)
- Professional yet motivational
- Action-oriented
- Honest about industry benefits

### Key Message Pillars
1. **Speed:** 10 weeks to certification
2. **Opportunity:** High-demand career path
3. **Quality:** Industry-recognized AWS training
4. **Support:** Comprehensive guidance through process
