## Company Info
Sin City Justicia is a criminal defense law firm based in Las Vegas. The firm is led by attorneys Ben and Alika who provide legal representation for clients facing criminal charges. They emphasize a client-centered approach focused on dedicated advocacy and understanding.

## Brand Voice & Messaging
- **Brand Theme:** "You Choose" — Emphasizes that clients have a choice in their legal representation
- **Core Message:** Sin City Justicia prioritizes clients as individuals, not case numbers
- **Tone:** Empathetic, understanding, committed, non-corporate
- **Key Differentiators:**
  - Personal attention and genuine care
  - Dedicated advocacy in legal battles
  - Clear communication without legal jargon
  - Passion and expertise combined
