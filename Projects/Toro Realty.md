## Campaign Strategy

### Lead Generation Campaigns (Facebook)
**Objective:** Recruit real estate agents by highlighting unique benefits and partnership with Toro Taxes database

**Ad Formats Developed:**
1. 8 graphic design concepts (4 English, 4 Spanish)
2. Targeted segments:
   - Currently licensed real estate agents (career advancement focus)
   - General population (career change / new opportunity focus)
3. Key messaging pillars:
   - Guaranteed warm leads
   - One-on-one coaching
   - Fastest-growing brand in the country
   - Family-oriented culture
   - Professional support and tools

**Lead Form Copy:**
- Headline: "Join TORO Realty's Elite Network!"
- Lead form questions: licensing status, current brokerage, what they're seeking in new brokerage
- Thank you page: "Welcome to the TORO Realty Family!"
- Follow-up messaging emphasizes family culture and genuine support
