# Sobel Westex Multi-Brand Campaign

## Status
**Active / Strategic Planning**

## Industry
Ecommerce / Luxury Linens / Travel & Hospitality

## Partner Brands
- **Carnival Home Collection** (Carnival Cruise Line)
- **Princess Luxury Bed** (Princess Cruise Lines)
- **Travel and Leisure Home** (Travel + Leisure Magazine)
- Norwegian Cruise Line (future)

## Company Info
Sobel Westex is a linen supplier that partners with major hospitality and cruise brands. They have created ecommerce stores selling the exact linens guests experience on:
- Cruise ships (Carnival, Princess, Norwegian)
- Resorts and hotels
- Branded collections (Travel + Leisure destination/resort lines)

**Sales Channels:** QR codes and sales links inside resorts/ships, plus marketing on brand partner websites.

**Launchpad's Role:** Drive ecommerce sales through social media and digital advertising (Meta Ads Manager, TikTok, Google, LinkedIn, and other platforms).

## Marketing Philosophy
**Content-First Approach:** Sell the experience, not just the product. Focus on brand archetypes and emotional connection rather than product specifications.

**Guiding Principle:** Nike sells the hero archetype and the tribe of winners, not the materials. Sobel Westex should sell the luxury vacation experience, the comfort of a cruise, the escape—not "best pillow ever."

## Q4 Campaign Strategy

### Core Concept
Holiday-themed, seasonal content that aligns each brand with specific seasonal moments and lifestyles:
- **Halloween (Oct):** Dress up your home; costume/party energy for Halloween-themed hospitality
- **Thanksgiving (Nov):** Family warmth, gathering, comfort, luxury at home
- **Christmas/Winter (Dec):** Gift the experience; festive luxury; holiday family moments

### Content Creation Approach
**Media Days:** Planned production shoots featuring:
1. **Mini commercials** with talent (actors/models) in lifestyle scenarios
2. **Product photography** (fresh photo shoots, not just website images)
3. **Motion video:** Panning shots of linens, product detail shots with cinematic quality
4. **Lifestyle shots:** Talent using products in seasonal, on-brand contexts

**Production Focus:**
- Quality over quantity (rich content for creative team to work with)
- Seasonal relevance (e.g., towels with hot tubs/spas in winter, not pools)
- Storytelling that connects to the brand archetype
- Diverse talent and authentic scenarios

**Deliverables to Creative Team:**
- High-quality lifestyle footage
- Product close-ups and detail shots
- Motion graphics candidates
- B-roll for editing team to remix into ads

### Brand-Specific Guidance

#### Carnival Home Collection
- **Brand Tagline:** "Fun for All - All for Fun"
- **Core Values:** Fun, social, participatory, spontaneous
- **Tone:** Playful, energetic, not serious
- **Brand Personality:** Beaming, positive, approachable
- **Guiding Principles:**
  - Promotes positive brand image
  - Maintains accurate funnel shape (Carnival logo/motif)
  - Maintains accurate funnel color (Carnival's signature color)
  - Avoids looking silly, juvenile, or tacky
  - Avoids party/booze cruise associations
  - Never alters brand colors (except B&W and 2-color options)
  - Never alters brand shapes

- **Q4 Approach:** Celebrate fun home moments, family gathering, bringing vacation joy into daily life
- **Content Themes:**
  - Family fun at home (movie nights, game nights on Carnival bedding)
  - Cozy gatherings during holidays
  - Gift-giving moments (Carnival products as gifts)

#### Princess Luxury Bed
- **Brand Focus:** Luxury, elegance, indulgence
- **Q4 Approach:** Sophisticated holiday entertaining, upscale relaxation
- **Content Themes:**
  - Elegant morning routines
  - Luxury entertaining (dinner parties, holiday gatherings)
  - Premium bedtime rituals
  - Regal, sophisticated holiday moments

#### Travel and Leisure Home
- **Brand Focus:** Destination-inspired, wanderlust, travel experiences
- **Collections:** Three destination collections + one resort collection
- **Q4 Approach:** Bring destinations home for the holidays; create travel-inspired escapes without leaving
- **Content Themes:**
  - Travel inspiration during holiday season
  - Exotic escapes (virtual travel through home products)
  - Destination-specific styling (Mediterranean, Asian, Tropical, etc.)
  - Holiday travel planning and preparation
