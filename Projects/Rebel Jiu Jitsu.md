## Company Info
Rebel Jiujitsu opened June 2021 and is **the only martial arts academy in Boulder City**. Founded by Daniel Fornasa, who relocated from Orange County, CA with his wife Ashley and two kids; he'd taught BJJ professionally for a decade, started in his garage, then opened the Buchanan Blvd space. Fornasa was a black belt at 20 and won the 2021 IBJJF Pan Championship. The academy grew past 50 students in its first year, hosted world champion Tainan Dalpra, and at one point described itself as operating in **both Henderson and Boulder City**. 4.7/5 across 31 Google reviews.

## Market Reality (researched 2026-09-05)
- **Boulder City pop. ~14,897. Median age 53.5.** 29.6% are 65+; only ~14% are under 15 (≈2,100 kids in the entire town). Median household income $74,757, but 22% of households clear $150k. Median home value $435,600.
- **Implication:** the in-town kids market is genuinely thin. Sustained growth requires pulling from Henderson (15–20 min away).
- **Henderson is saturated** — Gracie Barra Henderson, Cobrinha, 10th Planet, AE UNLTD, FTCC, Ground Zero, Bueno MMA, Flecha, Legacy. To pull a Henderson family past three closer gyms, Rebel needs a *reason*. Brand/culture is that reason — which is exactly why the aesthetic work is strategic, not cosmetic.
- **Boulder City is one of only two towns in Nevada where gambling is illegal** (the other is Panaca). Founded 1931 as a federal Hoover Dam workers' reservation, deliberately designed for "clean living" — gambling, liquor and prostitution all banned. Liquor legalized 1969; the gambling ban still stands. **A gym named REBEL sits in the one Nevada town that refused the thing Nevada is famous for.** Strong, true, ownable brand well — and it is natively Americana/retro, matching the aesthetic direction.
- **What parents actually screen for** (research): instructor quality first, then whether classes are structured vs. "a wild unorganized free-for-all," class size, and safety. Rebel's real differentiators map exactly onto these — so content should *demonstrate* them rather than claim them.

## The Actual Brand — from Anthony, 2026-09-05

**Critical distinction:** this is the community *at Rebel*, not the community *in Boulder City*. The town is where it happens, not the reason. Earlier drafts over-leaned on the town.

### Who actually walks in (Anthony's read)
Most guys don't start for fitness or self-defence — they start because they want to feel like a badass, and BJJ is the one where that's real and you don't get hit in the head. They want to say they fight as a hobby. Not a criticism, it's the market: Jonah Hill, Tom Hardy, Zuckerberg, Ashton Kutcher, Mario Lopez.

**The trap:** the customer wants the feeling; the brand can never name it. Jonah Hill on a podcast talking about beating people up = the failure mode. You give people the feeling by never mentioning it.

**The register:** grappling humour is relentlessly self-deprecating (Craig Jones's whole career). Taking yourself seriously marks you as an outsider. Also — the respected archetype is now the obsessive, not the meathead: Mikey Musumeci is a nerd who beats everyone on technical depth. **Depth reads as credibility now; strength doesn't.** That's the argument for instructional content.

**Tailwind:** BJJ is growing fast — CJI 1 & 2 landed, UFC has a BJJ division, comp programs opening everywhere, more professional paths for grapplers outside MMA.

### Operating principle — "Free Game"
Ref: https://free-game.virgilabloh.com/ — Virgil gave away lectures, templates, process; withholding keeps people out, access builds a following. This is already what Rebel does after class (everybody teaching everybody), so make it the rule: instructionals free, drills free, no "DM for info," no gated funnel.

### Why the reference brands work
- **Stüssy** — logo was Shawn's real signature; International Stüssy Tribe gave gear to genuine scene people per city. Membership, not product.
- **Vans** — built what skaters asked for (waffle sole, reinforced Old Skool sidewall). Skaters made Vans cool; Vans got out of the way.
- **Daisy Fresh** — evidence, not claim. Laundromat gym, kids sleeping on site, world champions. The bad lighting is the credibility.
- **Street X** — reframed the gi from equipment to garment.
- **Nike** — sells self-image back to you; "Just Do It" aimed at the amateur.
- **Shared mechanic:** none of them tell you what to feel. They hand you evidence and let you decide you belong.

### Content buckets (Anthony's framework, given to Nathan)
- **Entertaining 30%** — skits, trends. Reach.
- **Educational 40%** — 30–60s instructionals ("top 3 moves you need as a white belt"). Nathan is on board. Travels beyond Boulder City. Free Game in practice.
- **Empathetic 30%** — testimonials, community events, shared stories. Converts locally.
