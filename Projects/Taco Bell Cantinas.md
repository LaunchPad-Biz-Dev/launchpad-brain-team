## Brand Guidelines (Launchpad Strategy)
- **Brand Archetype**: Entertainer — fun, energetic, experience-driven
- **Overall Strategy**: Build off the Taco Bell Cantina brand as a whole rather than individual feel per location
- **Content Focus**: Drinks and product-focused, experiential, nightlife energy
- **Video Style**: POV shots of freezes walking through Cantinas, drink drops, talent shoots inside Cantinas (similar to Black Tap LV style)
- **Visual Inspiration References**: Happiest Hour (Dallas), Black Tap LV, Emilio's Las Vegas, Culinary Dropout
- **Long-Term Plan**: Individual pages for key Cantinas (Pacifica, Las Vegas, then lump the rest)

## Brand Identity Standards (from Taco Bell Brand Book Sept 2023)
- **Mission**: To inspire and enable the world to Live Más
- **Vision**: To fuel taco culture with real food, unique experiences, and relevant content
- **Tagline**: "Live Más"
- **Tone**: Bold, authentic, fun, real, and brave
- **Core Values**: People-first, fan-led, fearless, committed to being different
- **Design Objective**: Leveling Up to Iconic
- **Design Strategy**: Clear Branding, Bold Content, Warm Experience
- **Graphic Design Direction**: Bold, Colorful, Clever, Upbeat, Clean
- **Primary Color**: Taco Bell Purple (the brand's signature, dominant color)
- **Secondary Colors**: Accent colors used sparingly; gradient palettes (Day/Night) and heritage colors for specific storytelling
- **Typography**: GT America font family:
  - GT America Bold (Uppercase) — headlines, Tracking -5
  - GT America Bold (Case Sensitive) — subheads, 22pt, Tracking 25
  - GT America Regular (Case Sensitive) — body copy, 12pt, Tracking 0
  - All use Kerning: Optical
- **Typography Tiers**:
  - Brand Baseline — standard messaging (GT America Bold/Regular)
  - Brand Punchline — sparingly for impactful statements
  - Brand Whisper — technical info and legal disclaimers
- **Logo**: Updated Bell icon (sharpened upper shadow, adjusted rim curves, squared-off bottom corners) + one-line wordmark (no stacking allowed)
- **Auditory Signature**: "The Bong" — iconic sound cue for brand recall
- **Photography & Motion**: "Real Crave Content" — authentic, candid, playful. Highlight product attributes: cheesy, crunchy, melty, saucy, spicy
- **Cinematography**: Gritty and energetic — DV cam style, motion film, stop-motion, ultra slow-motion
- **Key Design Principles**:
  1. A fun brand doesn't need "fun" branding
  2. There is only one Bell, and it rings loud
  3. There is only one purple, and it's ours
  4. Simplified lockup approach
  5. Focused color strategy
  6. Timeless and timely font suite
  7. Universal pictogram program

## Allegiant Menu Photos — Post-Production Session (2026-09-08)
Anthony reviewed the raw shoot output (579 ARW frames, Drive folder above) against the client's chosen selects. Pattern: DRG's team picked portrait/high-angle frames where food is small and box lids read upside-down; better-composed low-angle landscape alternates exist in nearly every sequence with the wordmark readable — flagged as options in `marketing/output/creative/taco-bell-cantinas/photo-references/menu-photo-apollo-selects-2026-09-08.md`, with 26 alt frames copied to a Drive "apollo selects" subfolder for reference. Once the Allegiant email thread surfaced the real spec (see section above — vertical 1080×1920, cutout-ready, DESIRE approach), the portrait picks turned out to be correct for the format; empty headroom in each frame is intentional space for item name/price on the menu screen.

**Color-matching to brand book:** Taco Bell Purple is `#5d2994` (sRGB 93,41,148). Initial shoot frames measured with the LUXE mark at roughly half the brand's saturation and a blue-lavender cast across the white sweep. Built and iterated a Lightroom Camera Raw preset (Develop module → Presets panel → Launchpad group) to correct this — now at **v3**, saved to `marketing/output/creative/taco-bell-cantinas/photo-references/` (also copied to Desktop and the TUFSSD Tacobell folder for easy import). Iteration log and hex/HSL targets are in `tb-brand-color-targets.md` in that same folder.
- v1: warmed the sweep, pushed purple — undershot because the printed purple straddles Lightroom's Blue and Purple HSL ranges.
- v2: pushed both Blue and Purple sliders + Calibration — purple landed close to brand (measured 99,59,129 vs target 93,41,148), but yellow/orange lift made cheese, taco shell, and chicken breading all read the same washed-out yellow.
- v3: separated the warm tones — yellow sat/lum down, orange shifted toward red, chicken breading distinct from taco shell distinct from cheese. This is the one to use going forward.
- **Known gap:** v3's purple/blue push is tuned for the LUXE box print and will oversaturate the Freeze cup colors (blue, green freezes) if synced blindly. Apply v3 to box/food frames; for the 4 drink frames, either dial HSL/Calibration by hand or zero out Blue sat/lum and drop Purple sat to ~20 after applying.
- Small open item flagged, not yet fixed: lettuce in the v3 test frame still reads washed out — needs Green sat +12 / lum −6.

**Export path:** wrote `tb-menu-export.jsx` (Photoshop ExtendScript, same photo-references folder) to batch Select-Subject-mask, crop, and export at 1080×1920 PNG per the Allegiant spec. Untested against real files as of this session — run on one frame before batching.

Anthony is doing the masking/cleanup himself in Lightroom + Photoshop from here.

## 2025 Creative Playbook — what it adds for Allegiant (2026-09-08)
DRG wants the **Taco Bell Brand Creative Playbook 2025** (Adopt LLC, 147 pp) referenced for the Allegiant menu photos. It supersedes the Sept 2023 brand book for photography direction; brand colors are identical. Text extract + page notes: `launchpad-automation/marketing/output/creative/taco-bell-cantinas/photo-references/tacobell-creative-playbook-2025-text.txt` and `tb-brand-color-targets.md`. The PDF is 242 MB — never attach it to a session; read the text file or render single pages.
- **DESIRE** (Audrey's "creative approach") = the mid-funnel content tier whose placement list includes Kiosk / Menu. Content types: Hero Meal, Hero Details, Hero Enjoyment. Qualities: fresh, tempting, mouthwatering.
- **Baja Heat lighting:** key from the left, hard shadow right. Our 2026-08-26 frames match. Keep shadows.
- **Backgrounds replace the white sweep:** flat brand color (Yellow Corn, Nacho Orange, Taco Bell Purple) or the day-to-night orange↔purple gradient. Flat 75% / gradient 20% of the time. Hard color splits (orange | purple) are on-book.
- **Styling bar** is messier than what we shot: sauce drizzle, cheese overflow, crunch edges. Note for the next TBC food shoot.
