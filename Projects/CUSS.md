## Company Info

CUSS is a sub-brand of Mitchell Stankovic & Associates (MSA) designed to modernize credit unions through technology, shared services, and leadership support. CUSS serves as the "cloud-native first" brand in the credit union industry, offering fractional executives, shared business services, cloud-native core tech stacks, and retirement plan solutions.

### Brand Archetype
- **Core:** Innovator + Challenger
- **Promise:** "Anticipate, Assimilate, Innovate, Motivate"
- **Mission:** Help small & boutique credit unions stay competitive by modernizing away from legacy systems and predatory contracts

### The 4 Pillars
1. **Fractional Executives** - Solving succession gaps and leadership shortages in small credit unions
2. **Shared Business Services** - Centralized back office (ops, compliance, call centers, marketing)
3. **Cloud-Native Core Tech Stack** - API-driven, vendor-flexible, future-proof systems (moving credit unions away from legacy)
4. **Retirement Plans** - Sustainable executive transitions & benefits solutions
