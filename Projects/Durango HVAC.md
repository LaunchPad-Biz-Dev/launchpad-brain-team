## Video Content Notes (from IG Analysis — 2026-04-21)
*Source: @durangohvac Instagram — 20 posts scraped via Apify, April 21, 2026*

**Content formats used to date (do not repeat):**
- Direct-to-camera "call us today" clips — done repeatedly in English and Spanish
- Basic "estamos aquí para ustedes" team presence clips
- Simple ductwork and maintenance service announcements
- Raw candid phone number callouts
- "Un mensaje especial de @pancho_favela" style personal videos

**What performs best (engagement benchmarks):**
- Top performers (15–24 likes): warm, personal direct-to-camera with strong personality — Edgar OR Pancho's energy carries these
- The slogan "No problems. Only solutions." used as a close consistently outperforms videos that don't use it
- Spanish-language content gets solid engagement from Las Vegas bilingual audience — this is core to the brand, not optional
- Posts with captions tagging #hvacvegas #LasVegasLiving get more discovery

**Formats NOT yet attempted (open for ads):**
- POV skit format
- "Tell me without telling me" trend
- Green screen reaction
- Day-in-the-life cinematic BTS
- Listicle / numbered tips
- Comparison split-screen skit
- Narrative emergency story arc

**Key insight:** Durango's organic IG is authentic and warm but has zero format variety. Every paid ad we produce should use a format they have never done before.

## Ad Reporting Requirements (added 2026-08-20)

**Result Type: CALLS.** Confirmed by Amber Nardo 2026-08-20. Calls is its own result type, not a
variety of lead gen — this client is judged on phone calls, not form fills.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Headline metrics** | Calls generated · Cost per call |
| **Source of truth** | **The GHL tracking number.** Amber, 2026-08-20: *"Call clients are going to the number directed in GHL."* |
| **Supporting** | Form leads + CPL · callback requests · spend vs budget · CTR · CPC |
| **Do not report** | ROAS · purchases · revenue |

⚠️ **Guardrail — the six-call-metrics trap.** Meta exposes at least six different "call" metrics and they
do not agree with each other. This broke the MNL Q1→Q2 comparison in July 2026. **Report the GHL number
as THE number.** If a Meta call metric differs, footnote it and name which metric it is — never lead with it.
