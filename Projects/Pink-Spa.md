## Company Info
Pink Spa is a newly opened Asian massage parlor in Las Vegas offering traditional Oriental massage therapy. They specialize in various massage modalities including Swedish, Deep Tissue, Hot Stone, and Reflexology. The spa provides a serene, professional environment focused on therapeutic wellness and relaxation.
