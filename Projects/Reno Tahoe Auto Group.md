## Company Info
Reno Tahoe Auto Group is an established used car dealership specializing in 1-Owner vehicles with Extended Warranties. They maintain an inventory of 350+ vehicles with 30+ makes and 50+ models available. As a CARFAX Advantage Dealer, they offer Free Vehicle History Reports on every vehicle.

**Key Differentiators:**
- 1-Owner vehicles only
- Lifetime Engine Warranty on all vehicles
- No-commission sales team (pressure-free buying experience)
- Financing rates as low as 1.99% on approved credit
- Trade-in program with top dollar offers
- Extended warranties on most vehicles

**Mission:** To revolutionize the pre-owned vehicle market by providing quality vehicles, transparent information, and customer-centric service without pressure tactics.
