## About This Document
This is a master content library of carousel graphic ideas developed for Ben Nadig Law's social media and advertising campaigns. These carousels serve the firm's strategy of educating potential clients about common legal situations, mistakes, and how BNL can help.

## Carousel Strategy
- **Purpose:** Educational + Lead Generation for personal injury cases
- **Format:** Multi-slide carousel posts on Facebook, Instagram
- **Tone:** Informative yet approachable; uses real-world scenarios to educate without being alarmist
- **Call-to-Action:** Encourage consultation; position Ben Nadig Law as expert guide
