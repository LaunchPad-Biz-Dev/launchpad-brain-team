## Brand Guidelines
- **Brand Archetypes**: Caregiver (nurturing relationships, community-centered) + Hero (speed, reliability, solving urgent financial needs)
- **Brand Voice**:
  - Tone: Approachable, empathetic, and confident
  - Voice: Friendly, down-to-earth — like a trusted friend who helps customers handle financial tasks quickly and with care. Emphasize time saved and the personal touch.
  - Messaging: Focus on community, trust, and reliability. Customers should feel CCS understands their needs and offers a faster, more convenient alternative to traditional banks.
- **Primary Colors**:
  - Navy Blue: #000076
  - Gold/Yellow: #FFD008
  - Black: #000000
  - White: #FFFFFF
- **Typography**:
  - Primary Font: Arial Black
  - Secondary Font: Montserrat SemiBold Italic
- **Tagline**: TBD (not yet established)
- **Mission Statement**: Dedicated to serving the community by offering fast, convenient, and reliable financial solutions. Building lasting relationships through personalized services. Treating every customer as part of the family.
- **Core Values**:
  - Community Commitment — deeply rooted in local communities, hiring locally
  - Customer-Centric Service — care, respect, understanding; build trust and long-term relationships
  - Speed and Convenience — fast and easy services, multiple tasks in one visit
  - Trust and Transparency — no hidden fees, straightforward solutions
  - Diversity and Inclusion — accessible to everyone regardless of background
  - Reliability and Integrity — consistently high-quality, dependable services
- **Brand Fear**: Being viewed as just another financial service provider — must differentiate through community focus, transparent pricing, and superior customer service
- **Content Strategy**:
  - Focus on customer stories, community involvement, testimonials
  - Demonstrate commitment to personalized service
  - Position as the ultimate solution for fast, efficient, trustworthy financial services
  - Differentiate from traditional banks and DMVs through community ties, reliability, and all-in-one convenience
- **Customer Journey**:
  - CTA: Visit a CCS location or order online for currency exchange services
  - Success: Customer finishes tasks quickly and leaves feeling supported, respected, and part of a community
  - Avoiding Failure: Customer avoids the stress, frustration, and delays of traditional banks or DMV locations
