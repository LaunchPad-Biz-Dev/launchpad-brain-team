## BLOCK A — Storefront / The Build
*The 9-frame coverage matrix they asked for: Wide / Medium / Tight × Left / Head-on / Right*

Shoot **every frame twice — one clean (empty), one populated.** Clean plates go to the website and print. Populated goes to social.

### Wide — the build in stadium context
- [ ] **A1** — WIDE, head-on, centered/symmetrical. Full facade + enough concourse to read "this is inside a stadium."
- [ ] **A2** — WIDE, left 45°. Concourse leading line into the storefront. Show depth.
- [ ] **A3** — WIDE, right 45°. Mirror of A2.

### Medium — the ordering experience
- [ ] **A4** — MED, head-on. Counter + menu boards + staff behind the line.
- [ ] **A5** — MED, left. Kiosks / POS / order-in zone.
- [ ] **A6** — MED, right. Pickup + beverage station / freeze machines.

### Tight — the details that sell the brand

- [ ] **A8** — TIGHT, left. Neon, purple glow, graphic treatments, wall texture, materials.
- [ ] **A9** — TIGHT, right. Freeze machine / tap wall / cup stack / branded detail.

### Add-ons if time allows

## Format Guide

Every hero setup gets shot in more than one ratio. Frame loose enough to crop — decide the format at the camera, not in post.

| Ratio | Use | What to shoot | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **9:16 vertical** | Reels, TikTok, Stories | Handheld POV walk-ups, pour/dispense close-ups, single-subject portraits, drink raised to camera. Headroom at top for Stories UI. | V6, C4, C5, E3 |
| **4:5 feed** *(primary)* | IG feed, 1080×1350 | The TBC house format — most of the shoot composes for this. Populated storefront, crew lineup, drink in hand. Dark band top or bottom for type. | E2, V1, C5 |
| **1:1 square** | Feed carousel, grid | Centered product beauty, macro condensation/swirl, overhead flat-lay. Freeze on the center axis, even margins. | V2, C2, V7 |
| **16:9 horizontal** | Website header, PR, press | Ultra-wide storefront hero, concourse establishing, group behind counter. Shoot wider than feels right. | ⚠️ none |
| **BTS** | DRG channels, our case study | Crew and setup — lights going up, a shot being framed, team working the counter. | E1, E4 |

> ⚠️ **16:9 gap:** the inspo board is entirely portrait. There is no 16:9 reference in it, so that ratio is on us — shoot it deliberately or we won't have it.

## Technical Notes

**Lighting & color — this is the hard part.**
- Allegiant is a **domed stadium**. The concourse is 100% artificial light. Expect a nasty mix: LED menu boards, purple neon, overhead fluorescent, and TV screens — all different color temperatures in one frame.
- **Shoot RAW.** Set a custom white balance off a neutral surface at the counter, then check it again in the concourse. Do not trust auto WB.
- Expose for the **storefront glow** and let the concourse fall off dark. The dark surround is on-brand and gives us text space.

**Flash.**
- TBC's approved visual system is **direct-flash nightlife aesthetic**. Bring on-camera flash and use it — it's not a compromise, it's the look.
- Flash also kills the mixed-temperature problem on people shots. Use it for all of Block C and Block E.

**Glass.**
- 24–70 f/2.8 for coverage (Blocks A, B, C).
- Fast prime — 35 f/1.4 — for lifestyle and low-light crowd (Blocks E, F).
- Something long-ish if available for candid guest shots without invading space.

**Framing for downstream use.**
- Every hero gets a **horizontal, a 4:5, and a 9:16** version. Feed is 1080×1350; Stories and Reels are 9:16; website needs 16:9.
- **Leave negative space.** Our approved TBC system is 15–25% text placed in the photo's natural dark zones. If every frame is edge-to-edge busy, design can't use it. Consciously compose dark, empty areas into the top or bottom third on hero frames.

**Volume target.** 300–500 frames across the 3 hours. Ruthlessly cover Blocks A, C, D — those are the ask.

## Do NOT Shoot

- ❌ **No food.** Client-specified. No tacos, no trays, no food in the hero frames. Menu boards showing food items are fine.
- ❌ No empty/dirty tables, trash cans, spills, or back-of-house clutter in frame.
- ❌ No competitor concession brands legible in the background.
- ❌ No minors in the same frame as an alcoholic Twisted Freeze.
- ❌ No guests who visibly decline or look uncomfortable — move on immediately.

## Compliance & Clearances — Confirm With DRG Tomorrow

These are the things that will kill an asset in post if we don't handle them on the day.

1. **Early access** — badged in by 1:30–2:00 PM for clean plates. *(Highest priority.)*
2. **Alcohol depiction.** Twisted Freezes are spiked. Both Taco Bell corporate and the NFL have rules on alcohol imagery — typically everyone visible with an alcoholic beverage must appear 25+. Confirm which SKU we're shooting and whether alcohol can appear in public-facing assets at all.
3. **Talent releases.** Signed releases for every employee. Get a stack of guest releases too, or restrict guest shots to non-identifiable/background compositions.
4. **NFL / Raiders / Allegiant trademark clearance.** The Shield is strict about commercial use of team marks and stadium IP. Confirm in writing what we're licensed to show and where it can run — organic social vs. paid vs. website vs. print are different permissions.
5. **Shooting credentials.** Where can we set up? Tripod/light stand allowed on the concourse? Is there a media escort requirement?
6. **On-site wrangler.** Who from DRG is walking with us to pull employees and clear guests? Name and cell number.
7. **Camera-approved crew.** Which employees are cleared and will be in fresh uniform.
8. **Clean souvenir cups.** Pull unused stock for product shots.
9. **Usage & deliverable date.** When do they need first selects? Grand opening content is time-sensitive.

## Post-Production Direction

Per the approved TBC visual system:
- **Warm, moody, nightlife color grade.** Dark base, saturated purple, punchy highlights.
- **Photo is the hero.** Text enhances, never competes — 15–25% text, placed in the photo's actual negative space.
- Typography: GT America Bold, all caps, white, 48–68pt.
- Small TBC lockup, bottom corner, white.
- Feed format 1080×1350.
- Captions: 1–2 sentences, lowercase, casual, zero brand speak.
