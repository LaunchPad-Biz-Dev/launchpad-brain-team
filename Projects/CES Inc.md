# CES Inc. (Communication Electronic Systems Inc.)

> **NEW PROSPECT — Discovery meeting 2026-08-31.** Not yet in Notion Client Portals — track here until formalized. Proposal to be sent (website redesign + social media).

## 1. Company Overview

- **Full legal name:** Communication Electronic Systems Inc. (recently transitioned from LLC → Inc., ~1 year ago)
- **Industry:** Electrical contractor, Las Vegas-based, operating since the 1990s
- **Business model:** Historically subcontractors; now taking on general-contractor roles
- **Structure:** Currently transitioning to an ESOP (employee-owned) structure
- **Handles:** Social — "CES Vegas" (Facebook + LinkedIn only, no Instagram/TikTok)
- **Contact:** Purchasing manager, newly in role, given explicit ownership sign-off to pursue the redesign — millennial, change-oriented, more creative latitude than a typical contact
- **Internal risk:** Documented history of resistance to change — an email-signature standardization effort failed after ~4 revision cycles; ~3 competing logos and 12 different letterheads currently in circulation

## 2. Opportunity Scope

### Website Redesign
- Current site: severely outdated, on Squarespace, managed solely by an internal IT contact (Robert) — every change bottlenecks through him
- Doesn't reflect current capabilities; "CES" name collides with Consumer Electronics Show for SEO
- **Goals:** reposition as general contractor (not subcontractor); showcase major projects (hotel/casino builds, CCSB charter school, Sylvester Westcrab, City of Henderson Musco light upgrades, NFL Draft wiring, charter school scoreboards); modernize visual identity; emphasize systems/services over "basic installs"; set up for community/employee-focused content (apprenticeships, employee stories)
- **Reference sites:** Musco (praised for hero video — flagged as a must-have), Biamp (informational approach), Notifier/Honeywell (solution-focused narrative)
- **Pricing:** $3K (client supplies assets) – $5K (full videography incl. hero video, jobsite shoots, partner interviews); post-launch management/updates included for 1 year; extra pages/features billed at wholesale hourly after delivery

### Social Media
- Build footprint to support recruiting (apprenticeships, employee stories, culture)
- Content model similar to **[[Helix Electric]]** — video production, employee spotlights, jobsite shoots
- Monthly retainer option: content strategy, brand kit, shooting, editing, graphic design, posting, ad management (incl. recruiting-focused ads)
- **Bundle discount** if website + social signed together

## 3. Timeline

- **Target launch:** December 2026
- **Announcement:** January 2027 — coordinated with other company changes (new accounting software, branding refresh)
- **Leverage point:** contact plans to use the **[[Helix Electric]]** campaign launch (~2–3 weeks out as of 2026-08-31) as internal proof to win exec approval

## 4. Key Insights

- Strong cultural fit — CES values modernization, employee development, community; aligns with Launchpad's approach
- Ownership has approved budget exploration; contact is enthusiastic and empowered (not typical low-authority contact)
- Multi-channel opportunity: website + social + recruiting-ads (tied to website forms/CRM)
- December delivery is a firm deadline (linked to Jan 1 accounting-software launch + rebrand announcement)
- Internal rollout risk: past change initiatives stalled — may need to manage adoption after delivery, not just deliver the asset
