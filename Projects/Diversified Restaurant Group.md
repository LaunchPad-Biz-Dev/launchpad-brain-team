Diversified Restaurant Group (DRG) is a major franchise operator managing and growing large-scale restaurant brands like Taco Bell and Arby's across multiple states. DRG handles day-to-day operations, staffing, training, and local marketing for established national brands, including innovative concepts like Cantinas and food trucks. The operational backbone that ensures efficient execution and scalable growth across multiple locations.
