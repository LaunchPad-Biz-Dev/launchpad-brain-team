## Brand Identity

### Tone & Feel
Elegant, sophisticated, luxurious — brings the royal experience of Princess Cruise Lines into the home. Refined indulgence. Premium materials and craftsmanship.

### Visual Direction
- **Look:** Classic, refined, upscale — focus on premium materials and craftsmanship
- **Imagery:** High-resolution bedding in luxurious, well-appointed rooms. Highlight textures and fine details to convey premium quality
- **Design Direction:** Sophisticated palette, elegant design elements, focus on fine details and textures, beautifully styled rooms suggesting a high-end cruise experience

### Colors
- **Royal Blue:** `#002E5D`
- **Gold:** `#C5A300`
- **Elegant White:** `#F0F0F0`

### Typography
- **Headings:** Playfair Display (elegant serif)
- **Body:** Lato (clean sans-serif)

### Brand Guardrails
- ✅ Emphasize craftsmanship, texture, premium quality
- ✅ Tie back to the Princess Cruises elegance
- ❌ Avoid playful or "fun" tone (that's Carnival's lane)
- ❌ Avoid overly busy or colorful design
- ❌ Never compromise on visual polish — this brand sells refinement

## Ad Reporting Requirements — Result Type (added 2026-08-20)

*Additive to the Ad Reporting Requirements section above; does not replace it.*

**Result Type: SALES / PURCHASES.** Confirmed by Amber Nardo 2026-08-20. Backup metric: total sales revenue.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Headline metrics** | Purchases · Revenue · **ROAS scoped to the sales campaign** |
| **Supporting** | AOV · cost per purchase · add-to-cart · checkout value · CTR |

⚠️ **Guardrail 1 — never lead with a blended ROAS.** Do not mix a sales campaign with an awareness or
traffic campaign in the headline ROAS. July 2026 Carnival Home: blended 0.66, sales campaign alone 1.28.
Lead with the sales-campaign figure; show the blend beneath it and say what's in it. Amber has corrected
this twice (2026-06-26 and again in the June meeting prep).

⚠️ **Guardrail 2 — no pixel, no ROAS number.** If the purchase pixel is not installed, **do not print a
ROAS at all** and do not set Health off a purchase metric. Print: *"Purchase tracking not yet installed —
reporting on traffic for this period."* Amber, 2026-06-26: *"Even though it says that the website traffic
campaign got zero conversions to sales that doesn't mean we need to make any changes to it it just means
that we don't have the campaign set to track conversions to purchase."* This regressed into the July 2026
reports as "ROAS 0.00 — Off Track" on Princess and Travel + Leisure. It must not regress again.

**A zero ROAS has at least three different causes. Always say which one:**
1. No purchase pixel installed → report traffic, flag the pixel as a client action item
2. No spend / campaigns not delivering → this is a delivery problem, not a performance problem
3. Real spend, real tracking, no purchases → this is the only case that means the ads underperformed

**🔴 Live status (verified 2026-08-20, TikTok `7621253326994407431`):** **ZERO spend Aug 1–19.** All four
campaigns returning zero. Last delivery was July ($499.69, 107,264 impressions, 1,805 clicks, 1.68% CTR —
matches the July report exactly, so the advertiser ID is correct and the zero is real). Cause cannot be
determined from the API — paused, budget, or billing all look identical. Flagged to Amber 2026-08-20;
needs eyes in TikTok Ads Manager.
Note the correct advertiser ID is `7621253326994407431`; the old `7247978881058111489` ("Princess Luxury
Bed0623") is stale and has no delivery.
