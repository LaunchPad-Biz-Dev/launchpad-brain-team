## Company Info
Toro Taxes is a franchise opportunity designed to help tax professionals transition from tax preparation to business ownership. The franchise targets multiple audience segments including existing tax preparers, independent tax prep businesses, insurance agents, and real estate professionals seeking to expand their service offerings.

**Franchise Value Proposition:**
- Transition from employee to owner-operator
- Comprehensive support and training for franchisees
- Proven systems and business model
- Multiple business expansion models (single/multi-unit growth)
- Access to profitable tax services market
- Support for existing businesses seeking diversification

## Target Audience Segments
### 1. Tax Preparers (Employment → Ownership)
- Current tax prep professionals
- Target Message: Transition from preparing returns to running your own office
- Motivation: More income, independence, leadership, business ownership
- Key Benefit: Guided transition with proven systems

### 2. Existing Tax Prep Business Owners
- Current single/multi-location operators seeking growth
- Target Message: Expand from 1 office to multiple locations
- Motivation: Revenue growth, expansion, scaling
- Key Benefit: Proven expansion model and support

### 3. Insurance Agents
- Seeking to diversify service offerings
- Target Message: Add tax services to existing client relationships
- Motivation: More services = more client loyalty + more revenue
- Key Benefit: Leverage existing customer base; passive income stream

### 4. Real Estate Agents
- Seeking diversification and expanded client services
- Target Message: Serve clients beyond real estate; expand revenue per client
- Motivation: More touchpoints with clients; additional income stream
- Key Benefit: Enhanced value proposition to clients
