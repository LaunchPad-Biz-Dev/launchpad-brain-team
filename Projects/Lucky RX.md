## Company Info
LuckyRx is a prescription men's sexual-health brand (compounded, telehealth) positioned as **"The Relationship Experience Brand"**: it goes after the couple, not just the man, across three dimensions (sexual ~80%, mental ~10%, physical ~10% of focus today). Premium, aspirational, witty but never crude. Archetypes: Ruler + Hero now, Lover later. "Rx" doubles as "Relationship Experience" (trademark intended). Long-term goal: de-stigmatize enough to ship in branded packaging openly. Future pipeline: NAD+, peptides, GLP-1, skincare.

## Products
- **Lucky WOW** (hero, live): 5-in-1 sublingual rapid-dissolving tablet — Sildenafil 55mg (blood flow), Tadalafil 15mg (staying power), Apomorphine 2.5mg (desire), Oxytocin 50IU (connection), Vitamin B12 2.5mg (energy). CinnaMint. Competitors max out at 4-in-1; **B12/energy is the differentiator.** Pricing on site 2026-10-07: 6 tabs $9.17/dose · 12 $8.57 · 18 $8.27 ("Most Popular", $148.86/mo) · 24 $7.77 ("Best Value"). 1/3/6-month quantities, monthly or quarterly auto-ship, one-time available.
- **Lucky Weekend** (live): Tadalafil 15 / Vardenafil 15 / B12.
- **Lucky Ignite** (coming): lighter formula for men who don't need full ED support.
- **Lucky Lady** (coming, "a couple of months" from 9/15): female arousal/connection product, own funnel.
- **Purchase flow:** online intake (~2 min) → ID upload → licensed US physician review (≤24h) → compounded by a licensed US pharmacy → free 2-day discreet shipping. **Partners cannot order for each other.** US only; **South Carolina excluded** (per Cynthia 9/15; full state list unverified).
- **Unboxing:** "Get Lucky" pillow, two travel pouches, "Do Not Disturb, Getting Lucky" door hanger, "Give Her the WOW" PDF. No competitor has this.
- **Onset (client-stated, NOT for ads):** ~5 min to feel, ~15 min full; 9/22 said 5–45 min depending on body/diet.

## Audience
- **Primary:** men 30–45 to cast (buyers 25–60), entrepreneur / professional / luxury-lifestyle, "James Bond" avatar. Audiences see themselves as younger than they are; avoid extremes.
- **Secondary:** women buying the idea for their partner (20–30% of creator voices at first; shift to couples when Lucky Lady launches).
- **What they learned running ads themselves (Sept 2026):** V2 creative (cleaner fonts, premium) beat V1; static beat video; whisper-style UGC testimonials work organically; "one month free" pulled deal-seekers with card declines; lead quality is their #1 complaint.

## Brand Guidelines
- **Tone:** premium, witty, one dry line, never slapstick/cheesy/crude. Creators may be more playful; brand-produced content stays elevated. "Insinuate rather than show."
- **Visual (10/01):** upscale, elegant; green/black/satin; vignette and gradient depth; settings = date night, restaurant, car, nightstand, front door (bedroom only where paid policy allows, i.e. not in Meta ads). Fonts finalized 10/01 (primary headline + sans body); logo/colors stable, logo "subject to change" per 9/15. Stock: Death to Stock / Envato; textures: Texture Labs. Avoid stock-looking imagery.
- **Yes-words:** enhance, fun, "lucky" / "Who wants to get Lucky?"
- **Content pillars:** Sexual, Mental, Physical. **Buckets:** Relationship Experience, Product Itself, Male POV, Female POV. **Frameworks:** Educate, Entertain, Empathy.
- **Lead magnet that works:** Crave Code ("9 Powerful Tips to Make Her Crave You") → SMS/email via Attentive.

## Prohibited (client's written ad rules, 9/22; stricter than Meta)
No results / speed / duration claims · no "FDA approved" (compounded) · no before/after, timers, countdowns · no competitor names · no explicit, suggestive, or bedroom content · no alcohol with product · no medical advice · no unapproved pricing or promos · no fabricated testimonials · nobody under 18 · no copyrighted music · **nothing posts without client approval**. Written prohibited-words list promised 9/22, not yet received. "Performance enhancement" framing pending Josh (legal). **Known conflict:** the site itself says "works within 5 minutes" and "36-hour window"; ads follow the stricter rule.
