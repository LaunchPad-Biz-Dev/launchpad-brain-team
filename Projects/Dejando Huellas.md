# Dejando Huellas de Amor

## Client Overview
**Official Name:** Dejando Huellas de Amor (DHA)
**Translation:** "Leaving Traces of Love"
**Founder/Leader:** Angelica Curiel
**Industry:** Wellness, Mentorship, Spiritual Healing, Personal Development
**Location:** Las Vegas, Nevada
**Services Model:** Group programs, individual therapies, workshops, seminars, membership program

## Mission & Focus
Provide transformational workshops, mentorship, Reiki, and self-paced therapy programs focused on personal development, healing, and spiritual growth for all age groups (kids, teens, adults).

## Services Offered

### Workshops & Seminars
- **Launch Plan:** Nationwide and eventually worldwide expansion (January 2024 launch)
- **Format:** Interactive, transformational workshops
- **Goal:** Reach diverse audiences with healing and personal development content
- **Pricing:** $300/person per seminar
- **Hard Costs:** $40-50 per person
- **January Goal:** 30 participants minimum

### Group Programs (Regular Offerings)
- **Kids Group (8-11 years):** 3-4 people, $20/session
- **Teens (12-13+):** 4 people, $20/session (maturity-dependent)
- **Adults:** 20-30 participants per session, $20/session

### Individual Therapies
- **Frequency:** 3-4 per month on average
- **Format:** Weekly meetings or by request
- **Volume:** ~120 total individual therapy slots annually
- **Price:** TBD (not specified in notes)

### Sanaciones (Healing Sessions)
- **Frequency:** Once per month
- **Attendance:** 10-15 people per session
- **Duration:** 7-8 hours per session
- **Price:** $100 per session

### Mentorship Programs
- **Target:** All age groups (kids, teens, adults)
- **Schedule:** Tuesdays (for adults)
- **Format:** Ongoing guidance and support
- **Integration:** With workshop and seminar content

### Planned Membership Program
- **Duration:** 7-month program
- **Format:** Weekly self-paced therapy videos + reflection component
- **Platform:** Circle (or similar community platform)
- **Price:** $50/month
- **Revenue Model:** Subscription-based recurring revenue

### Future E-Commerce Products
- Quartz/crystals
- Sprays (possibly aromatherapy/energetic)
- Sound therapy products
- Branded merchandise (shirts)

## LaunchPad Approach (Budget Constraints)

### Phase 1: Rapid Foundation (December—January Launch)
**Duration:** Immediate—1 month
**Budget-Conscious:** Focus on maximum ROI with minimal spend

#### Week 1-2: Brand & CRM Setup
1. **Brand Kit Development**
   - Simple but effective logo, color scheme, typography, taglines
   - Milestone: Complete within 1 week
   - Focus: Warm, nurturing, transformational aesthetic

2. **CRM & Landing Pages Setup**
   - Platform: Go High Level (white-labeled for DHA)
   - Purpose: Lead capture, customer management, accounting support
   - Landing Page 1: Seminar sign-up (January workshop)
   - Landing Page 2: Membership program promotion ($50/month)
   - Milestone: Operational by end of week 2

#### Week 2-3: Social Media Launch
1. **Social Media Profiles**
   - Platforms: Facebook, Instagram (primary)
   - Content: Seminar promotion, membership highlights, testimonials
   - Milestone: Initial follower base and buzz creation

2. **Organic Content Strategy**
   - Posts about seminar details
   - Membership program education
   - Team introductions
   - Testimonial/success stories
   - No paid advertising initially

3. **Membership Program Development**
   - Platform: Circle (or Kajabi alternative)
   - Content: 7-month video library of therapy content
   - Structure: Weekly self-paced videos + reflection prompts
   - Launch: Promote simultaneously with seminar

#### Week 3-4: Seminar Promotion Push
1. **Landing Page Copy & CTAs**
   - Clear seminar details (date, time, price, format)
   - Urgent messaging (January launch)
   - Easy registration flow
   - Early-bird discount if applicable

2. **Social Media Campaign**
   - Daily posts about seminar
   - Share Angelica's transformation story
   - Team member spotlights
   - Testimonials from past participants
   - Countdown posts

### Phase 2: Community Engagement (February—March)
- Optimize seminar follow-ups
- Begin membership content creation
- Grow social following through organic engagement
- Plan next seminar/workshop

### Phase 3: Digital Expansion (April+)
- Website launch (if budget allows)
- Paid digital advertising campaigns
- Merchandise design and sales
- App development for mentorship program
- Expanded membership program features
