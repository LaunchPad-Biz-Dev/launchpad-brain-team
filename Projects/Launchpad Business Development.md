### Brand Voice & Tone
Confident but not corporate. Creative but clean. Professional, strategic, and visually engaging. A marketing company that practices what it preaches — modern, knowledgeable, and approachable. NOT stiff, stuffy, overly salesy, or generic. Launchpad speaks like a trusted partner who knows what they're doing, not a vendor trying to close.

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Authority Building** — case studies, testimonials, performance results, success stories
2. **Educational Content** — marketing strategy, trends, practical tools (Content/Leads/Systems)
3. **Behind-the-Scenes** — people, processes, shoots, meetings, team energy, warehouse life
4. **Engagement-Driven** — response videos, challenges, hot takes, Q&A
5. **Lifestyle & Brand Personality** — company culture, who we are, how we work

### Key Phrases / Language to Use
- "Content → Leads → Systems"
- "We practice what we preach"
- "Full-service marketing"
- "From shoot to post to leads"
- "Launchpad Productions" (when referencing photo/video)
- "BrandWithUs" or "BWU" (when referencing print)

### Language to Avoid
- "Guru" or "expert" in a self-proclaimed way
- Generic marketing jargon without substance ("synergy," "leverage," "disrupt")
- Overly casual/memey language that undermines credibility
- Anything that sounds like a template or stock content

## 3. Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Color Name | HEX | Use Case |
|-----------|-----|----------|
| Licorice | #1F1515 | Dark backgrounds, base contrast, body text |
| Day's Gray | #5B595B | Secondary accents, subdued backgrounds |
| Silver | #A9A6A6 | Divider lines, icons, soft contrast |
| Perm Red | #9A0101 | Primary CTA, logo accents, emphasis text |
| Rosewood | #650A0B | Rich overlays, grounding shades, shadows |
| LaunchPad Red | #E73023 | Hero accents, highlight elements, social-first graphics |
| Graphite Gray | #656263 | Neutral text, cards, caption areas |

**Color Rules:** Don't overpower legibility. Apply colors purposefully. Maintain consistency within a series.

### Typography

| Type | Font | Usage |
|------|------|-------|
| Headline | Modern sans-serif, bold | Headlines with visual hierarchy |
| Body | Clean sans-serif | Supporting body text |
| Accent | Editorial-style | Thoughtful white space, padding, alignment |

### Logo Usage
- Primary logo in red + dark variants
- Clear space maintained
- Never distort, recolor outside palette, or crowd

### Photography & Visual Direction
Clean, natural, and intentional. Studio lighting, high-resolution stills, candid behind-the-scenes moments. Capture work environment, client interactions, team culture, warehouse energy. Show the real work — shoots in progress, editing sessions, team meetings, client deliverables. NOT generic stock photography or overly polished corporate imagery.

### Design Rules
- Simple, flat or line-based iconography
- Legible at small sizes
- Color aligned with overall palette
- Bold headlines with thoughtful white space
- Avoid clutter, overly stylized fonts, or inconsistent alignment
