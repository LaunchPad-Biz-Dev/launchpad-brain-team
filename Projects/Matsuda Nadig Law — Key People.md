# Matsuda Nadig Law — Key People (client side)

Client-side roster for Matsuda Nadig Law (NV) and Matsuda Nadig Van Law (AZ). Separate from
`Teams/`, which holds Launchpad staff only. Built 2026-09-09 from Anthony while prepping the
Q4 *From the Beach to the Bar* episode.

**Why this note exists:** these are the people who appear on camera and who Launchpad talks to
daily. Before any MNL/MNV shoot, podcast, or content run, read this so nobody gets introduced
wrong or scripted around a guess.

---

## Tammy — Case Manager *(last name to confirm)*

- **Role:** Case manager. Serves **both Nevada and Arizona** — one of the few people who works
  across both offices.
- **Tenure:** Long-time. Has known **Ben and Jess 10+ years**.
- **Age:** 40s.
- **On camera:** **Spicy personality and great on camera.** Anthony's words. Treat her as a real
  on-camera asset, not a background staffer. She can carry a segment and she can push back on
  Ben and Jess, which is exactly what a four-person table needs.
- **Family:** **Sage is her daughter. Bobby is her son.** Both work at the firm (see below).

> ⚠️ **Last name unconfirmed.** Sage goes by Lopez, but do not assume Tammy shares it. Confirm
> before putting her name in a lower third, a graphic, or a caption.

**Content angles she unlocks:**
- Cross-border perspective. She's the person who can actually say how a NV case feels different
  from an AZ case in practice, not in theory.
- The case manager view: what happens to a client between "signed up" and "settled." Nobody
  covers this and clients constantly wonder about it.
- The family-at-the-firm story (see note below).

## The family angle — handle with care

Tammy, Sage, and Bobby are a mother, daughter, and son all working at the same firm, and Tammy
has known the founders for over a decade. That is a genuinely good story: it supports the firm's
client-first, "we treat you like family" positioning without anyone having to say that sentence
out loud.

**But get their permission before it goes public.** It is family information, it came from
Anthony rather than from them, and it should be their call whether it becomes content. Fine as
internal context for scripting and seating immediately. Not fine as a caption until someone says
yes.
