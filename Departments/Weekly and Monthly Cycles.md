## Monthly Content Cycle (4-Week Rotation)

Each month follows a predictable 4-week content production cycle. This is the backbone of how Launchpad delivers for clients.

### Week 1 — Report + Create
- **Focus**: Review last month's performance, plan new content
- **Key Activities**:
  - Pull analytics and reporting for all active clients
  - Monthly performance reports generated and reviewed
  - Creative briefs written for the upcoming month
  - Content calendars drafted
  - New concepts and campaign ideas developed
- **Who's Involved**: [[Anthony Mendoza]], [[Abril Rodriguez]], [[Cynthia Hernandez]], [[Amber Nardo]] (ad performance)
- **Deliverables Due**: Previous month's performance reports, new content calendars

### Week 2 — Produce
- **Focus**: Content production — shoots, graphic design, video creation
- **Key Activities**:
  - Photo and video shoots scheduled and executed
  - Graphic designers creating assets from briefs
  - Video editors begin working on raw footage
  - Ad creatives produced
- **Who's Involved**:  [[Kyle Fuller]] (content creation), [[Arija Imendia]] & [[David Canizalez]] (graphics), [[Munzelle Jazz]] & [[Ral Luna]] (video editing), [[Abril Rodriguez]] (Creative Director), [[Cynthia Hernandez]] (Ideation of Graphic Design / General Art Direction), [Emigen Lagman] (Admin Assistant to support team for overflow)
- **Deliverables Due**: Raw content captured, design drafts started

### Week 3 — Produce + Review (Post-Production + Review)
- **Focus**: Finish production, internal review, client review
- **Key Activities**:
  - Video editing completed
  - Graphics finalized
  - Internal creative review — [[Abril Rodriguez]] & [[Cynthia Hernandez]] reviews all content
  - Client review and revision rounds
  - Revisions executed based on feedback
- **Who's Involved**: [[Abril Rodriguez]] (final review), all creative team, clients for their content when approval is needed. 
- **Deliverables Due**: Completed content ready for client approval

### Week 4 — Schedule + Deliver
- **Focus**: Approved content goes live, scheduling and delivery
- **Key Activities**:
  - Approved content scheduled across platforms
  - Social media posts queued in Sprout Social
  - Ad campaigns launched or updated
  - Final deliverables sent to clients
  - Content library updated
- **Who's Involved**: [[Cynthia Hernandez]] (scheduling/posting), [[Amber Nardo]] (ad launches), [[Abril Rodriguez]] (final sign-off)
- **Deliverables Due**: All content posted/scheduled, campaigns live

## Weekly Recurring Meetings

### Team Weekly (Content Cycle Meeting)
- **When**: Weekly (typically Sunday/Monday)
- **Purpose**: Track progress against the monthly content cycle
- **Attendees**: Core team — Anthony, Abril, Cynthia, Kyle, remote editors
- **Notion Type**: "Team weekly"
- **Notes**: Meeting name reflects the current cycle week (e.g., "Week 2 - Produce")

### Weekly Accountability
- **When**: Weekly (typically Thursday)
- **Purpose**: Goal tracking, KPI review, individual accountability
- **Attendees**: Full team (in-house + remote)
- **Notion Type**: "Team weekly" (historically "Goal Planning")
- **Format**: Uses LP Accountability Meeting Worksheet (Q2 2026 version)
- **Zoom Link**: https://zoom.us/j/2361314377

### Morning Huddle
- **When**: Daily/weekly (Monday mornings historically)
- **Purpose**: Quick sync on the week's priorities and blockers
- **Attendees**: In-house team
- **Notion Type**: "Morning Huddle"
- **Notes**: Named by date range (e.g., "6/2 - 6/6 25'")

- **When**: As needed
- **Purpose**: Design team sync — [[Arija Imendia]], [[David Canizalez]], and [[Cynthia Hernandez]]
- **Notes**: Coordinates design workload and brand consistency

## How Cycles Connect to Deliverables

The monthly cycle directly maps to the **Deliverable Tracker in Notion**. Each client's deliverables should progress through this flow:

1. **Week 1**: Deliverable appears in tracker as "Planned" or "Briefed"
2. **Week 2**: Status moves to "In Production"
3. **Week 3**: Status moves to "In Review" → "Revision" → "Approved"
4. **Week 4**: Status moves to "Scheduled" → "Delivered" / "Posted"

If a deliverable is still in "In Production" during Week 4, the team is behind and needs to be flagged.
