# Launchpad Productions Department

## Purpose
Handle all photography, videography, and production work for clients and Launchpad itself. Operate studio facilities.

## Entity
Launchpad Productions (separate entity under Launchpad Business Development umbrella)

## Team Members
- Robert Quiantanace — Partner / Lead Videographer & Photographer
- Kyle Fuller — Content Creator / Videographer (supports shoots)

## Facilities (Las Vegas Warehouse)
- 20x20 cyc wall
- 2 podcast studios
- Full production equipment

## Core Responsibilities
- All photo and video shoots for clients
- Pre-production planning (shot lists, scheduling, briefs)
- Studio management and bookings
- Post-production oversight

## Production Phases
1. Pre-Production — shot list, brief, schedule
2. Production — shoot day execution
3. Post-Production — upload to Drive, assign to editors
4. Delivery — approved content to social/ads team
