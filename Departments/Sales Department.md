## Sales Process

### Lead Sources
- Referrals (current primary source)
- Website inquiries (launchpadbizdev.com)
- Social media presence
- Networking events (Las Vegas local)
- Cold outreach (building this capability)
- AI sales agents (planned)

### Sales Pipeline Stages
1. **Lead** — New contact, not yet qualified
2. **Discovery** — Initial conversation to understand needs
3. **Proposal** — Package recommendation + pricing presented
4. **Negotiation** — Discussing terms, scope, customization
5. **Closed Won** — Contract signed, moves to [[Client Onboarding Workflow]]
6. **Closed Lost** — Did not convert (document why for learning)

### Proposal & Contract Process
- Proposals are sent via **PandaDoc** for signing
- See: [Pandadoc - Proposal Signing Process](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GEx_3rzn_8qTlHdEQRAm67zgZJppXQeo) in Sales Resources
- Standard packages can be sent as-is; custom packages need [[Anthony Mendoza]] approval

**PandaDoc Workflow:**
1. **Select the Package** — Choose the pre-configured package that fits the client's needs
2. **Customize the Proposal** — Enter client details, review for accuracy
3. **Send the Proposal** — Initiate the document for client review and signature
4. **Client Interaction** — Client reviews, signs, and completes CC Authorization Form via PandaDoc
5. **Finalize** — Confirm final details and close the document

### Onboarding Handoff
When a deal closes, the client moves from Sales to Ops:
1. Sales confirms scope, package, and ad spend
2. Client details entered in Notion Client Portals (collection://175b01f7-7d6d-81c9-9b65-000b018ec443)
3. [[Client Onboarding Workflow]] is triggered
4. [[Abril Rodriguez]] assigns the creative team based on package (see [[Packages and Pricing]] for team role mapping)
5. Google Drive folder created, GHL account set up, intro meeting scheduled

### What the Agent Can Do
- Answer questions about packages and pricing

- Send follow-up emails
- Qualify leads (budget, timeline, services needed, industry)

### What the Agent Should NOT Do
- Offer custom pricing without Anthony's approval
- Promise specific results or guarantees
- Commit to timelines without checking team capacity

A qualified lead should have:
- **Budget:** Minimum $2,500/mo for a package OR $500+ for a la carte
- **Need:** At least one of: marketing, ads, content, social media, CRM
- **Timeline:** Ready to start within 30 days
- **Decision maker:** Person we're talking to can sign or has direct access to the signer
- **Industry fit:** Service-based business (preferred) or local business

### Tone & Brand Voice
- Professional but approachable — we're a Las Vegas company, not corporate stiff
- Confident in our work — show don't tell (reference portfolio)
- Solution-focused — listen first, recommend based on what they actually need
- No pressure — education-based selling, let the value speak

## Proposal Strategy & Customization

### Base Proposal Templates
Standard packages have pre-built PandaDoc proposals. Customize only the **"Your Unique Digital Marketing Package"** summary section.

**Customization Framework:**
- Lead with: "This package will amplify [Client Name]'s [specific goal]..."
- Focus on their primary objective (brand awareness, lead generation, conversion, retention)
- Tie to the three pillars (Better Content → Better Leads → Better Systems)
- Keep tone professional, matching their brand voice
- Include specific deliverables that address their stated pain points
- Close with next steps and timeline

**Tone examples:**
- Brand awareness focus: "...focus less on lead gen, more on brand awareness and reach"
- Growth focus: "...converting reach and traffic into qualified leads and sales"
- Professional tone: "Clean, professional, while conveying the same message"

### Proposal Follow-Up Emails
After sending PandaDoc:
1. **Confirmation email** — Confirm follow-up call (specific time/day)
2. **Reference the proposal** — Remind them of key highlights
3. **Set excitement** — "We're really excited about the opportunity"
4. **Offer support** — "Send over any questions, we're happy to clarify"
5. **Attach extras** — Include related invoices, design mockups, previous work samples as relevant

## Pricing Strategy & Positioning

### Cost Justification Framework
When prospects say "it's too expensive":

**Breakdown strategy:** Show cost per deliverable
- 4 videos (average $1,500 each) = $6,000
- 8 graphics (average $300 each) = $2,400
- Ad management (typical agency charge 15-20% of spend = $750-$1,000/mo minimum)
- Social media management (typically $1,000-$2,000/mo)
- CRM setup and management (typically $500-$1,500/mo)
- **Total separately: $10,650-$13,400**
- **Our total: $2,500**

**Full-service advantage:** Consolidate 5 different vendors into one partner = lower total cost + better coordination + consistency

**Scalability angle:** Start at Essential, upgrade to Comprehensive or Ultimate as business grows

### Ad Spend Separation
Always position ad spend separately from service fees:
- **Service fee** = Launchpad's work (creative + management + CRM)
- **Ad spend** = Dollar amount going to advertising platforms
- **Minimum ad spend** typically $500-$5,000/month depending on package and goals
- **Ad spend models:**
  - Fixed monthly budget (client decides spend, we optimize)
  - Performance-based (cost per lead targets)
  - Multi-platform tiers (client chooses platforms based on budget)

## Two-Meeting Sales Process Details

### Discovery Call Script Framework
1. **Warm greeting** — Build rapport
2. **Ask about current marketing** — "What does your current marketing look like?"
3. **Understand what's working** — "What's generating results for you right now?"
4. **Identify pain points** — "What's not working or frustrating you?"
5. **Learn growth goals** — "Where do you want to be in 6-12 months?"
6. **Understand past experience** — "Have you worked with an agency before? What was that like?"
7. **Timeline and budget** — "What's your timeline? What are you able to invest?"
8. **Decision-making** — "Who else is involved in this decision?"
9. **Close** — "I'm going to take everything you've shared and put together a custom recommendation. Let's meet [next week] to review it."

### Tailored Presentation Script Framework
1. **Thank you and recap** — "Thank you for sharing all that. Here's what I heard..."
2. **Show you listened** — Reference specific pain points and goals mentioned
3. **Recommend package** — "Based on your goals, I recommend the [Essential/Comprehensive/Ultimate] package"
4. **Walk through deliverables** — Show what's included, monthly breakdown
5. **Show portfolio** — Share relevant examples from their industry
6. **Timeline expectations** — "Here's what the first 90 days looks like..."
7. **Address concerns** — "Do you have any questions or concerns?"
8. **Send proposal** — "I'm going to send you the proposal via PandaDoc. You can sign electronically."
9. **Follow-up** — "I'll follow up this week to see if you have any questions."
