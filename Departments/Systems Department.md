## Notion Systems

### Q1 2026 Execution & Accountability Board
Master database for LaunchPad's internal project management and weekly accountability meetings.

**Purpose:** Single source of truth for tracking commitments, phases, status, and progress across all internal initiatives.

**Structure:**
- One master database with multiple views
- Gallery view for weekly accountability meetings
- Table/Board views for execution tracking by phase
- Properties: Commitment (Title), Owner, Phase, Status, Due Date, Week Of, Blockers/Notes
- Formula property for overdue tracking
- Manual meeting note updates (live during accountability sessions)

**Phases (Locked):**
1. **University Foundation & Backend** — Platform build, branding, infrastructure
2. **Course Content Production** — Recording, editing, PDFs, lesson completion
3. **CRM Trial Engine** — 30-day trial automation setup and stabilization
4. **Certificate & Automation** — Completion logic and delivery systems
5. **Community & Experience** — Community activation, support channels

**Weekly Accountability Cadence:**
- Tuesdays at [time] — team meetings to review, assign, and track commitments
- Commitments due by following Tuesday
- Status updated live during meeting (not retrospectively)
- Overdue visibility enables early course correction

### Finance Hub
Centralized tracking for personal and business finances, billing, and cash flow.

**Purpose:** Clear visibility into income, expenses, payroll, and financial health across Kyle's personal accounts and LaunchPad operations.

**Scope:**
- Monthly income tracking (multiple payment sources)
- Fixed expenses (mortgage, utilities, insurance, subscriptions)
- Variable expenses (payroll, contractors, tools)
- Paycheck timing and cash flow management
- Tax planning and deduction tracking
- Payroll management (employee payments, frequency)

**Key Metrics Tracked:**
- Recurring monthly expenses (categorized)
- Month-end remaining balance
- Cash flow gaps (if any)
- Estimated quarterly taxes (if applicable)
- Debt payoff timeline
- Savings/buffer targets

**Not in scope:** Investment portfolio, retirement accounts, or long-term financial planning (recommend external financial advisor).
