## Marketing Strategy & Positioning

### Core Philosophy
Marketing at Launchpad is built on **Better Content → Better Leads → Better Systems**, a three-pillar framework that connects:
- **Better Content:** Strategic, research-driven content creation (video, photography, graphics)
- **Better Leads:** Converting attention into action through paid advertising and targeting (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- **Better Systems:** CRM setup, automation, and nurture workflows that convert leads to customers (GoHighLevel)

### Service Architecture
**Three-tier recurring packages:**
1. **Essential Growth** ($2,500/mo) — 4 videos, 8 graphics, basic ads, standard CRM
2. **Comprehensive Growth** ($3,500/mo) — 8 videos, 16 graphics, multi-platform ads, professional CRM
3. **Ultimate Business Builder** ($4,500/mo) — 16 videos, 24 graphics, premium ads, advanced CRM + optimization

**Plus:** A la carte services (videography, photography, design, studio rental) and pilot programs for testing.

### Marketing Agency Positioning
**Key differentiators:**
- **One partner, not five** — Consolidate creative + ads + CRM into one integrated team
- **Quarterly planning** — 90-day roadmaps with clear deliverables, not monthly guessing
- **Results-focused** — Tied to business outcomes (leads, conversions, ROAS), not vanity metrics
- **Proven framework** — Tested across law, HVAC, real estate, restaurants, franchises
- **Artist + Engineer model** — Creative excellence paired with systematic optimization

### Media Buying & Ad Strategy
- **Primary platform:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for most lead gen campaigns
- **Secondary platforms:** Google Ads (keyword-driven), TikTok (brand awareness), LinkedIn (B2B)
- **Budget philosophy:** Minimum $500-5,000/month depending on package; separate service fee from ad spend
- **Optimization approach:** Continuous A/B testing, creative refresh every 2-4 weeks, scaling winners
- **Key metrics:** Cost Per Lead (CPL), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), conversion rate, customer acquisition cost

### Content Creation Approach
**Research-first methodology:**
1. Study what's working (intentional scrolling, pattern recognition)
2. Reverse engineer psychology (why did this stop the scroll?)
3. Identify transferable trends (adapt hooks, not invent new ones)
4. Create intentional content (not random; tied to strategy)

**Content buckets (varies by client):**
- Educational/How-To (industry tips, tutorials, "red flags")
- Service/Offer Promos (features, seasonal offers, hero content)
- Testimonials/Case Studies (success stories, transformations)
- Behind-the-Scenes/Culture (team, company values, lifestyle)
- Engagement-Driven (Q&A, polls, relatable humor)

**Production workflow:** Research → Planning → Production → Post → Delivery → Social + Ads
