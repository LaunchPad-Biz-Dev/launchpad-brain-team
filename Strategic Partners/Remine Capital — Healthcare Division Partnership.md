# Remine Capital — Healthcare Division Partnership

## Status
**In negotiation — contract at v6 (final business terms), pending healthcare-attorney review before signing.** As of 2026-07-08.

## Who
- **Remine Capital LLC** (Nevada LLC). Principals **Sam Remine** and **Ethan Remine**, both currently medical-sales reps with a real book of business + sphere of influence in the healthcare space; they also advise the companies they sell for on business development.
- They approached LaunchPad to **push their healthcare clients to LP** and grow it as a **pseudo white-label "Healthcare Division"** — an extension of what LP does. They wanted exclusivity to the healthcare space.
- Their core fear: *"LP takes all our clients then boots us and keeps them."* Anthony's mirror fear: LP's existing healthcare relationships/referrals get pulled into their division. The deal is built to neutralize both.

## The deal in one line
Sam & Ethan bring healthcare clients to LP; LP does the marketing; they earn a **flat 15% of the recurring monthly package fee** on the clients they originate. It's a business-development/commission arrangement — **not equity, not a division ownership stake.**

## Final negotiated terms (v6)
- **Compensation:** Flat **15%** of the monthly recurring package fee on each Partner-originated **Qualified Client**, paid monthly as LP collects. No payment options, no bonuses. Individual/a-la-carte production services = **10%**.
- **Post-termination residual:** 15% continues **only on Partner-originated clients**, for **24 months** after termination, then stops.
- **Performance:** Must originate ≥1 client in **180 days**; miss it (or 180 days idle) → **written Performance Improvement Plan** (≤60-day cure) → PIP failure = LP can pull exclusivity or terminate. This is LP's exit valve.
- **Exclusivity:** Narrow — scoped to **Partner's Healthcare Sphere** only. LP stays free everywhere else in healthcare. Nothing outside their sphere is commissionable without a separate written deal.
- **Lead attribution:** Activity-based — when reps overlap, the lead goes to whoever is **actively working it** (documented activity), not first-touch. LP's good-faith call breaks ties. 24-month protection window.
- **Mutual protections:** Confidentiality/NDA, non-solicit, non-circumvention, non-disparagement — all mutual, term + 12 months.
- **Ownership:** LP owns all CRM, data, IP, and client records. Signing grants Partner **zero** rights to the company — only commission on business they bring in.
- **Healthcare compliance (§19):** Comp is framed as payment for marketing services, NOT patient/referral generation; Partner warrants Anti-Kickback / Stark / HIPAA / state patient-brokering compliance.

## The REMINESOI mechanism (important — replaces Schedule A)
Instead of a written schedule/exhibit, **Partner's book of business and sphere of influence are tracked in the GHL CRM under the tag `REMINESOI`.**
- Within **15 days** of signing, Partner hands over their book (names + contact info) to be tagged REMINESOI. **Anything not tagged in 15 days is not protected** (waiver).
- New clients they originate also get tagged REMINESOI.
- The tag defines their protected sphere, their originated clients, and the scope of exclusivity/attribution. **Set this tag up in the LP AI Sales Team GHL sub-account when they onboard.**

## What we removed from their original proposal (Antonia + Anthony)
Their first draft (via Sam Remine) tried for: lifetime 16.74% residual, 24-month post-termination residual tail, a mandatory early-termination buyout (24 mo comp or 15% annualized), participation/leadership rights, and comp surviving a sale of LP. **All struck.** Anthony later chose to give a flat 15% ongoing residual (down from 16.74%) as the comp, with the 24-month post-term cap and the PIP exit.
