# Launchpad Business Development — Packages & Pricing

## Service Packages

### Essential Growth — $2,500/mo
- FB & IG Ads
- 4 Monthly Videos
- 8 Monthly Graphics
- Monthly Photo Shoot
- Social Media Posts (FB, IG, GMB)
- Standard CRM

### Comprehensive Growth — $3,500/mo
- Full Suite of Advertising Platforms (FB, IG, Google, TikTok, YouTube)
- 8 Monthly Videos
- 16 Monthly Graphics
- Monthly Photo Shoot
- Full Suite Social Media Posts
- Professional CRM
- Reputation Management

### Ultimate Business Builder — $4,500/mo
- Full Suite of Advertising Platforms (FB, IG, Google, TikTok, YouTube)
- 12 Monthly Videos
- 24 Monthly Graphics
- Monthly Photo Shoot
- Full Suite of Social Media Posts
- Advanced Social Media Strategy
- Premium CRM
- Reputation Optimization

## Other Package Types
- **Ads Only** — Digital advertising management only (pricing varies)
- **Creative Only** — Content production without ads or CRM (pricing varies)
- **A La Carte** — Project-based work for events, conferences, one-off needs
- **PILOT PROGRAM** — Trial engagement for prospective clients

- Podcast Session — $500 (2hr studio, raw clips + edited final, 4hrs total)

- House Tour / Walk Through — $500 (up to 5min + 30sec reel, 6hrs total)

### Photography

- Team Headshots (4 people) — $700 (group + individuals, 6hrs total)
- Product Photography — $500 (up to 30 images, 6hrs total)
- Event Photography — $500 (4hr coverage, 8hrs total)
- Corporate Photo Shoot — $750 (up to 20 images, 8hrs total)
- Luxury Branding Photoshoot — $1,500 (half-day, 3 locations, 30 images, 12hrs total)
- Real Estate Listing Photos — $250 (up to 35 photos + drone, 7hrs total)

### Graphic Design (billed at $50/hr)
- Logo Design — $300 (transparent, vector, printable, 4 revisions)
- Brand Kit — $150 (logo, fonts, colors, social kit, 4 post layouts, 4hrs)
- Social Media Posts Package (30 posts) — $500 (16hrs)
- Flyer — $30 (2hrs)

### Studio Rental
- Podcast Room (2hr) — $300
- Studio Session (2hr) — $150

### CRM (GoHighLevel) Monthly Plans
- Standard — $10/mo (2-way text/email, GMB, web chat, reputation mgmt, missed call text back)
- Professional — $25/mo (Standard + calendar, CRM, opportunities, email marketing)
- Premium — $50/mo (Professional + funnels, memberships, websites, workflows, forms, surveys, campaigns, social planner, invoices, blogs)

### CRM Custom Work
- Custom Landing Page (2-step funnel) — $500 (6hrs)
- Custom Automation Package — $1,500 (hot lead follow-up, booking calendar, power dialer, pipeline, 16hrs)
