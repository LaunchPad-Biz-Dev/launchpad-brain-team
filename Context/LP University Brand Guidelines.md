# LP University — Creative Direction & Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
LaunchPad University exists to create community, learn, and connect by teaching business owners, entrepreneurs, marketers, and creators how to plan marketing correctly, execute content consistently, turn attention into leads, and manage it through systems.

## Core Framework
Content → Leads → Systems
- LP University teaches the framework
- LaunchPad BizDev executes the framework
- LaunchPad Systems operationalizes the framework

## Brand Archetype
Creator + Sage = Artist + Engineer
- **Sage**: Teaches truth. No fluff. No fads. Structured and grounded
- **Creator**: Visionary, visual, connective, architectural

## Target Audience
1. **Primary**: Business owners (5+ employees, $10K/mo marketing budget capacity, existing sales infrastructure)
2. **Secondary**: Entrepreneurs and small business owners (time/resource poor, need fundamentals)
3. **Third Lane**: Creators and marketers (saturated by gurus, need truth and infrastructure)

## Positioning
"LaunchPad University is a free marketing and systems university built for business owners who want real growth, not random posting."

## Offer Structure
- Newsletter → Free Course (Digital Marketing 101) → 30-day free CRM → 90-day pilot (not public) → Full service
- 90-day pilot is a sales lever, NOT publicly promoted

## Messaging Pillars
1. Structure beats motivation
2. Alignment beats volume
3. Hybrid beats polarized thinking
4. Execution proves truth
5. Creative is not separate from structure

## Tone & Voice
Educational + edgy + creative. Professional but friendly. Approachable, relatable. Straight to the point, no fluff. Trustworthy, valuable, authentic.

### Voice References
- **StoryBrand**: Clarity, customer journey, guide positioning
- **Alex Hormozi**: Directness, constraints, measurable outcomes
- **Casey Neistat**: Honest educational style, raw, off the cuff
- **Peter McKinnon**: Craft, cinematic credibility, creator meets discipline

## Color Palette
- Burnt Orange: #CF5C3C
- Deep Orange Brown: #9C3A00
- Dark Grey: #606060
- Dark Charcoal: #232629
- Light Orange: #E7E2DA
- Bright LP Red: #E63024 (use sparingly)

## Typography
- **Owslad Bold**: Subheadings and bold statements
- **Poppins**: Paragraph clarity
- **Biro Script**: Handwritten accents only
- **Anca Coder**: Stylistic tech accent, never as body copy

## Imagery
Hard flash, editorial, vogue, creative portraits, behind the scenes. Favor real environments, real work, real humans. Avoid generic stock business photos. Show build mode, not polished corporate mode.

## Background & Composition
Gradients, grain, floating dust, film, paper, lined, grid, crumble, scrapbook, vintage, retro, doodles, threshold. Scrapbook, edgy, modern poster energy with enlarged items and realistic icons.

## Content System
3 posts/week: 1 carousel (problem/solution), 1 still (punch statement), 1 video (short direct lesson)

### Content Buckets
- **Content**: Creative execution, hooks, messaging, creative discipline
- **Leads**: Lead gen logic, offer clarity, proof stories, conversion
- **Systems**: CRM, follow-up, booking flow, retention, pipeline
- **Hybrid**: How the three connect (this is where the brand lives most)

## Governance
Only Abril (CD) and Anthony (CEO) can change positioning, tone, tagline, palette, typography, core messaging pillars, offer ladder rules, or 90-day pilot visibility rules.

## What This Brand Must Never Become
- A generic marketing tips page
- A guru vibe program
- A meme page
- A trend chasing clip farm
- A corporate university aesthetic
