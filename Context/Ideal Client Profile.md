## Disqualifier List (We Do NOT Work With)

- Startups pre-revenue or pre-product-market fit
- Businesses with <$2,500/mo marketing budget (below Essential Growth floor)
- National enterprise accounts outside current delivery capacity
- MLM / adult / crypto / cannabis (compliance risk)
- Businesses running negative PR / active litigation we'd inherit
- Owners not the decision-maker + no direct access to the signer
- Timeline >90 days to start (not serious)

## Trigger Events (From Antonia v1 — Kept)

Companies in buying mode typically show ≥1 of:
- **Expansion phase** — new locations, hiring sprees
- **Revenue stagnation** — plateau despite increased effort
- **Increased competition** — new entrants eating their share
- **Leadership change** — new CMO, GM, or managing partner

## Decision-Making Structure (From Antonia v1 — Kept)

**Primary Buyer** — Owner / Founder / Managing Partner → Revenue, growth, ROI framing
**Operational Influencer** — Marketing Manager / Operations Lead → Execution, systems, efficiency
**Strategic Buyer (larger accounts)** — CMO / VP Marketing → Scalability, consistency, performance

Messaging adapts by buyer type. Default = Primary Buyer framing.

## Core Business Problems (From Antonia v1 — Kept)

These are NOT marketing problems — they are revenue problems:

1. **Revenue Inefficiency** — Leads generated but not converted / no follow-up system
2. **Lack of Ownership** — Dependent on third-party platforms / no internal marketing infrastructure
3. **Brand Underperformance** — Brand doesn't reflect service quality / low authority
4. **Inconsistent Growth** — Seasonal or unpredictable revenue / plateau despite effort

## Decision Drivers (From Antonia v1 — Kept)

Prospects say yes based on:
1. Proof of results (case studies)
2. Industry-specific success (Quintet references by vertical)
3. Trust and clarity (straight pricing, no BS)
