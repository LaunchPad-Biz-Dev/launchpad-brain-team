# Launchpad Business Development — Company Overview

## Founded
2022 by Anthony Mendoza. In 2023, Nick Maldonado and Antonia Andrade joined as partners.

## The 3 Entities
- **Launchpad Business Development** — umbrella company, full-service marketing agency
- **Launchpad Productions** — photography and videography
- **BrandWithUs (BWU)** — physical print goods and print shop

## Headquarters
5,000 sq ft warehouse, Las Vegas, Nevada
- 20x20 cyc wall
- 2 podcast studios
- Print shop
- Office area (front of space)

## Website
https://launchpadbizdev.com

## Philosophy
Grounded in Ikigai — what we excel at, what we love, what the world needs, and what sustains us financially. Adaptability, resilience, excellence, and transparency guide everything we do.

## Mission
Lead businesses into the digital future, revealing new opportunities and crafting meaningful connections.

## Notable Success Stories
- Toro Taxes — expanded franchise footprint significantly
- Personal Injury Attorney — built brand to become one of the largest in the Latino market
- Car Dealership — cut marketing costs while increasing sales, built lasting systems
- Franchisees — helped add 69 new units to a national footprint through digital marketing
