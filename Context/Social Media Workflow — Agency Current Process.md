# Social Media Workflow — Agency Current Process

Documented during a 2026-04-13 workflow mapping session between Anthony Mendoza and Abril Rodriguez. Purpose: map how content moves from ideation to posting across all clients so the Apollo AI system could be built around it.

---

## Current Workflow (Pre-Apollo)

### 1. Ideation & Brainstorming
- Abril and Kyle collaborate using the Content Brainstorm Management board in Notion
- Research inputs: quarterly plans, seasonal relevance, national awareness days, client-specific case/trend research
- **Client-specific nuance:**
  - Legal clients (MNL, MNB): seasonal case spikes (motorcycle summer, DUI holidays)
  - Healthcare (Upbeat): health topics + audience needs
  - MNL tone: more friendly; MNB: different approach
- Ideas documented with Instagram, Pinterest, and past successful content references
- **Meme content:** Manual adjustment required — ChatGPT doesn't capture current trends

### 2. Brief Creation
- Abril creates graphic design briefs in the Graphic Design Notion board
- Uses ChatGPT for headline and subtext options per client tone/brand voice
- Platform segmentation for Upbeat: Instagram (humorous/meme) vs Facebook (informational)
- Briefs include: visual references, layout suggestions, design direction

### 3. Design & Review
- Graphic designers (Arija + team) work from briefs
- Google Drive structure: organized by month → content type (Carousels, Stills, Scheduled)
- Abril review criteria: grammar, aesthetics, design principles (alignment, hierarchy, image appropriateness)
- Stock photography often requires personal revision from Abril
- Revision feedback via Google Drive comments
- Reference files maintained in Canva and shared with designers
- Toro and Durango require fewer revisions due to consistent established patterns

### 4. Caption Creation
- Captions created just before posting (NOT immediately after briefs)
- Uses ChatGPT with client-specific instructions: length, hashtag count, emoji, tone
- **Upbeat:** all punctuation required per client request
- Captions saved in the graphic design brief under "Social Media Caption" section

### 5. GoHighLevel Posting Flow
- Upload to Google Drive first → then to GHL media storage
- Platform selection: Feed, Reel, Story
- Posts scheduled one by one; times intentionally varied
- Method: **"Send Post for Approval"** (not direct scheduling)
- Approval notification chain: Email (Abril) → Text (Emmy) → Phone call (April/Abril)
- Most clients approve within 1–2 revision rounds

### 6. Client-Specific Notes
- CCS: no "paycheck" references
- Some clients: capitalize all letters in specific contexts
- **Gap identified:** Do's and Don'ts documentation needs dedicated maintenance in client dashboards — repetitive revision requests could be avoided

---

## Apollo Automation Layer (2026-04-13 Decision)

**Decision:** Build Apollo AI automation system based on this documented workflow.
**Roll-out strategy:** Test with one client first → progressively add more over following month.
**Assigned:** Anthony to build; Abril to identify test client.

### Automation Targets (High-Value Touchpoints for Apollo)
- Caption generation (highest-volume, currently manual with ChatGPT)
- Brief creation (currently manual with ChatGPT + Abril judgment)
- Content ideation support (trend research, seasonal calendars)
- GHL upload formatting + scheduling (CSV bulk upload via Social Media Poster agent)
- Client-specific rule application (Do's/Don'ts enforcement per client)
