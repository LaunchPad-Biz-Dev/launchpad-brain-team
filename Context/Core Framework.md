# Core Framework: Content → Leads → Systems

This is the foundational philosophy of Launchpad Business Development and what we promote to every client.

## The 3 Pillars

### 1. Content
Create content to get attention.
- Professionally produced media (photo, video, graphics)
- Social media presence across all platforms
- Brand storytelling and identity

### 2. Leads
Use that attention to generate leads.
- Media buying across Meta, TikTok, Google, LinkedIn
- CRM integration and lead capture
- Targeted campaigns to the right audience

### 3. Systems
Build systems to follow up with leads and get business.
- GoHighLevel CRM (internal + white-labeled for clients)
- Automated follow-up sequences
- Pipeline management and lead nurture

## Why This Framework Matters
Most clients come to Launchpad with one or two of these pieces missing. The framework gives us a clear diagnostic — we identify where their funnel is broken and build the missing pieces.
