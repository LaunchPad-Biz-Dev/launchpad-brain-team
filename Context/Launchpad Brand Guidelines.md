## ⚡ CURRENT BRAND (as of late April 2026 — synced 2026-05-04)

This is the active, in-use brand spec. Use this for all new work.

### Brand Identity
- **Positioning:** Confident but not corporate. Creative but clean. Professional, strategic, and visually engaging.
- **Tone:** Modern, knowledgeable, approachable — a marketing company that practices what it preaches.
- **Visual feel:** Bold, minimal, high-contrast. Disciplined palette. Modern geometric display + editorial serif for narrative weight.

### Color System (4 colors — disciplined)

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use Case |
|------|-----|------|----------|
| **Crimson Flame** | `#CB0003` | 203, 0, 3 | Deep brand red — primary brand color, hero blocks, large color fields |
| **System Red** | `#DC1716` | 220, 23, 22 | Brighter accent red — CTAs, emphasis, callouts, highlight elements |
| **Black** | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | Body text, dark backgrounds, primary contrast |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Light backgrounds, reverse text, breathing room |

**Color Rules:** No grays in primary palette. Black, white, and the two reds are the entire system. Use red intentionally — for hero moments and CTAs, not as decoration.

### Typography (Locked Type System)

| Font | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Syne Extrabold** | Display headlines, slide titles, hero typography |
| **Syne Bold** | Subheads, section markers |
| **Baskervville Regular** *(note: two v's — official spelling)* | Editorial body copy, narrative paragraphs, long-form |
| **Baskervville Italic** | Emphasis within body, pull quotes |
| **Satoshi** | Modern sans utility — labels, captions, UI elements, small text |

**Typography Rules:** Bold, confident hierarchy. Generous white space. Mix Syne (display) with Baskervville (narrative) for editorial feel. Use Satoshi sparingly for utility text.

## Content Pillars
1. **Authority Building** — case studies, testimonials, performance results
2. **Educational Content** — marketing strategy, trends, practical tools
3. **Behind-the-Scenes** — people, processes, shoots, meetings, team energy
4. **Engagement-Driven** — response videos, challenges, hot takes, Q&A
5. **Lifestyle & Brand Personality** — company culture, who we are, how we work

## Content Buckets
1. **AI in Marketing** — sleek, tech-forward, gradients, circuit textures
2. **Branding** — bold, design-conscious, typography play, editorial layouts
3. **Marketing Insights** — informative, clean, graphs, stats, white space
4. **Gen Z** — energetic, culture-aware, emojis, stickers, pop graphics

## Photography Direction
- Clean, natural, and intentional
- Studio lighting, high-resolution stills, candid BTS moments
- Capture work environment, client interactions, internal culture

## Iconography
- Simple, flat or line-based
- Legible at small sizes
- Color aligned with overall palette
